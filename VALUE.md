# VALUE.md — Value Lane Boot Pointer
### The second of two lane pointers at the vault root. It routes; it does not rule.
### `NORTH_STAR.md` owns destination. `CURRENT_STRATEGY.md` owns the vehicle. `value_production_goal.md` owns the proof ladder. The Watchtower owns signal promotion. CASTLE owns gates and sequencing. This file adds none of that and may not restate it.

## Why this file exists

Value-lane research has been running in chat and landing nowhere. The cause is
structural, not motivational: `WATCHTOWER.md` correctly refuses raw research
("do not pad the radar") and hands it to the owning wiki — and the owning wikis
went cold when the semester started. `REVENUE_LAB` 2026-08-21, `BUSINESS`
2026-08-16, `TECHNOLOGY` 2026-08-18, `AI_AUTOMATION_SYSTEMS` 2026-08-22.
Research now falls between the rungs and dies there.

This pointer aims the lane at the owning wikis and requires every session to
write into one. It creates no new evidence home.

## Preload boundary

Start with the value branch above. Load `TUTOR.md`, school schedules, learner
paths, or CASTLE weekly plans only when Chris's request actually crosses into
them. A lane narrows default context; it never blocks a direct request.

School capacity is governed by `capability_development_goal.md` § Fall 2026
selection overlay, not by this lane. This lane runs inside the protected
technology/business floor (`AGENT.md` priority 2) and never above a fixed
school deadline.

## Opening test

Use Chris's five questions before research, building, buying, or committing:

1. What outcome is required?
2. What constraint or failure matters most?
3. What evidence already exists?
4. What is the smallest test that reduces uncertainty?
5. What result would change the next move?

For company and income work, add: **which reachable person has this problem, and
what economic outcome would make solving it valuable?**

Access first, capability second. Both are required; neither substitutes.

This is not a new question. It is `NORTH_STAR.md` §1 — *enter an unfamiliar
problem, find what actually costs money or time, fix it, proven first on real
use* — reduced to the one input currently missing.

## Resolve live state; do not cache it here

Read `capability_development_goal.md`, `value_production_goal.md`, and CASTLE's
current position for the live gate. Use `CURRENT_STRATEGY.md` for vehicle choices
and `radar.md` only for a qualifying external signal. Access to a real workflow
and observed performance outrank another round of speculative research.

## Where output lands

`00-BRAIN\WHERE_IT_GOES.md` owns routing. Use one existing owner: REVENUE_LAB for
demand or revenue evidence; BUSINESS for offers and market decisions; SYSTEMS for
workflow diagnosis; TECHNOLOGY for adoption and capability research;
AI_AUTOMATION_SYSTEMS for AI/agent work; `05-BUSINESS` for approved sanitized
reusable assets. Promote a Watchtower signal only after its threshold is met.

A finding that names no buyer and no outcome is a note. Write it to the owning
wiki as a note. Do not promote it, and do not carry it forward as progress.

## Boundaries — from `value_production_goal.md` and `NORTH_STAR.md` §3

- No outreach, offer, price, publication, or client commitment without Chris's
  explicit approval. Consequential actions stop and ask.
- Active client-specific work stays outside `.ROOT`; only approved sanitized
  evidence and reusable assets return.
- Course submissions and restricted course material never become business assets.
- A bounded test states expected evidence, capacity cost, owner, stop rule, and
  review date before it begins.
- AI may propose a change to `NORTH_STAR.md`, `CURRENT_STRATEGY.md`, or the goal
  files; it may not write to them without explicit approval.

## Session success bar

A value session succeeded if it produced **one** of:

- movement of one rung on the `value_production_goal.md` proof ladder
  (capability → use → problem → outcome → demand → revenue → repeatability), or
- evidence written into an owning wiki that could move a rung.

Anything else logs as no output. A run of no-output sessions means the lane is
browsing rather than hunting, and should be surfaced to Chris as such.
