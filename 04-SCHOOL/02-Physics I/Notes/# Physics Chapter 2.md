# Physics Chapter 2

## Lesson 1
1. **Postion**- where something is.
2. **Displacement**- how much position changes.
3. **Average Velocity**- how fast position changes over time.
- Chapter 2 is about explaining motion in a strait line, like a truck driving down one road or a cart rolling across a jobsite.
- Describing where something is, where it went, and how fast that change occurred.

### Position
- **Position** means the location of an object compared to an origin.
- The **origin** is the zero point
