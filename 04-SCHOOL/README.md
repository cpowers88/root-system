# 04-SCHOOL — Course-file and tutoring-source home

This folder holds material Chris may bring into an on-demand tutoring session.
**Chris owns due dates and submission status in live D2L.** `.ROOT` does not
promise a complete assignment calendar. Local maps, plans, and the workbook are
reference views whose dated claims require Chris's live confirmation.

## Two responsibilities, one clean boundary

1. **Execution:** Chris checks D2L, chooses what must be submitted, and tells the
   agent the subject and task. `.ROOT` does not infer completeness from silence.
2. **Learning:** `TUTOR.md` loads the subject hat, owner, learner position, source,
   and miss log. The wiki path supplies sequence and durable practice, not today's
   assignment list.

## On-demand school workflow

1. Chris names the course, desired outcome, and available time.
2. If graded work is involved, confirm the course's AI boundary first.
3. Load the owning wiki and the exact source needed for that task.
4. Teach or test; do not re-plan the semester.
5. Record only real learner evidence: a cold pass, a cold miss, or a returned score.
