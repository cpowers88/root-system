## What the spine makes visible

**1. This course is one long build, not a series of assignments.** Everything from Oct 20 onward
is the Technical Report. Diamond gates it in four review checkpoints — Introduction (Oct 27),
Project Description (Nov 3), Recommendation/Conclusion/Exec Summary/Transmittal (Nov 10), Slides
& Bibliography (Nov 17) — then Progress Report, rough draft, peer review, presentation, and the
Reflective Memo. **Miss a checkpoint and the next one compounds.** The report is effectively due
in pieces starting seven weeks before Dec 3.

**2. Four presentation-heavy weeks, Sep 29 – Oct 15.** Fairy Tale group presentation, Elevator
Speech, then three consecutive Individual Project Proposal presentation days. Chris does not
control which of Oct 8 / 13 / 15 he presents on, so he has to be ready for the earliest.

**3. The graded mechanics are unusually strict.** File naming is quizzed and enforced; the section
number is **04**; several items are MS Word only; the Sep 10 lab day requires physically bringing
equipment. These are free points that are lost by inattention, not by ability.

**4. Two items already carry drafts in the inbox.** `Powers_04_Business Email Draft.docx` and
`Powers_04_Ethics Analysis.docx` are sitting in `77-INBOX`. Both belong in
`04-SCHOOL\03-TCOM\work\`, not the inbox — the inbox is intake, never storage.
