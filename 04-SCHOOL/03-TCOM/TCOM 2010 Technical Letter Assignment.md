## TCOM 2010 Business Email Unit

**Purpose:** The purpose of this assignment is to give you practice with formal communication in a technically-oriented workplace. Keep in mind your **audience** and **purpose** when you establish the **tone** of your responses.

- ***Directive***: You are a manager at a company related to your major. You have had to change or add a policy or procedure due to some problem at work. An incident (accident, injury, mistake, breach, etc.) occurred at work and you need to write an incident report addressed to your supervisor to inform him or her of the occurrence. ## RUBRICS:

**Business Email Draft (10 points)**

- (Clarity) Email is straightforward, concise, descriptive, and polite (2 points)
- (Content) - 3 points
	- **Directives** begin with an explanation of the policy being implemented and end with a statement of the policy.
		- **Incident reports** relate the sequence of events in chronological order.
- (Formatting) Subject line, Opening Greeting, Closing Greeting, Signature Block (1 point)
- (Grammar) Grammar, phrasing, punctuation, formatting (2 points)
- (Tone) Polite, frames communication in regard to what the reader wants or needs to know (1 point)
- **AI Usage Statement:** (1 point)
	- **Did you use AI?** Be honest. Lying invalidates your assignment.
		- Non-AI users will need to reflect on your writing process and how any non-AI tools aided you.
				- If you used AI, you will need to answer the AI Use Statement for the assignment.
			- What AI you used?
						- What was your prompt?
						- What did you adapt from the output?
						- Name 2 reliable sources that you used to verify your output. (Hint: Reddit is not a viable source.)

**Business Email Final (100 points)**

***\* You must correct your final using the feedback from your draft. If not, then you will be marked down significantly more.***

- (Clarity) Email is straightforward, concise, descriptive, and polite (20 points)
- (Content) - 40 points
	- **Directives** begin with an explanation of the policy being implemented and end with a statement of the policy.
		- **Incident reports** relate the sequence of events in chronological order.
- (Formatting) Subject line, Opening Greeting, Closing Greeting, Signature Block (10 points)
- (Grammar) Grammar, phrasing, punctuation, formatting (20 points)
- (Tone) Polite, frames communication in regard to what the reader wants or needs to know (10 points)

| **Category** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **Subject line** | **Summarize topic of email** |
| **Opening Greeting** | **Dear Title of Person**  **for example: Dear Ms. Hampton, OR Dear Mr. Blankensop,** |
| **Introductory Paragraph** | **State the purpose of the email.** |
| **Main Body Paragraphs** | **Provide the information in 1-3 sentence paragraphs.**  **Each sentence should be descriptive and concise.** |
| **Closing Paragraphs** | **Conclude the actions you are taking next, or what you expect someone else to do for you.** |
| **Closing Greeting** | **Sincerely,** |
| **Signature Block** | **Name   Email address** |
