## What this is

Purdue OWL's Professional, Technical Writing section: "research and compose
documents for the workplace, such as memoranda and business letters,"
including report and scientific-abstract guidance. Free, no login, widely
cited academic writing-center reference.

## Why this is here

Direct topic overlap with TCOM 2010's own unit structure: memos (Week 1),
audience analysis (Week 2), proposals (Week 4-5), technical reports (Weeks
6-12), instructions (Weeks 13-14). Items 8, 12, 15, and 17 above map most
directly onto graded units in the actual syllabus. TCOM's AI policy (editing/
proofreading only, cited) means this is reading material to study from, not
a source to paraphrase into submitted drafts.
