Attendance — the most expensive rule in the course

  • First 3 unexcused absences are free.
  • From the 4th: roughly −3.5 points off your final grade, each.
  • 6 unexcused = minus one letter grade. 9 = minus two letter grades.

  • Excused absence or lateness must be arranged ahead of time.
