The one scheduling change worth making this week

Three PHYS blocks — Mon 2.25 h, Wed 2.0 h, Fri 2.0 h — exist to service weekly WebAssign. In week 1 there is no WebAssign due. The live gradebook shows the first homework as HW1 (Due Aug 31st), which lands on the Monday of week 2. That frees roughly four hours inside blocks that are already on the calendar.

Spend it on Chapter 5, not on more Chapter 1–4. Stages 1–3 are closed and Ch 4 is open only at circular motion, so this week's reading-ahead is largely review for you — cheap. Ch 5 and §§6.1–6.2 are untouched, and both are on Unit Exam 1. The Exam 1 gate is full scope cold by Sun 13 Sep, and weeks 1–4 are the entire window to build that lead. From week 5 there is no surplus at all.

Where it goes concretely: Monday's 2.25 h → the D2L day-one tab, which must happen and is the highest-value block in the week. Wednesday's 2.0 h → start HW1 five days early. Friday's 2.0 h → finish HW1, then open Ch 5. That converts a deadline block into a lead block without touching family time.

PHYS is deliberately at the top of its band: it is 37% of the outside-class load, and exams plus the final are 75% of that grade. ENGR stays at two ten-minute D2L checks until its exact section syllabus arrives. The surge pool is Saturday and Sunday afternoon — pre-committed to whatever the week is actually failing at, not spare time.

The four rules this runs on

01

Read before the lecture, never after

PHYS pre-class reading is graded. Monday evening reads Wednesday; Wednesday evening reads Friday.

02

Work a course next to its class

Every block touches its course's lecture the same day. That is why Tuesday 11:00 is TCOM and not PHYS.

03

The surge pool is not free time

Saturday and Sunday afternoon belong to whatever is failing. In an exam-sweep week, they are the sweep.

04

Protect the deadline before the plan

The only irreversible loss in the week is WebAssign — late is a flat zero. File the extension first, recover the day second.
