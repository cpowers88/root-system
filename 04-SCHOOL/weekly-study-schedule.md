and there are seven collision weeks (5, 6, 8, 11, 12, 13, 14).

**The fix is not more hours in the evening.** It is three unclaimed on-campus blocks that
are already sitting between existing commitments.

## The four rules this schedule runs on

1. **Read before the lecture, never after.** PHYS pre-class reading is graded. Monday and
   Wednesday evenings exist for exactly this — Monday reads Wednesday, Wednesday reads Friday.
2. **Work a course adjacent to its class.** Every block above touches its course's lecture
   on the same day. That is the whole design; it is why Tuesday 11:00 is TCOM and not PHYS.
3. **The surge pool is not free time.** Saturday and Sunday afternoon are pre-committed to
   whatever the week is actually failing at. In an exam-sweep week they are the sweep.
4. **When a day collapses, protect the deadline before you protect the plan.** The only
   irreversible loss in the week is WebAssign — late is a flat zero. File the extension
   request first, recover the day second.
