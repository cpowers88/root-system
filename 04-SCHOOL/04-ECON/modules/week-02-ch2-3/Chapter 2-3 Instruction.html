Week 2 - Chapter 2 and Chapter 3

Chapter 2 - Fund. Econ. Questions & Gains from Specialization Chapter 3 - Economic Systems: Capitalism versus Socialism

Learning Outcomes:

  • Identify the three fundamental questions of what to produce; how to produce it; for whom to produce it

  • Demonstrate knowledge of the sources of the gains from trade and identify the benefits of an open trading system and apply fundamental economic question

  • Describe the roles played by households and consumption and production of goods/services

  • Illustrate a  simple model  (of a two-person,   two commodity economy)  is established and analyzed,  in order to illustrate precisely how specialization in production allows a society to maximize the amount of output that it produces

  • Differentiate the similarities and differences between Capitalism and Socialism as organizing systems of an economy. Explain the underlying moral principles of both Socialism and Capitalism
