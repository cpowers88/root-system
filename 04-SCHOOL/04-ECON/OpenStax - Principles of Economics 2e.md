## What this is

OpenStax's *Principles of Economics 2e*, publisher Houston, Texas, published
2017-10-11. Free to read online at the source URL above; free PDF and print
options are offered from the same book landing page
(`https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-economics-2e`, a
JavaScript-rendered page that did not return static text to this capture —
open it directly in a browser for the download/print links).

## Coverage captured this session

Only Chapter 1 ("Welcome to Economics!") was fetched and confirmed live:

- What Is Economics, and Why Is It Important?
- Microeconomics and Macroeconomics
- How Economists Use Theories and Models to Understand Economic Issues
- How Economies Can Be Organized: An Overview of Economic Systems

The full book runs to roughly 20 chapters covering demand/supply, elasticity,
competition/monopoly, market failure and externalities, poverty and
inequality, GDP and growth, unemployment, money and the monetary system,
inflation, and international trade — this was not independently re-confirmed
chapter-by-chapter in this pass (the SPA-rendered table-of-contents page
would not return text to automated fetch). Each chapter page follows the same
URL pattern as the one captured (`.../pages/{number}-{slug}`) and rendered
cleanly, so pulling additional chapters as ECON 1000 reaches each topic is a
low-effort follow-up, not a new source hunt.

## Why this is here

Recommended as the open-textbook backbone for building a structured ECON 1000
pathway (see `03-WIKIS\EDUCATION\wiki\fall-2026-course-briefs.md`) — its
