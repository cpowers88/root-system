## How to play this course

**Only two quiz scores count, and they are the two highest.** There are four graded quizzes plus
one extra-credit quiz. That is 50% of the grade decided by the best two of four. It changes the
strategy: **the first two quizzes are not low-stakes warm-ups — they are auditions.** A strong
Ch 1-2-3 and Ch 4-5 result effectively locks half the grade before October, and the October and
November quizzes become insurance.

**Both exams are single-day, ~15-hour windows, with no make-up under any circumstance.**
Sep 29 and Dec 3. Those two dates carry 50% of the grade and are the least forgiving items in the
entire five-course semester. They belong on a calendar with an alarm, not in a plan file.

**Every quiz has a practice quiz in the same window, ungraded.** Take the practice first, every
time. It is a free read on the graded item and the syllabus states practice scores do not count.
