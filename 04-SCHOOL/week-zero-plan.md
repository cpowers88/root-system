## The one thing to understand before the list

**Week zero is not a reading week. It is a rules week.**

Five courses print five different late policies, five naming conventions and five AI
policies, and **week 1 grades three of them**. Meanwhile there is no new chapter you
urgently need, because you are ahead in the two courses that would otherwise hurt.

So the ordering below is not by subject. It is by **what gets graded first**, and that
turns out to be almost entirely TCOM.

   No re-writes are permitted for plagiarized work under any circumstances.

   of classes. An empty D2L is normal and is not an account problem.
