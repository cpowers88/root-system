
  # CASTLE Elevation and .ROOT Folder Architecture

  ## Summary

  Elevate CASTLE from 00-BRAIN\CASTLE to top-level 00-CASTLE without renumbering or moving the mature BRAIN, NORTH
  STAR, LIBRARY, WIKIS, or BUSINESS realms.

  CASTLE becomes the operational cockpit—not the constitutional authority:

  Chris decides
      ↓
  NORTH STAR defines direction
      ↓
  BRAIN defines governance and AI behavior
      ↓
  CASTLE selects sequence, owner, next action, and proof
      ↓
  Owning realm performs the work
      ↓
  Verified results return to CASTLE

  No files are to be moved until Chris separately approves execution.

  ### 00-CASTLE — Operational Command Center

  Purpose: convert North Star direction, live owner truth, commitments, capacity, and evidence into the highest-value
  next action.

  CASTLE owns:

  - Current position, roadmap, phase map, skill map, opportunity queue, and proof status.
  - Decisions about sequence, ownership, next action, completion evidence, and return destination.
  - Root NOW.md as the detailed current-action interface.
  - Gates that prevent ideas, tools, or opportunities from becoming work without evidence.
  - Templates used specifically for CASTLE decisions and proof tracking.

  CASTLE does not own:

  - North Star direction.
  - AI governance or safety rules.
  - Domain research or learner truth.
  - Source evidence.
  - Project execution files.
  - Active client work.
  - External trend sensing.
  - General intake.

  Internal synopsis:

  - OPERATIONS.md — authoritative CASTLE operating contract.
  - HOW_TO_USE.md — short human navigation and startup guide.
  - CLAUDE.md and CODEX.md — local pointers into BRAIN governance and CASTLE operations; no duplicated rules.
  - wiki/ — steering memory: sequence, capability maps, phases, proof projects, decision rules, index, and log.
  - templates/ — approved CASTLE record templates, not a general system-template laboratory.
  - No active raw/ folder.

  ### 00-BRAIN — Governance and AI Runtime

  Purpose: define how AI works safely and consistently throughout .ROOT.

  Owns:

  - Universal AI behavior and boot chain.
  - Chris’s runtime profile.
  - Model capability profiles.
  - Hats and operating modes.
  - Placement and metadata authority.
  - System flags and validated system learnings.
  - Shared skills, scripts, session logs, maps, graph configuration, and machine instructions.

  Does not own daily priorities, domain knowledge, strategy, project work, or CASTLE sequencing.

  00-BRAIN remains unchanged in name and position because it is the stable governance and boot layer.

  ### 01-NORTH_STAR — Direction and Strategic Truth

  Purpose: define why .ROOT exists, what Chris is becoming, the fixed destination, current strategic vehicle, durable
  commitments, and system contracts.

  Owns:

  - NORTH_STAR.md.
  - Goals, milestones, current strategy, school plans, and weekly plans.
  - Capability and information-flow contracts.
  - Ratchet review rules and strategic assumptions.

  CASTLE reads and serves the North Star. CASTLE may challenge or surface evidence but cannot silently rewrite
  direction.

  ### 02-LIBRARY — Sources, School Files, and Projects

  Purpose: hold material used to learn, build, or verify without pretending that stored material is refined knowledge.

  Owns:

  - Official school and course files.
  - Active bounded project workspaces.
  - Read-mostly reference collections.
  - Books, PDFs, datasets, cheat sheets, and provenance material.

  Refined knowledge goes to 03-WIKIS; reusable business assets go to 05-BUSINESS.

  ### 03-WIKIS — Domain Knowledge and Capability Engines

  Purpose: transform evidence and learning into durable, retrievable, applied knowledge.

  Each bounded wiki owns:

  - Its immutable raw/ evidence.
  - Its refined wiki/ pages.
  - Current learner or domain truth.
  - Learning paths, source maps, indexes, logs, gaps, and next domain actions.
  - Local operating instructions.

  Existing domains remain:

  - AI_AUTOMATION_SYSTEMS
  - BUSINESS
  - EDUCATION
  - PHYSICS
  - PYTHON
  - REVENUE_LAB
  - SYSTEMS
  - TECHNOLOGY

  Wikis research, teach, and maintain domain truth. They do not decide global priority; qualifying results return to
  CASTLE.

  ### ...projectSuccess — External Watchtower

  Purpose: sense material external changes without allowing trends to steer the system directly.

  Owns exactly:

  - WATCHTOWER.md — promotion threshold and external-signal operating contract.
  - radar.md — evidence-backed external signals awaiting review, gating, testing, or outcome recording.

  Flow:

  Owning wiki evidence
      → Watchtower qualification
      → radar row
      → CASTLE gate
      → bounded test in owning realm
      → measured result
      → strategy consideration

  The Watchtower has eyes, not hands. It does not implement improvements, rank general internal recommendations, or
  replace CASTLE.

  ### 05-BUSINESS — Reusable Value and Revenue Assets

  Purpose: preserve reusable, sanitized assets that help diagnose, deliver, demonstrate, or sell valuable work.

  Owns:

  - Audit templates and methods.
  - Sanitized field notes.
  - Approved case studies.
  - Pricing models.
  - Proposal and SOW patterns.
  - Capability-library assets.

  Active private client work remains outside .ROOT. BUSINESS wiki research remains in 03-WIKIS\BUSINESS.

  ### 77-INBOX — Manual Capture and Routing Queue

  Purpose: provide one low-friction entry point for unsorted files, AI recommendations, ideas, and material Chris
  manually introduces.

  Rules:

  - Temporary, not permanent storage.
  - Cleared through the existing placement authority.
  - Recommendations route to the appropriate wiki, CASTLE queue, system flag, project, or archive.
  - Source material only enters a wiki’s raw/ after its correct evidence owner is identified.

  This replaces the proposed CASTLE raw-recommendation intake.

  ### 88-JOURNAL — Private Human Realm

  Purpose: Chris’s private reflection and personal records.

  AI never reads or writes this folder. The restructure does not inspect, move, rename, index, or otherwise touch it.

  ### 99-ARCHIVE — Preserved System History

  Purpose: retain obsolete, replaced, or inactive material for provenance and reversal.

  Nothing is deleted. Archived replacements receive dated names. Historical records are not rewritten to pretend old
  paths never existed.

  ### Clippings — Automatic Web Capture

  Purpose: receive material created by the Obsidian web clipper.

  Clippings is temporary intake, reviewed alongside 77-INBOX, and routed to the appropriate evidence, reference, or
  archive home.

  ### outputs and tmp — Noncanonical Working Material

  Purpose: hold generated exports, temporary renderings, test artifacts, and other noncanonical working files.

  Neither folder may become a source of truth. Durable results must return to their owning realm.

  ### Root Files — Human and AI Interfaces

  - AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, CODEX.md — entry pointers into the BRAIN boot chain.
  - START_HERE.md — stable human map and routing interface.
  - NOW.md — CASTLE-owned detailed current-action dashboard.
  - MORNING_BRIEF.md — generated start-of-day interface.
  - EVENING_READING.md — generated prerequisite-reading interface.
  - ROOT_OPERATING_MANUAL.md — full human operating reference.

  These remain at the root because they are entry surfaces, not content realms.

  ## CASTLE Operating Contract

  Every material CASTLE decision must identify:

  1. Why the action matters now.
  2. Which realm owns the work.
  3. What Chris approval boundary applies.
  4. The one next action sized to real capacity.
  5. Observable proof that closes the action.
  6. The authoritative file that receives the result.

  CASTLE points to owner truth rather than copying it. Work happens in the owning realm; only sequencing and proof-
  state changes return to CASTLE.
