# Audit Interview Guide

For Days 2–4 of the [[smb-ai-audit-method|audit]]. Two versions below: owner and frontline staff. Record (with permission) or take timestamped notes. The golden rule: **what people say they do and what they do always differ** — so every claimed process gets one "show me" request.

Evidence discipline: use [[customer-discovery-and-evidence|Customer Discovery
and Evidence]]. Ask about specific past events, current workarounds, frequency,
cost, prior spend, and who owns the next decision. Compliments, hypotheticals,
generic claims, and feature ideas are not findings until anchored to behavior.
Every interview should include one question whose answer could disprove the
current diagnosis.

---

## Owner / GM Interview (45–60 min)

### Opening (2 min)
"My job over the next two weeks is to find where this business loses time and money to manual work, and put a dollar figure on each one. Nothing you say gets attributed to anyone in the report. The more candid, the more useful the result."

### The business (10 min)
- How does money come in — walk me through your revenue streams by rough %?
- Which type of work is most profitable? Least? *(Note if they can't answer — that's a [[data-and-dashboard-pathway|dashboard]] finding in itself.)*
- What are you trying to grow toward in the next 2 years?
- What's constraining that — sales, capacity, cash, people?

### Operations pain (15 min)
- Where does work get stuck? Where do balls get dropped?
- What's the most expensive mistake that happens repeatedly?
- What do YOU do every week that feels beneath your pay grade?
- Intake said [X] was the wand-wave problem — tell me the story of the last time it bit you.

### Numbers fishing (10 min)
- How long from lead to quote? Quote to answer? Job done to invoice sent? Invoice to paid?
- How many leads/quotes/invoices a month? What share of quotes win? Do you know why the losers lose?
- If a lead calls and nobody's available, what happens to it?

### Systems & appetite (10 min)
- What software do you pay for and secretly hate?
- Past automation/software attempts — what did you learn?
- If I find $100K/yr of recoverable waste, what budget appetite exists to fix it? *(Sets Phase 1 sizing — [[pricing-models|Pricing Models]].)*

### Close (3 min)
- "What should I have asked that I didn't?"
- Confirm staff interview list and the live observation session.
- Ask for one advancement commitment: named introduction, live observation,
  sample non-sensitive data, or decision-maker participation.

## Frontline Staff Interview (30–45 min)

### Opening (2 min)
"I'm mapping how work actually flows here — not evaluating anyone. The people doing the work always know where the real problems are, which is why I'm talking to you and not just the boss. Nothing gets attributed to you."

### Their day (10 min)
- Walk me through yesterday, start to finish.
- What do you do every single day/week that's basically the same each time?
- What do you retype, copy-paste, or enter into two systems?

### The dig (15 min) — for each repetitive task named:
- Show me. *(Screen share or over-the-shoulder — this is the most valuable 10 minutes of the audit.)*
- How long does one take? How many per day/week?
- What goes wrong? How do you catch it when it does?
- What are the exceptions/weird cases? *(These define automation feasibility — [[workflow-automation-pathway|Workflow Automation]].)*
- Who touches this before you? After you?

### The gold questions (5 min)
- "What's the dumbest thing you have to do here?" *(Verbatim quote — often the report's headline finding.)*
- What would you automate first if you could?
- What information do you constantly wait on others for?

### Close
- "If a new person started tomorrow, what would take them longest to learn?" *(Maps tribal knowledge — feeds [[internal-ai-assistants-pathway|assistant pathway]] later.)*

## After Each Interview (10 min, immediately)
- [ ] Log each repetitive task: **task / minutes per instance / instances per period / who / systems touched / failure modes**
- [ ] Separate facts and observed behavior from compliments, hypotheticals, and ideas
- [ ] Record the next commitment and owner; "keep me posted" is not advancement
- [ ] Flag discrepancies vs. what the owner or intake said
- [ ] Update the running waste table ([[smb-ai-audit-method|audit method Step 3]]) while numbers are fresh
