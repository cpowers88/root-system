# Case Study Template

> The standard format for turning every delivered project into a sales asset — plus the process for actually capturing them.

## Purpose
Provide a fill-in-the-blanks template and a capture process so that proof accumulates automatically from delivery, instead of depending on memory and good intentions.

## Key Idea
In this market, **proof outsells promises 10:1**. An SMB owner doesn't believe "AI can transform your business"; they believe "a plumbing company 40 minutes from you cut quote turnaround from 2 days to 2 hours and closed 22% more jobs." Every project already contains a case study — because the [[fulfillment-system|fulfillment system]] captures baseline metrics at kickoff and results at handoff. This page just formats what delivery already produced.

## Why It Matters
- Case studies are the highest-converting sales asset in every channel of the [[sales-system|Sales System]] — outreach, partner referrals, talks, proposals.
- Vertical case studies compound vertical dominance ([[market-map|Market Map]]): three HVAC case studies make you *the* HVAC systems firm in your region.
- They discipline delivery: knowing every project ends in a published before/after keeps scoping honest and measurement rigorous.

## The Capture Process (When and How)
1. **At kickoff:** baselines recorded in the scope doc (already mandatory — [[fulfillment-system|Fulfillment System]]).
2. **In the contract:** a case-study clause — client agrees to an anonymized case study, with named/quoted version by approval. Founding-client discounts are *traded* for this clause ([[pricing-models|Pricing Models]]).
3. **At handoff (satisfaction peak):** measure the afters, ask for the quote, draft within one week while details are fresh.
4. **At 30/90-day check-in:** update results with matured numbers — they're usually better, and updated case studies beat new-project recency.
5. **Publish and arm:** one-page PDF for proposals, short version for LinkedIn/outreach, verbal version rehearsed for sales calls and talks.

## Practical Actions
- Put the template in your docs system now; pre-fill one with a hypothetical to test the format.
- Add the case-study clause to your standard agreement this week.
- Set the rule: **no project is "closed" in the PM board until its case study is drafted or explicitly waived.**

## Intermediate Version
Every case study includes the human-agent angle — what the staff's job became, not just what got automated ("their office manager now approves AI-drafted quotes in 20 minutes instead of assembling them for 6 hours") — because that's the detail that disarms the next prospect's staff-replacement fear. Aggregate stats begin: 3+ studies per pathway in the primary vertical.

## Beginner Version
Your first "case studies" can be internal builds and founding-client projects, honestly labeled. Even "I automated my own invoicing and lead tracking; here's the before/after" beats zero proof. Target: 3 real client case studies by month 6 ([[first-90-days|First 90 Days]]).

## Advanced Version
A case-study library organized by vertical and pathway, refreshed quarterly, with aggregate stats ("across 23 clients: average 14 hrs/week saved, average payback 2.7 months") — which becomes the firm-level proof that closes bigger clients and, eventually, impresses acquirers ([[ten-year-scale-plan|Ten-Year Scale Plan]]).

## Revenue Connection
Every case study lowers customer-acquisition cost and raises close rate and price tolerance simultaneously — it's the only sales asset that appreciates. A vertical with three strong case studies typically sees close rates double and referral velocity follow.

## Human-Agent Management Connection
The strongest case studies document the [[human-role-redesign|role shift]] alongside the metrics: named humans doing better work is the proof that the [[progressive-operating-thesis|thesis]] holds in the field, and it's the line that makes the next owner's staff root *for* the project instead of against it.

## Risks / Failure Modes
- **No baseline captured** — the unfixable failure; without a before, there's no case study, ever. Baselines at kickoff, no exceptions.
- **Waiting for permission that never comes** — the case-study clause goes in the contract, not in a hopeful email six months later.
- **Inflated numbers** — one exaggerated result discovered by a prospect poisons the whole library; conservative and verifiable, always.
