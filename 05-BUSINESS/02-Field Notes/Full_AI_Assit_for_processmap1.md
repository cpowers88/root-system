  ## 22. Process-level foundations needed first

  Before choosing software, the company needs:

  - One job identifier
  - One customer record
  - One defined workflow
  - One owner for each step
  - One place for current job state
  - Standard intake fields
  - Standard site-visit fields
  - Standard scope format
  - Standard estimating assumptions
  - Standard proposal format
  - Standard change-order process
  - Standard daily log
  - Standard job-cost categories
  - Standard invoice process
  - Standard closeout process
  - Explicit approval rules
  - Explicit exception rules
  - Evidence and document retention rules
  - Basic access permissions
  - Backup and recovery
  - Human review gates
  - Measurable baseline performance

  The highest-value starting points from your map are:

  1. Written change-order approval before extra work.
  2. Estimate assumptions and job-cost tracking.
  3. Centralized customer/job information.
  4. Estimate-to-actual profitability review.
  5. Payment documentation and collection workflow.

  Those five attack the demonstrated money leaks without jumping immediately to building a giant construction platform.
