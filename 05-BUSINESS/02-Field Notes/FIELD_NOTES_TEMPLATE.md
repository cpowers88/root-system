## Business Observation Log | Chris Powers

---

## HOW TO USE THIS
Two observations minimum per week. Fill this out within 24 hours of the observation.
These are future clients and future case studies. Do not let them live only in your head.

---

## OBSERVATION TEMPLATE

**Date:**
**Business type:**
**Location/context:**

**What I observed:**
(Describe the operation, process, or interaction you witnessed)

**Where the friction is:**
(Be specific — what slowed down, broke down, or got lost?)

**The root cause:**
(Is this a people problem, a process problem, or a technology problem?)

**What state lives where:**
(Where is the information being stored? Head, spreadsheet, email, system?)

**What feedback exists:**
(How does the business know if this process worked or failed?)

**What breaks if I remove this:**
(What depends on this person, spreadsheet, or process?)

**Potential solution:**
(Keep it high level — what would fix this and roughly how?)

**Revenue potential:**
(What would a business pay to fix this? Estimate honestly.)

**Follow-up action:**
(Do I know someone at this business? Should I reach out?)
