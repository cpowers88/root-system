# Process Map of Workflow Observation 1

## Step 1 - First Contact
- Lane: Customer → Owner / Estimator
- Action: Customer call / emails / texts ; contact information, job description in customers words, and meeting set if needed.
- Systems touched: Whatever is around to record the relevant information as lots of time is done while working on another job.
- Output: Appointment time and date set for site visit

## Step 2 - Site Visit
- Lane: Owner / Estimator → Customer
- Action: First eyes on job, meet and great with home owner or customer, measurements taken, scope of work defined and shared with customer, any relevant notes for the task to be completed.
- Systems touched: Notebook or Ipad and camera 
- Output: All needed contact information for future contaact with customer, a very good idea of scope of work, and all the data to get an accurate estimate completed.

## Step 3 - Estimate
- **This is different process for all companies and the newer the company the more stressful pricing a job can be.**
- Lane: Owner / Estimator
- **Painful process of putting an accurate number on the work to be performed without going to high so you lose the job and without going to low that you hardly or don't turn a profit.**
- Action:  This is a point to look at especially with small construction companies lots break down every nail and board that goes into every job and guess at labor with no room for error just to attempt to get the job without being undercut. There is so much lost in no organized pricing strategy across the industry.
- Systems touched: Auto CAD, any estimation software available to company, pen and paper, online web search (pricing material). The smaller the company the harder and more stressful this process is.
- Output: Price for scope of work, exact scope of work, timeline of job, rough material list or better.

## Step 4 - Discussing Estimate With Client
- Lane: Owner / Estimator → Customer
- Action: Quick phone call and email of estimate and exact scope of work along with a discusion to make sure everything is covered and everyone is on the same page for the work to be completed for set price and exactly what is and isn't included.
- System touched: Phone call and Email to customer and previous estimate
- Output: Client desides if they will move forward with us, if they have more questions, concerns or edits to contract, or if they are simply not interested.

## Step 5 - small to medium size jobs - Hopefully Job Start Preperations **LARGER JOBS DIVERGE HERE, requiring more preparations**
- Lane: Owner / Project Manageer → Jobsite readiness
- Action: Order all needed material, deliver all tools, and obtain permit (when needed) so the first day on the job runs smoothly
- Systems touched: Supplier (home depot or equivilant), Phone and internet (placing orders), Truck (delivery of tools and sometimes materials)
- Output: Jobsite ready to roll on day one of job

## Step 6 - Do the Work 
- Lane: Builders
- Action: Get to the job and complete the task at hand, this is were the labor is actually done
- Systems touched: tools, raw materials, blue prints
- Output: The envisioned goal of the client

### Step 6a - Changes
- Lane: Builder → Owner → Customer
- Action: Extras often found as the job goes on, example a rotten silplate inside the house when building a deck, or mold inside the walls when they need to be opened to do work like drywall or window replacement.
- Systems touched: On smaller jobs **this is often never even recorded** and done with just a phone call explanation to the client and approval.
- Output: When work is completed clients always like to negotiate for these after the fact if the are even willing to pay for them and business owners done't like to bring people to court or pusue it further as they believe it will be expensive and be bad for thier name branding.

## Step 7 - Final Client Meeting and Payment
- Lane: Owner / Estimator → Client
- Action: Meet with client on jobsite and review the work completed and obtain the customers approval
- Systems touched: The final product and communication with client
- Output: a happy client and payment collected — often late, often less than invoiced

**Alternate Job ending**
- Lane: When clients feel like they are paying too much
- Action: sometimes it is as simple as the job was completed very fast and the customer doesn't think your knowledge is worth the money they are paying as the time to do the job doesn't reflect the cost in thier eyes, sometimes this happens when extas are added and clients don't feel responsible after they gave the approval to do the work, sometimes people are just not happy.
- Output: This is the worst cases ending in either partial payment, no payment, or court either way costs more time and money and aggrevation.
