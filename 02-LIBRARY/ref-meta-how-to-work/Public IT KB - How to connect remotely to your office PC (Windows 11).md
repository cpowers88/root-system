### Introduction

The following describes how to connect remotely to your office PC from off-campus using a VPN connection and a remote desktop connection.

### Instructions

1. After [connecting to VPN using Global Protect](https://kennesaw.service-now.com/sp?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0013387) on your off-campus PC, click the **Start** menu.
2. To search for the Remote Desktop Connection application, begin entering the term **remote desktop**.
3. As you type, the search results will begin to populate with matches to the text you entered. Click **Remote Desktop Connection**.

![](https://kennesaw.service-now.com/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=df4606291b135e10857311b6b04bcbec)

**Open Remote Desktop Connection**

4. Enter the **full name** of your office computer, adding **win.kennesaw.edu** or **dyn.kennesaw.edu** to the end of your computer name (e.g. KSUP12345.win.kennesaw.edu or KSUP12345.dyn.kennesaw.edu).  
	**Note:** To find the name of your computer, please see the article [How to find your PC's device name](https://kennesaw.service-now.com/sp?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0014713).
5. Click **Connect**.

![](https://kennesaw.service-now.com/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=308646691b135e10857311b6b04bcbb1)

**Enter Computer Name/Connect**

6. Your **login name** will be populated (e.g., WIN\\juser1). Enter your **password**.

**Note:** If your login name does not include the domain name WIN\\, click the **More choices** link to enter WIN\\NetID as the login name.

7. Click **OK**.

![](https://kennesaw.service-now.com/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=778686691b135e10857311b6b04bcb7e)

**Enter Credentials**

**Note**: You may configure and save both computername.win.kennesaw.edu and computername.dyn.kennesaw.edu as separate connections to allow you to select what works better for you in your current location.

8. You are now remotely connected to the device entered.

![](https://kennesaw.service-now.com/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=6a960a691b135e10857311b6b04bcbd1)

**Connected to PC**
