## What this is

"An open-access textbook for any first course in economics, whether
principles, micro, or macro," built around real-world problems and data by
CORE Econ, a cooperative of knowledge producers (key contributors: Wendy
Carlin, UCL/CORE director; Samuel Bowles, Santa Fe Institute; Camila Cea,
University of Chile). Explicitly organized around contemporary issues —
inequality, climate change, financial instability, the future of work.

**Microeconomics — Actors, Institutions, and Outcomes**

4. Inflation and unemployment
5. Macroeconomic policy: Inflation and unemployment
6. The financial sector: Debt, money, and financial markets
7. Macroeconomic policy in the global economy
8. Economic dynamics: Financial and environmental crises
9. Uneven development on a global scale
10. Government as economic actor: Economics, politics, public policy

## Why this is here

Secondary/supplementary open text alongside OpenStax for ECON 1000 —
strongest specific fit for the syllabus's market failure, government failure,
inequality/redistribution, and macro-policy (inflation/unemployment)
chapters, which this text treats with more real-world data than a standard
principles text.
