# SYSTEMS

The engineering-of-systems corpus: feedback structure, stock-and-flow modeling,
factory physics, queuing theory, MRP and inventory theory, operations research,
process mining, and business-cycle dynamics.

Built to serve two things at once — ISYE 2600 readiness, and the ability to walk
into an unfamiliar operation and diagnose where time, money, and capacity leak.

Start with:

1. `HOW_TO_USE.md` for the human workflow.
2. `wiki\index.md` — the complete retrieval map.
3. `wiki\raw-source-coverage-and-intake-status.md` — what has and has not been
   read, and why.

`OPERATIONS.md` is the local contract for any AI working here.

Most of this corpus was inherited from FORGE (retired July 7, 2026). A
`North Star Connection` section on an inherited page is an application
hypothesis, not doctrine.

`raw\` is immutable. Presence in `raw/` is not coverage — the ledger is the only
statement of what was actually read.
