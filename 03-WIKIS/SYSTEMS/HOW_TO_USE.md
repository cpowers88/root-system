## Standard Work Loop
```text
concept -> diagnostic question/model -> course/audit application -> evidence
```
Many inherited pages have a `North Star Connection` section. Read it as a possible
school, systems, or business application; the loop closes only when the concept is
used correctly, not when the connection paragraph is read.

## What Counts as Proof
A concept is proven when it correctly diagnoses or models a real situation — an
independent practice/course example within academic-integrity limits, a practice
audit finding, or a client-diagnosis pattern actually applied. Reading is not proof.

## Outputs and Where They Go
Study application stays in-vault. If a pattern becomes a repeatable audit diagnostic,
route it to `05-BUSINESS\06-Capability Library` or
`00-BRAIN\CASTLE\wiki\skills\field-observation.md`; do not duplicate the concept.

## Boundaries
Distinct from `03-WIKIS\BUSINESS` (offer layer, audit method, client-facing pathways) and `03-WIKIS\TECHNOLOGY` (tool/landscape research) — SYSTEMS feeds both but owns neither. No orphan knowledge: every concept studied connects to ISYE 2600 prep or a named audit use case.

## How the Hub Learns From Use
New ISYE coursework or system-dynamics sources follow the existing pages' shape (Summary/Sources/Last updated, Key Ideas, Connects to, North Star Connection, Ranking, Use/Retrieval Notes) so the wiki stays one consistent format as it grows past its FORGE-inherited base.
