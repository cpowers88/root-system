# HOW TO USE — SYSTEMS

This wiki answers: **what system-dynamics or ISYE concept is worth knowing, and
what audit or coursework does it strengthen?**

It's a reference corpus you retrieve from, not a staged path you walk. That
changes when ISYE 2600 activates.

## Start

1. `wiki\index.md` — the complete retrieval map. Everything is listed there.
2. `wiki\raw-source-coverage-and-intake-status.md` — what's been read, what
   hasn't, and why. Check this before assuming a source was covered.

Retrieve by topic tag or property. Inherited pages also answer to their
FORGE-era `subject/...` tags — `subject/queuing-theory`,
`subject/factory-physics`, and similar.

## Work loop

```text
concept -> diagnostic question or model -> course/audit application -> evidence
```

The loop closes when the concept is *used* correctly, not when the page is read.

## What it's for — both at once

- **School:** the ISYE 2600 spine and the engineering fundamentals under it.
- **Audit work:** walking into an unfamiliar operation and finding where time,
  money, and capacity actually leak.

Every page should answer "how does this help diagnose a real operation," not
just "what does the textbook say."

## What counts as proof

A concept is proven when it correctly diagnoses or models something real — a
course example within academic-integrity limits, a practice audit finding, or a
diagnosis pattern actually applied. Reading isn't proof. A synthesis page isn't
proof.

## Reading inherited pages

Most of this corpus came from FORGE. Two things to know:

- A `North Star Connection` section is an **application hypothesis**, not
  settled business doctrine. Check `CURRENT_STRATEGY.md` before treating one as
  current strategy.
- A `Ranking` table says how and when a page should be used.

## Boundaries

- Distinct from `03-WIKIS\BUSINESS` (offer layer, audit method, client-facing
  pathways) and `03-WIKIS\TECHNOLOGY` (tool and landscape research). This hub
  feeds both and owns neither.
- A pattern that becomes a repeatable audit diagnostic gets **routed** to
  `05-BUSINESS\06-Capability Library` or CASTLE's `field-observation` skill page
  — not copied there.
- No orphan knowledge: every concept connects to ISYE prep or a named audit use
  case.
- `raw\` is immutable.

## Close

Log which pages were used and how. Update a `Ranking` line only if its timing
really changed, `wiki\index.md` only if a page was added, and the coverage
ledger whenever a source's disposition moved.

## Where the rules live

`OPERATIONS.md` is the machine contract every AI follows here. You don't need to
read it to use the wiki.
