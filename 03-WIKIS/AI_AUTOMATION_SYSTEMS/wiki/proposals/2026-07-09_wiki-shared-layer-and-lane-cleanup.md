## Why Better Than Status Quo

One copy of every shared rule is the system's own stated law (AI_Agent.md
footer, WHERE_IT_GOES "one copy, zero drift"). The chunking rule was even
labeled "system-wide" while existing only as seven pastes. Dedup removes
the guaranteed future drift; the lane closure removes the one real
two-structures-one-lane overlap; the BUSINESS slim removes a live drifted
mission copy the night before go-live.

## Risk / Blast Radius

8 operating files + WHERE_IT_GOES. Boot chain unchanged — AI_Agent.md is
already loaded first in every session, so the shared rules are always in
context before any wiki file. Residual risk: a tool that loads ONLY a wiki
CLAUDE.md without the OS would see pointers instead of full rules —
accepted, since the router boot makes that a rule violation already. No
pages, raw files, or folder structures moved.
