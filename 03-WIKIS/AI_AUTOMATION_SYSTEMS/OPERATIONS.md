# AI_AUTOMATION_SYSTEMS — OPERATIONS

## Function

Maintain traceable knowledge about AI agents, automation, model platforms,
evaluation, safety, governance, adoption, and `.ROOT` self-evolution.

This wiki researches and proposes. It does not activate tools, change strategy,
or govern `.ROOT`.

## Authority

| Owns | Authority |
|---|---|
| Permanent direction and AI limits | `01-NORTH_STAR\NORTH_STAR.md` |
| Current capability and value outcomes | Goals & Milestones |
| Governance and architecture decisions | `00-BRAIN` and Chris |
| Review, activation, and sequencing | CASTLE and `NOW.md` |
| AI/agent/automation knowledge | this wiki |
| General and applied technology reference | `03-WIKIS\TECHNOLOGY` |
| Python implementation learning | `03-WIKIS\PYTHON` |
| Business methods, markets, and offers | `03-WIKIS\BUSINESS` |
| External change signals | Watchtower |

Research here MAY produce a proposal. Only Chris or the owning review mechanism
may promote it into live governance or execution.

## INGEST

1. State the exact research question and intended owner.
2. Rank sources by authority, density, currency, independence, and relevance.
3. Read large sources in explicit complete chunks.
4. Separate verified fact, source claim, inference, hypothesis, and unknown.
5. Update an existing page before creating another.
6. Mark provider/product claims with source and as-of date.
7. Record source disposition in `raw-source-coverage.md`.
8. Update `index.md` and append `log.md`.

## QUERY

1. Enter through `index.md`.
2. Load only the relevant cohort.
3. Prefer primary and current evidence for volatile mechanics.
4. Preserve contradictions and context-dependent variants.
5. Route durable outputs to the correct owner.
6. Do not treat research volume as proof or authorization.

## LINT

Check:

- unresolved links, orphans, and index drift;
- duplicate pages or mixed ownership;
- stale provider features, prices, shutdown dates, and regulations;
- source claims presented as verified conclusions;
- cross-source synthesis based on non-independent sources;
- proposals without outcome, evidence, verdict, or review trigger;
- raw sources without disposition;
- research that should return to BUSINESS, TECHNOLOGY, CASTLE, or Watchtower;
- system recommendations that bypass approval.

## Proposal contract

Every proposal MUST state:

1. observed friction and evidence;
2. exact owner and affected files;
3. proposed change;
4. expected benefit;
5. risk and blast radius;
6. post-change check and `check_at`;
7. approval status; and
8. after application, outcome plus keep/modify/revert verdict.

## Safety and raw boundary

`raw\` is immutable. AI MUST NOT create, edit, move, rename, archive, or delete
anything under it without Chris explicitly authorizing a named exception.

Research does not authorize software installation, external communication,
credentials, purchases, deployment, autonomous background work, or governance
changes.

## Close

A change is complete when its source coverage is honest, catalog navigation
resolves, contradictions and volatility are visible, the log records the
operation, and any proposed action is routed to an authorized owner.
