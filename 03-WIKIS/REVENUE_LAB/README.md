# REVENUE_LAB

Evidence-first research into revenue lanes that could fund Chris's studies.
Created July 14, 2026, after the school funding cut made additional income a
condition of continuing past Fall 2026.

This hub scores lanes. It does not earn, publish, or commit.

Start with:

1. `HOW_TO_USE.md` for the human workflow.
2. `wiki\revenue-lane-scorecard.md` for the ranked lanes and gate result.
3. `wiki\index.md` for the full catalog.
4. `00-BRAIN\CASTLE\wiki\opportunity-queue.md` for live opportunity status —
   more current than this wiki.

`OPERATIONS.md` is the local contract for any AI working here.

Nothing is published, posted, priced, purchased, or sent without Chris's
explicit approval. Proof is money received or a pre-named measured indicator —
not a page, a score, or a built tool. `raw\` is immutable.
