# CLAUDE.md — Revenue Lab Wiki OS

## Purpose

Find, prove, and operate a digital revenue stream that funds Chris's studies —
evidence first, build second.

**Why this hub exists (recorded July 14, 2026):** a major cut in school funding
means Chris needs additional income to continue past the Fall 2026 semester.
That changed the profit-gate math: income that keeps Chris enrolled directly
serves the school spine, the #1 track. This hub is the contained home for that
work — including research that is *off the named audit-company path*. Off-path
research is allowed HERE by charter; it is not allowed to leak into the other
hubs or displace their charters.

The controlling question:

> What digital revenue lane, supported by Tier 1–2 evidence, fits inside
> Chris's existing daily footprint and produces income soonest — and what
> one-sentence proof project gets the first dollar?

## The Ranking Rubric — Every Lane Gets Scored On

1. **Time-to-first-dollar** — realistic, sourced, not hoped
2. **Daily-footprint fit** — must layer onto what Chris already does daily
   (at the PC, building `.ROOT`, studying, family life); it must not be a
   second job
3. **Skill overlap** — uses or grows skills already on the roadmap
   (Python, SQL, data analysis, AI tooling, construction expertise)
4. **Compounding value** — does it build an owned asset (audience, product,
   reputation) or just trade hours
5. **Variance** — how wide is the gap between median and best-case outcome;
   survival money cannot ride on a lottery ticket alone

A portfolio answer is allowed (e.g., one fast-money lane + one compounding
lane in parallel) — but each lane still gets scored honestly on its own.

## Division of Labor

- This wiki **researches, scores, and proposes**. It follows the same
  eyes-not-hands split as every other hub.
- A lane graduates from research to build only through the CASTLE profit gate
  (`00-BRAIN\CASTLE\wiki\decision-rules\adding-a-profit-skill.md`) — re-run
  with the current gate inputs, which now include the funding constraint.
- **Consequential actions stay Chris-approved, always**: publishing content,
  posting on any platform, outreach, pricing, spending money, creating
  accounts. Research never publishes.
- Once a lane is live and earning, this hub tracks its numbers (revenue,
  hours, growth) as evidence pages — real data over vibes.

## System Boundary

- `03-WIKIS\BUSINESS` owns the audit/retainer company — the March 2027
  first-client path. That charter is untouched. If a revenue lane here feeds
  that engine later (audience → leads, content → trust), it hands off through
  the normal review cadence.
- `03-WIKIS\TECHNOLOGY` owns general tech-landscape research;
  `03-WIKIS\AI_AUTOMATION_SYSTEMS` owns AI/agent research. If a lane needs a
  tool evaluated, that research happens in the owning hub and gets referenced
  here.
- Fixed school commitments and academic-integrity rules
  (`AGENT.md § Academic Integrity`) are absolute. October 5 – November 11,
  2026 is a high-load window: AI warns once when optional revenue work threatens
  those commitments, recommends the smallest safe scope, then follows Chris's
  decision. Any chosen path must tolerate reduced capacity during that window.
- Chris's family and private life appearing in content is Chris's call alone,
  per piece, always.

## Shared Wiki Rules

The shared layer for all `03-WIKIS` hubs — raw/ immutability, large-source
chunking, session start/close minimums, update-over-create, contradiction
flagging, recency markers, and the lint pass — lives in
`00-BRAIN\AGENT.md § Wiki Shared Layer`. One copy, zero drift.

## Strategy and Watchtower Return

Revenue Lab may test an adjacent continuity-income vehicle without redefining
Chris's identity. Results return to CASTLE and the continuity-income milestone in
`CURRENT_STRATEGY.md`. A verified external platform/market change reaches
Watchtower only when it is material and names its evidence page, affected
assumption/choice, consequence/test, and review trigger. Lane scans and our own test
results remain here.

## Final Operating Principle

Evidence governs AI-initiated recommendations. A path with no Tier 1–2 support
doesn't get built on AI initiative no matter how exciting it feels. Chris may
authorize a direct test after an explicit impact review; record that override
and its success/stop evidence. A boring path with strong evidence and fast
money beats an exciting one with neither. The rubric exists so survival decisions don't
get made on hope.
