# Lane C — Online Tutoring (Math / Physics / Intro Programming)
### Evidence page | Scan Session 1 — July 14, 2026 | Status: EVIDENCE GATHERED, scored

## The Hypothesis

Steady per-hour digital income that reinforces Chris's own coursework — every
tutoring hour is also a study rep for the KSU spine.

## Tier 1 Evidence (platform primary sources)

- **[Wyzant fee structure](https://support.wyzant.com/tutors/tutor-payments/what-is-the-fee-structure-for-tutors-listed-on-wyzant/):**
  flat **25% platform fee — tutor keeps 75%** of posted rate, every lesson.
  Exception: students the tutor refers pay out at 100%.
- **[Preply commission model](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/4171383-preply-commission-model):**
  **33% for new tutors**, sliding to 18% after 400+ taught hours — and Preply
  takes **100% of each new student's trial lesson**. Materially worse than
  Wyzant for a newcomer.

## Tier 2 Evidence (market rates)

- **[Wyzant's own pricing guide](https://blog.wyzant.com/how-much-does-tutoring-cost/):**
  platform average **$35–60/hr** posted rate.
- Math tutors on the platform average around **$55/hr** posted
  ([Glassdoor aggregate](https://www.glassdoor.com/Hourly-Pay/WyzAnt-Math-Tutor-Hourly-Pay-E333464_D_KO7,17.htm));
  credentialed physics tutors run $40–80/hr, but **peer-level/uncredentialed
  tutors run $15–35/hr** — that's the honest bracket for a first-year ISYE
  student without teaching history.

## Honest Position Check

Chris's credible tutoring surface *today* is middle/high-school math
(algebra, geometry, word problems) — strong numerical aptitude, 20 years of
applied jobsite math, father-of-9 experience explaining things to kids. It is
**not** college physics (he's at Stage 3 Vectors himself) or programming
(Stage 2 Python). Realistic posted rate: **$25–40/hr → $19–30/hr net** after
Wyzant's 25%.

## The Footprint Problem (the real weakness)

Tutoring demand concentrates on **evenings and weekends** — exactly the hours
a family of nine and Heather's realtor schedule already claim, and exactly the
hours Fall coursework will compete for. Unlike Lane B, sessions are
appointments: fixed, recurring, unmovable without disappointing a paying
family. This is the worst daily-footprint fit of the fast-money lanes, and it
gets worse — not better — when the semester starts and during danger weeks.

## Independent Evidence Audit — July 14, 2026 (Codex)

The original research is retained above. This pass separates verified platform
facts from the personal earnings forecast.

### Verified

- Wyzant verifies that tutors retain **75%** of their posted rate and Wyzant
  retains a 25% platform fee. Wyzant's public math-tutor page currently shows
  an average **$33–55/hr** range; this is a marketplace reference, not proof
  that a new tutor will book at that rate.
- Wyzant changed tutor payments to **semimonthly** (the 1st and 15th), for
  lessons submitted at least five days before the payment date. The earlier
  "weekly" payout claim was incorrect.
- Preply verifies 100% commission on each new-student trial lesson and an
  18–33% commission on subsequent lessons; it is a poor newcomer cash-flow
  option relative to Wyzant's stated fee.

### Corrections / Unknowns

- $25–40/hr as Chris's starting posted rate, a 2–4-week first booking, and
  $300–900 monthly by December are not source-backed forecasts. They require a
  gate-approved test and actual inquiry/booking data.
- Student matching, cancellation risk, and recurring evening availability make
  the income stream less predictable before a tutor has clients; the original
  variance score overstated certainty.
