# Lane E — AI-Assisted / Faceless Content Variants
### Evidence page | Scan Session 3 — July 14, 2026 | Status: EVIDENCE GATHERED, scored (evidence-only standard)

## The Hypothesis

Lower-lift content formats (AI voiceover, screen-capture, shorts pipelines,
faceless channels) trade personality for volume — supplemental income without
on-camera time.

## Tier 1 Evidence — The Policy Wall

- **[YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en):**
  effective July 15, 2025, the "repetitious content" policy was renamed and
  clarified as **"inauthentic content"** — mass-produced, templated, or
  repetitive content is **not eligible to monetize**. Named examples include
  image slideshows with minimal narrative and AI-generated content made with
  generic templates "without adding the creator's original, authentic insights
  or perspective." Platform enforcement has included suspensions of AI-volume
  channels at scale.
- **What remains eligible (same source):** AI-assisted content that carries
  original creative vision, insight, or narrative — with the "altered or
  synthetic content" disclosure toggled where relevant. YouTube's rule targets
  mass-production, not AI use.
- **TikTok Creator Rewards** (Session 2 evidence) requires **original** videos
  ≥60s — the same wall from the other platform.

## The Structural Finding — E Is Not a Lane, It's a Production Method

The faceless-volume model — the version of E that promises income without
presence — is precisely what the platforms' own policies now demonetize. The
*compliant* version of E (use AI tooling to cut scripting/editing/production
hours on content carrying Chris's real insight) *is just Lane A produced
efficiently.* There is no evidence-backed standalone lane here.

This is also a slop-risk boundary the charter already anticipated: content
built to game volume rather than deliver Chris's genuine knowledge fails both
the platform policy (Tier 1, documented) and the North Star's proof-led brand
intent (`HANDOFF_0713_CODEX.md`: "publish the real systems, learning, and
value created; never create content merely to maintain a posting machine").

use AI tooling to compress production hours on original-insight content, with
the synthetic-content disclosure used where relevant.
