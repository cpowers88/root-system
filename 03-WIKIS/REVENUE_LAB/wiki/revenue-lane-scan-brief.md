# Revenue Lane Scan — The First Evidence Brief
### Created: July 14, 2026 | Status: ACTIVE — this is the hub's first deliverable
### Driver: school-funding cut; income needed to continue studies past Fall 2026

## The Real Requirement (design against this, not against excitement)

- **Money by:** meaningful income arriving or clearly inbound by ~December 2026
  (end of first semester). Anything whose realistic first dollar is later than
  that can only be the *compounding* lane of a portfolio, never the survival lane.
- **Hours available:** fits inside the existing CASTLE-tagged capacity
  (~26h/wk now, shrinking hard at semester start Aug 24; near zero new capacity
  during danger weeks Oct 5 – Nov 11).
- **Daily-footprint constraint (Chris, July 14):** the lane must layer onto what
  already happens daily — at the PC, building `.ROOT`, studying, family life.
  Recording/documenting existing actions is cheap; net-new production is not.
- **Assets on hand:** ~20 years construction/estimating expertise · Python
  (Stage 2) + SQL (building) + AI-tooling fluency (advanced daily user) ·
  a real systems-build story (.ROOT itself, ISYE degree path) · large-family
  lifestyle with genuine content range · extroverted on-camera-plausible
  personality · Heather's real-estate network.

## Evidence Bar (per lane, before scoring)

1. At least one **Tier 1 source** — platform's own docs: monetization
   requirements, payout mechanics, policy constraints (e.g., YouTube Partner
   Program thresholds, TikTok monetization terms, Upwork fee structure).
2. At least one **Tier 2 source** — credible market data: earnings studies,
   platform transparency reports, rate cards, marketplace listing data
   (actual posted freelance rates count; Reddit income screenshots do not).
3. **Named numbers** — time-to-first-dollar estimate with the source that
   justifies it; realistic monthly range at 3, 6, 12 months; hours/week.
4. Tier 4 material (creator hype, "how I made $10k" videos) may suggest where
   to look. It scores nothing.

## Scoring

Each lane gets the five rubric scores from `CLAUDE.md` (time-to-first-dollar,
daily-footprint fit, skill overlap, compounding, variance), 1–5, with a
one-line justification each, on its evidence page. The scan's final output:

- **`revenue-lane-scorecard.md`** — the ranked table, one screen
- **A portfolio recommendation** — likely shape: one survival lane (fast
  money) + one compounding lane (audience/asset), each with a one-sentence
  proof project — taken to the CASTLE gate for the build decision
- **What it displaces** — named honestly against the current weekly reps

## Time-box

Three research sessions, target completion within one week (by ~July 21).
Session 1: Lanes B + C (fast-money lanes — survival first). Session 2: Lane A
(content data-scan design + platform monetization facts). Session 3: Lanes
D + E + scorecard + portfolio recommendation. If a session's evidence comes
up thin, the lane scores what the evidence supports — no extensions to
protect a favorite.

## Standing Rules for This Scan

- Research never publishes, posts, or creates accounts — Chris approves all
  consequential actions per `CLAUDE.md`.
- No build work (no channel setup, no scripts, no product drafting) until the
  scorecard exists and the gate has been re-run on the recommendation.
- Wrong estimates get recorded and kept when reality reports back —
  the rubric learns from misses.
