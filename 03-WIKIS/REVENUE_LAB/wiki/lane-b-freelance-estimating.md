# Lane B — Freelance Construction Estimating / Takeoff
### Evidence page | Scan Session 1 — July 14, 2026 | Status: EVIDENCE GATHERED, scored

## The Hypothesis

Contractors and GCs pay remote freelancers real money for estimating, takeoffs,
and change-order/scope writeups. Chris's ~20 years of construction expertise is
deliverable from the PC. Fastest plausible first dollar of the five lanes.

## Tier 1 Evidence (platform/government primary sources)

- **[BLS Occupational Outlook — Cost Estimators](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/business-and-financial/cost-estimators.htm):**
  median wage **$77,070/yr (May 2024)** ≈ $37/hr employed baseline; top 10%
  >$128,640. The skill has established, government-documented market value.
- **[Upwork freelancer service fee](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee):**
  variable **0–15% per contract** (typically ~10%), locked when the proposal is
  sent. Proposals cost Connects ($0.15 each, 2–16 per job).
- **[Upwork hourly payment timing](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063668-How-payments-for-hourly-contracts-work):**
  weekly billing (Mon–Sun) + client review + security period → funds available
  **~10 days after the work week ends**. Money moves fast once work exists.

## Tier 2 Evidence (market data)

- **[Upwork construction-estimating category](https://www.upwork.com/hire/construction-estimating-freelancers/):**
  **427 open construction estimator jobs** at scan time; services in demand:
  takeoffs, cost estimates, GC bidding, PlanSwift/Bluebeam workflows.
- **[Upwork on-screen takeoff specialists](https://www.upwork.com/hire/on-screen-takeoff-freelancers/us/):**
  posted rates from **~$45/hr**; weekly-package listings around $1,500/40hr.
- **Competition signal (honest):** top-ranking freelancer profiles in the
  category include heavily offshore providers (e.g., Lahore-based) at low
  rates. Pure quantity-takeoff labor is price-compressed. The defensible US
  premium is **judgment work**: scope interpretation, change-order writeups,
  bid sanity-review, "unknowns" pricing — exactly what
  `05-BUSINESS\02-Field Notes\observation_one.md` documents contractors
  struggling with.

## The B2 Variant — Direct Network (no platform)

Chris's own field notes are themselves evidence: owners doing estimates on
pen-and-paper at 16 hours/day, and Chris reports contractor friends "still
call me to kick quotes around before sending them." A direct arrangement
(remote estimate/change-order support for 1–3 known contractors, simple
invoice, no platform fee, no ramp-up) could produce a first dollar in **1–2
weeks** instead of the platform's 3–6 week proposal grind. This variant needs
one thing the platform doesn't: Chris naming a price to people he knows —
which doubles as the neutral change-order replay interview the CASTLE gate
already asked for (July 14, 09:41). One conversation, two purposes.

## Independent Evidence Audit — July 14, 2026 (Codex)

The original research is retained above. This pass separates what the cited
sources prove from what still needs a real-world test.

### Verified

- BLS verifies that the **employed** cost-estimator occupation had a $77,070
  May 2024 median annual wage ($37.05/hr), and that experienced construction
  workers can qualify without the typical bachelor's degree. It does **not**
  establish a freelance rate or Chris's likely earnings.
- Upwork verifies a **0–15% per-contract** freelancer fee and that standard
  hourly earnings are available 10 days after a work week ends. For fixed-price
  work, funds clear five days after client approval. Those are payment mechanics,
  not evidence that a contract will arrive in a particular time window.
- Upwork's current category page supports real demand for the service and shows
  a wide, price-compressed market: current public examples span roughly $10–65/hr,
  while its client guide lists small fixed-price estimating work at $150–800.

### Corrections / Unknowns

- The earlier "427 open jobs" and "~$45/hr" are volatile page observations and
  cannot carry the score. A later public category view showed 272 sample jobs;
  neither count is a durable market-size measure.
- **1–2 weeks direct**, **3–6 weeks platform**, $35–55/hr net, $40–65/hr direct,
  and the December monthly ranges are hypotheses, not source-backed forecasts.
  Treat them as numbers to test only after a gate-approved proof project exists.
- The market does support paid estimating work, but quote acquisition and price
  competition make income variance materially higher than the original score said.
