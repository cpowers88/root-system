# REVENUE_LAB_WIKI — OPERATIONS

## Function

Find, score, and prove a revenue lane that funds Chris's studies. Evidence
first, build second.

This hub researches and scores. It does not earn, publish, or commit.

## Why this hub exists

Recorded 2026-07-14: a major cut in school funding means Chris needs additional
income to continue past Fall 2026. Income that keeps Chris enrolled directly
serves the school spine, so it clears the profit gate's relevance test on its
own.

This is the one hub where **off-the-named-path research is permitted by
charter** — "not part of the current Advisor-Builder vehicle" is not a
disqualifier here. That permission is contained: off-path research MUST NOT
leak into another hub or displace its charter.

The controlling question:

> What revenue lane, supported by Tier 1–2 evidence, fits inside Chris's
> existing daily footprint and produces income soonest — and what one-sentence
> proof project gets the first dollar?

## The ranking rubric

Every lane is scored 1–5 on each, for 25 total:

1. **Time-to-first-dollar** — realistic and sourced, not hoped.
2. **Daily-footprint fit** — layers onto what Chris already does daily (at the
   PC, building `.ROOT`, studying, family life). A second job scores low.
3. **Skill overlap** — uses or grows skills already on the roadmap: Python,
   SQL, data analysis, AI tooling, construction expertise.
4. **Compounding value** — builds an owned asset (audience, product,
   reputation) versus trading hours.
5. **Variance** — spread between median and best case. Survival money cannot
   ride on a lottery ticket.

A portfolio answer is allowed — one fast-money lane plus one compounding lane —
but each lane is still scored honestly on its own.

## Evidence standard

Tiers, as used throughout the lane pages:

| Tier | What it is | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Platform or government primary source — official policy, documented threshold, published rate, regulation. | Can support a recommendation. |
| **Tier 2** | Market data — observed rates, published market figures, third-party measurement with a named method. | Can support a recommendation. |
| **Tier 3** | Market hypothesis or source lead — vendor claim, anecdote, forum figure, single self-report. | May suggest where to look. Never supports a recommendation. |

Rules:

- Every rate, threshold, RPM, policy, and market size carries an as-of date and
  source. These age fast; an undated number is not evidence.
- Vendor, affiliate, anecdotal, and self-reported figures MUST be labeled.
- A forecast is a scenario until real outcomes calibrate it.
- Platform mechanics being documented does **not** make demand-for-Chris
  verified. Name the unverified acquisition step explicitly in every lane.
- Willingness to talk is not willingness to pay. A proposal is not revenue.
  Revenue is recorded only when received.

## Operations

### SCAN and INGEST

1. State the lane question and which rubric criterion the source can move.
2. Classify each source by tier, date, scope, and volatility before using it.
3. Read large sources in bounded chunks; record coverage.
4. Update an existing lane page before creating a new one.
5. Separate verified claim, source claim, inference, hypothesis, and unknown.
6. Record contradictions and supersession explicitly; never overwrite a claim
   silently.
7. Update `wiki\index.md` and append `wiki\log.md`.

### SCORE

1. Score all five criteria; show the reasoning, not just the number.
2. A criterion with no Tier 1–2 support scores on the unverified case, not the
   hoped case.
3. State the single unverified step that would most change the score.
4. Re-score when new evidence lands; keep the prior score visible with its date.

### QUERY

1. Read `wiki\index.md`, then the scorecard.
2. Check CASTLE's opportunity queue for live status before answering.
3. Answer with claim status and source traceability.
4. Do not convert a conversation, estimate, or scorecard into strategy or proof.

### LINT

Check: unresolved links and moved external targets; stale or undated rates,
policies, and platform thresholds; contradictions and silently superseded
claims; lane pages whose score no longer matches their evidence; scorecard
disagreement with CASTLE's opportunity queue; scenarios presented as plans;
proof claimed from research; and index-versus-tree drift.

## Action and safety

Pages here MAY explain and recommend an action. They MUST NOT perform or
authorize one.

**Chris explicitly approves, every time:** publishing or posting anything on
any platform, outreach of any kind, pricing, spending money, creating accounts,
contracts, filing, and use of private or client data.

Research never publishes. A scanner, script, or dataset built here is internal
evidence tooling until Chris approves an external step. An approved bounded
test is approved for exactly its stated scope, cost, and time cap — record the
cap and stop at it.

## Gate and return

A lane graduates from research to build only through the CASTLE profit gate
(`00-BRAIN\CASTLE\wiki\decision-rules\adding-a-profit-skill.md`), re-run with
current inputs including the funding constraint.

This hub runs the **PROVE** and **MEASURED OUTCOME** stages of the System Loop.
Results return through the Return Packet to CASTLE and the continuity-income
milestone in `CURRENT_STRATEGY.md` — both canonical in
`01-NORTH_STAR\System Contracts\ROOT_CAPABILITY_CONTRACT.md`. Do not define a
competing loop or packet here.

A verified external platform or market change reaches Watchtower only when it is
material and names its evidence page, affected assumption, consequence or test,
and review trigger. Lane scans and our own test results stay here.

## Proof

Proof is **real dollars received**, or a measured leading indicator explicitly
named in that lane's proof sentence before the test ran.

A research page is not proof. A ranked scorecard is not proof. A built tool is
not proof. An approved test is not a result.

## Learning from misses

Every operated lane records predicted versus actual — time-to-first-dollar,
hours spent, revenue. **Wrong predictions are kept and marked, never
overwritten.** The rubric improves from its own misses, which requires the
misses to remain visible.

## Close

Append `wiki\log.md`. Update `wiki\index.md` when pages or lane status changed.
Reconcile against CASTLE's opportunity queue and record any disagreement found.
State the single next action and, for any lane in motion, its evidence status.

A change is complete when claims stay traceable, navigation resolves, external
targets still exist, and a fresh session can retrieve lane status without oral
history.

## Final operating principle

Evidence governs AI-initiated recommendations. A path with no Tier 1–2 support
does not get built on AI initiative no matter how exciting it feels. Chris may
authorize a direct test after an explicit impact review; record that override
and its stop evidence.

A boring path with strong evidence and fast money beats an exciting one with
neither. The rubric exists so survival decisions do not get made on hope.
