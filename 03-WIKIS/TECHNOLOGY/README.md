# TECHNOLOGY

Two layers in one hub:

- **Landscape research** — what tools and categories exist, and when one becomes
  worth learning or recommending.
- **Applied reference** — how the thing actually works, pulled from directly
  during audits and builds. Largely inherited from FORGE (retired July 7, 2026).

Start with:

1. `HOW_TO_USE.md` for the human workflow.
2. `02-LIBRARY\REF-AI-AUTOMATION\TECHNOLOGY_LIBRARY_STRATEGY.md` — the 12-category
   spine and Recommendation Ladder, and the landscape frontier of record.
3. `wiki\index.md` for applied lookups.
4. `wiki\raw-source-coverage-and-intake-status.md` for what has and hasn't been
   read.

`OPERATIONS.md` is the local contract for any AI working here.

The applied collection is a retrieval library, not a study queue — page count is
not a signal of what to learn next.

This hub ages faster than any other: dated claims carry an as-of marker and need
re-verification before use. `wiki\ai-and-llm\` is closed to new intake — AI and
agent research goes to `03-WIKIS\AI_AUTOMATION_SYSTEMS`. `raw\` is immutable.
