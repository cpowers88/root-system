# Unit Conversion Drill

## Skill Being Practiced

Converting between SI and US customary units, including compound units (speed, density).

## Prerequisites

[[../concepts/unit-conversion]], [[../concepts/si-base-units]]

## Instructions

Convert each quantity, showing the conversion factor(s) used and confirming which units cancel.

## Problems

1. Convert 100 miles to kilometers.
2. Convert 38.0 m/s to mi/h.
3. Convert 2.70 × 10³ kg/m³ (density of aluminum) to g/cm³.
4. A room is 4.0 m × 3.0 m. Convert its area to square feet.
5. Convert 9.46 × 10¹⁵ m (one light-year) to miles.

## Check Yourself

Problem 1: ≈160.9 km. Problem 2: ≈85.0 mi/h. Problem 3: 2.70 g/cm³ (densities in g/cm³ and kg/m³ relate by a factor of 1000 — confirm you get the same numeral, just shifted).

## Mastery Signal

Chris converts a compound unit (like density or speed) correctly on the first attempt, without flipping a conversion ratio.
