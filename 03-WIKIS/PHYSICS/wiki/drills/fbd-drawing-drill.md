# FBD Drawing Drill

## Skill Being Practiced

Drawing correct free body diagrams from a verbal description of a physical situation.

## Prerequisites

[[../concepts/force]], [[../concepts/free-body-diagram]], [[../concepts/normal-force]]

## Instructions

For each scenario below: (1) draw the object as a dot, (2) draw and label every force acting ON the object, (3) choose a coordinate system and write the ΣF = 0 or ΣF = ma equation for each axis. Do not solve for numbers yet — just set up the equations.

## Problems

**Problem 1.** A book sits at rest on a horizontal table.
*Forces on book: weight (down), normal force from table (up).*

**Problem 2.** A box slides to the right across a rough horizontal floor (kinetic friction present). No rope or applied force.
*Forces on box: weight (down), normal (up), kinetic friction (left, opposing motion).*

**Problem 3.** A person pushes a crate to the right across a rough floor with a horizontal force F.
*Forces on crate: weight (down), normal (up), applied force (right), kinetic friction (left).*

**Problem 4.** A block hangs at rest from a single vertical string.
*Forces on block: weight (down), tension T (up).*

**Problem 5.** A block sits at rest on a frictionless inclined plane at angle θ (30°). 
*Forces on block: weight mg (straight down), normal force n (perpendicular to incline surface). The weight has components along and perpendicular to the slope.*

**Problem 6.** Two blocks, A on top of B. B is on a horizontal frictionless surface. A horizontal force F is applied to B. Draw the FBD for each block separately.
*Block B: weight_B down, normal from floor up, applied force F right, normal from A down, friction from A (direction depends on acceleration).
Block A: weight_A down, normal from B up, friction from B (reaction to friction on B).*

**Problem 7.** A hanging traffic light of mass m is supported by two cables making angles θ₁ and θ₂ with the horizontal.
*Forces on light: weight mg (down), tension T₁ (up-left at angle θ₁), tension T₂ (up-right at angle θ₂).*

## Check Yourself

- Every force arrow starts at the object (or from the dot representing it).
- All forces are labeled (n, w, f_k, T, F, etc.).
- No reaction forces are drawn (forces the object exerts on other things don't belong here).
- You chose a clear +x and +y direction.
- You wrote ΣF_x = ma_x and ΣF_y = ma_y using the labeled forces.

## Mastery Signal

Chris can draw a correct FBD for any of these scenarios within 90 seconds, without prompting.
