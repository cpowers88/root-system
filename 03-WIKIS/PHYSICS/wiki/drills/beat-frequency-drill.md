# Beat Frequency Drill

## Skill Being Practiced

Calculating beat frequency and using beats to tune instruments or identify frequency differences.

## Prerequisites

[[../concepts/superposition-principle]], [[../equations/beat-frequency]]

## Key Equation

```
f_beat = |f₁ − f₂|
```

The beat frequency equals the absolute difference between the two source frequencies. The combined sound grows louder and softer at this rate.

## Problems

1. Two tuning forks vibrate at 440 Hz and 444 Hz simultaneously. How many beats per second are heard?

2. A piano string is supposed to be tuned to concert A = 440 Hz. When struck together with a 440 Hz tuning fork, 3 beats/second are heard. What are the two possible frequencies of the string?

3. Two guitar strings are played together and 2 beats/second are heard. One string vibrates at 330 Hz. What are the possible frequencies of the second string?

4. Two sound sources produce 5 beats/second. One has frequency 512 Hz. If the other source's frequency is then increased slightly (by tightening the string), the beat frequency drops to 3 beats/second. What is the original frequency of the second source, and what is its new frequency?

5. A student is trying to tune a violin string to 196 Hz using a digital tuner. She hears 4 beats/second. She tightens the string slightly and hears 6 beats/second. Is the string now higher or lower than 196 Hz? What was the original frequency?

6. Three tuning forks have frequencies 440 Hz, 443 Hz, and 447 Hz. How many distinct beat frequencies would be heard if all three are struck simultaneously?

## Challenge

7. Two organ pipes — one open (L₁ = 2.00 m) and one closed (L₂ = 1.05 m) — are sounded simultaneously. Using v = 343 m/s, find the fundamental frequency of each and the beat frequency.

## Check Yourself

Problem 1: f_beat = |444 − 440| = 4 beats/second
Problem 2: Either 443 Hz or 437 Hz (3 above or 3 below 440)
Problem 4: If increasing frequency reduces beats from 5 to 3: the second source must have been ABOVE 512 Hz (originally 517 Hz → moved to 515 Hz). Or if it was below (507 Hz → moved to 509 Hz), beats would increase from 5 to a larger number — contradiction. So original = 517 Hz.

## Mastery Signal

Chris can calculate beat frequency, determine which of two sources is higher/lower based on how beats change when one is adjusted, and use beats conceptually for tuning problems.
