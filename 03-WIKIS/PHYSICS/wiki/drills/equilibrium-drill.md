# Static Equilibrium Drill

## Skill Being Practiced

Applying both conditions of equilibrium (ΣF = 0 and Στ = 0) to solve for unknown forces, tensions, and reaction forces on beams, ladders, and hinged structures.

## Prerequisites

[[equilibrium-conditions]], [[../problem-types/static-equilibrium-beam]], [[../problem-types/ladder-against-wall]]

## Instructions

For each problem: (1) Draw a complete extended free body diagram showing WHERE each force acts. (2) Choose a pivot point (pick it wisely to eliminate an unknown). (3) Write Στ = 0 about that pivot. (4) Write ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0. (5) Solve for unknowns.

## Problems

### Part A — Simple Beam Problems

1. A uniform 4.0 m beam of mass 30 kg is supported at both ends. A 70 kg person stands 1.0 m from the left end. Find the support forces at each end.

2. A 5.0 m horizontal beam of mass 20 kg is attached to a wall at the left end and supported by a vertical rope at the right end. A 100 kg load hangs 3.0 m from the left end. Find the rope tension and the wall reaction force.

3. A 6.0 m uniform beam weighs 200 N. It is supported at the left end by a pivot and at the right end by a support force that acts at 60° above the horizontal. Find the magnitude of the support force.

### Part B — Ladder Problems

4. A 4.0 m uniform ladder of mass 15 kg leans against a smooth vertical wall at 65° from the horizontal. The floor is rough. A 75 kg person stands at the midpoint of the ladder. Find the normal force from the floor, the friction force at the floor, and the normal force from the wall.

5. What is the minimum angle a ladder can make with the floor before it slips, if the coefficient of static friction at the floor is 0.40? The wall is smooth. The ladder has uniform mass.

### Part C — Mixed

6. A 3.0 m horizontal rod of mass 2.0 kg is attached to a wall by a pivot at the left end. A wire attached at the right end makes a 40° angle above the horizontal and supports the rod. A 5.0 kg mass hangs from the right end. Find the wire tension and the pivot reaction force.

## Check Yourself

Problem 1: Take pivot at left end. Στ = 0: −(30)(9.8)(2.0) − (70)(9.8)(1.0) + F_R(4.0) = 0. F_R = (588 + 686)/4.0 = 318.5 N. F_L = (30+70)(9.8) − 318.5 = 980 − 318.5 = 661.5 N.

Problem 4: F_wall = (15)(9.8)(2.0 cos 65°) + (75)(9.8)(2.0 cos 65°) / (4.0 sin 65°). F_floor_y = (15+75)(9.8) = 882 N. f = F_wall.
