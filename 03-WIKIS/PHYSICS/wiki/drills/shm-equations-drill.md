# SHM Equations Drill

## Skill Being Practiced

Writing and applying x(t), v(t), and a(t) for simple harmonic motion; finding period, frequency, and angular frequency for spring-mass and pendulum systems.

## Prerequisites

[[../concepts/simple-harmonic-motion]], [[../equations/shm-position]], [[../equations/shm-period-spring]], [[../equations/shm-period-pendulum]]

## Instructions

For each problem, identify the amplitude, angular frequency, and phase constant before writing the position equation. Then answer the specific question.

## Problems

### Part A — Period and Frequency

1. A 0.50 kg mass is attached to a spring with k = 20 N/m. Find ω, T, and f.

2. A 2.0 kg mass oscillates on a spring with T = 0.80 s. What is k?

3. A simple pendulum has L = 1.20 m. Find its period on Earth (g = 9.8 m/s²). On the Moon (g = 1.6 m/s²)?

4. What length pendulum has a period of exactly 1.00 second on Earth?

### Part B — The Position Equation

5. A spring-mass system has A = 0.15 m, ω = 4.0 rad/s, and φ = 0 (starts at max displacement). Write x(t) and v(t). Find x and v at t = 0.50 s.

6. A mass starts from rest at the equilibrium position. It reaches amplitude A = 0.10 m. Write x(t) with the correct phase constant. (Hint: if x(0) = 0 and v(0) = v_max, use sine.)

7. At t = 0, a mass is at x = +3.0 cm moving in the −x direction, and A = 5.0 cm. Find the phase constant φ. (Hint: x(0) = A cos φ and v(0) < 0 → φ ∈ (0, π/2).)

### Part C — Acceleration

8. A mass in SHM has ω = 6.0 rad/s and A = 0.20 m. What is the maximum acceleration? At what position does this occur?

9. At x = 0.10 m (halfway between equilibrium and amplitude A = 0.20 m), find the acceleration if ω = 5.0 rad/s.

### Part D — Challenge

10. A mass-spring system has A = 0.25 m and T = 2.0 s. At t = 0, the mass is at x = 0 moving in the +x direction. (a) Write x(t). (b) At what time does the mass first reach x = +A/2?

## Check Yourself

Problem 1: ω = √(k/m) = √(20/0.50) = √40 = 6.32 rad/s; T = 2π/ω = 0.994 s; f = 1/T = 1.006 Hz
Problem 3 (Earth): T = 2π√(1.20/9.8) = 2π(0.350) = 2.20 s
Problem 5: x(t) = 0.15 cos(4.0t); v(t) = −0.60 sin(4.0t); at t = 0.50 s: x = 0.15 cos(2.0 rad) = 0.15(−0.416) = −0.062 m

## Mastery Signal

Chris can write x(t) from initial conditions, find period from k and m (or L and g), and evaluate x, v, and a at any time — in under 3 minutes per problem.
