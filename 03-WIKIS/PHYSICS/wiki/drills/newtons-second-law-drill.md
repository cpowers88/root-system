# Newton's Second Law Drill

## Skill Being Practiced

Applying ΣF = ma along each axis to find acceleration or unknown forces, using a FBD as the starting point.

## Prerequisites

[[../concepts/newtons-second-law]], [[../concepts/free-body-diagram]], [[../drills/fbd-drawing-drill]]

## Instructions

For each problem: draw the FBD, write ΣF_x = ma_x and ΣF_y = ma_y, and solve. Show units in every step.

## Problems

**Problem 1 — Flat surface, no friction.**
A 5.0 kg block is pushed to the right by a 20 N horizontal force on a frictionless surface. Find the acceleration.

**Problem 2 — Flat surface, friction.**
A 10 kg crate is pushed with 50 N to the right across a floor with μ_k = 0.20. Find the acceleration. (g = 9.80 m/s²)

**Problem 3 — Vertical (hanging mass).**
A 3.0 kg object hangs from a string. The object accelerates upward at 2.0 m/s². Find the tension in the string.

**Problem 4 — Two-block system (horizontal, frictionless).**
Block A (2 kg) is behind block B (3 kg). A 10 N force pushes A into B on a frictionless surface. Find (a) the acceleration of the system and (b) the contact force between the blocks.

**Problem 5 — Inclined plane (frictionless).**
A 4.0 kg block slides down a frictionless incline at 30°. Find the acceleration down the slope.

**Problem 6 — Inclined plane with friction.**
A 6.0 kg block is pulled up a 25° incline by a rope with tension T = 40 N. μ_k = 0.15. Find the acceleration up the slope. (g = 9.80 m/s²)

**Problem 7 — Atwood machine.**
Mass m₁ = 3.0 kg and m₂ = 5.0 kg hang over a frictionless massless pulley. Find (a) the acceleration and (b) the string tension.

## Answers (Check Only After Attempting)

1. a = 4.0 m/s²
2. n = 98 N, f_k = 19.6 N, a = (50 − 19.6)/10 = 3.04 m/s²
3. T − mg = ma → T = m(g + a) = 3.0(9.80 + 2.0) = 35.4 N
4. a = 10/(2+3) = 2.0 m/s²; contact force F_AB = m_B × a = 3 × 2.0 = 6.0 N
5. a = g sinθ = 9.80 × sin30° = 4.9 m/s²
6. Along slope (+up): T − mg sin25° − μ_k mg cos25° = ma; solve for a.
7. a = (m₂ − m₁)g/(m₁ + m₂) = (5−3)(9.80)/(8) = 2.45 m/s²; T = m₁(g + a) = 3(9.80 + 2.45) = 36.7 N
