# Inclined Plane Drill

## Skill Being Practiced

Setting up the inclined-plane coordinate system, decomposing weight correctly, and applying Newton's 2nd Law along and perpendicular to the slope.

## Prerequisites

[[../concepts/newtons-second-law]], [[../concepts/friction]], [[../problem-types/inclined-plane]]

## Instructions

Each problem involves an object on a slope. For each: (1) draw the FBD, (2) label axes parallel and perpendicular to the slope, (3) write ΣF_∥ = ma and ΣF_⊥ = 0 (or ma if accelerating perpendicular — usually 0), (4) solve.

## Problems

**Problem 1 — Frictionless slide.**
A 2.0 kg block slides freely down a 35° frictionless incline. Find its acceleration.
*(a = g sin35° = 9.80 × 0.574 = 5.62 m/s² down the slope)*

**Problem 2 — Block held by friction.**
A 5.0 kg block is at rest on a rough 25° incline. Find the static friction force.
*(ΣF_∥ = 0: f_s = mg sin25° = 5 × 9.80 × 0.423 = 20.7 N up the slope)*

**Problem 3 — Sliding with friction.**
A 4.0 kg block slides down a 40° incline with μ_k = 0.20. Find the acceleration.
*(Along slope: mg sin40° − μ_k × mg cos40° = ma. a = g(sin40° − 0.20 cos40°) = 9.80(0.643 − 0.153) = 4.80 m/s²)*

**Problem 4 — Pushed up an incline.**
A 3.0 kg block is pushed up a 30° incline by a force F parallel to the slope. μ_k = 0.25. The block accelerates up at 1.5 m/s². Find F.
*(Along slope (+up): F − mg sin30° − μ_k mg cos30° = ma. F = m(a + g sin30° + μ_k g cos30°) = 3(1.5 + 4.9 + 2.12) = 25.6 N)*

**Problem 5 — Finding the angle for impending motion.**
For a block on an incline with μ_s = 0.45, what is the maximum angle θ at which the block will remain stationary?
*(At impending motion: tan θ = μ_s. θ = arctan(0.45) = 24.2°)*

**Problem 6 — Inclined plane with a horizontal applied force.**
A 6.0 kg block is on a 20° incline. A horizontal force F = 30 N pushes the block into the slope (toward the surface). No friction. Find the normal force and acceleration along the slope.
*(n must balance: mg cos20° + F sin20°. ΣF_∥: F cos20° − mg sin20° = ma. Solve for a.)*

## Mastery Signal

Chris can set up any inclined-plane FBD in under 2 minutes with correct force decomposition and axis labeling.
