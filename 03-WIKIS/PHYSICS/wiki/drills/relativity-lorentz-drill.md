# Special Relativity — Lorentz Factor, Time Dilation, and Length Contraction Drill

## Skill Being Practiced

Calculating γ for given speeds, applying time dilation (Δt = γΔt₀) and length contraction (L = L₀/γ), and identifying who measures proper time and proper length.

## Prerequisites

[[../concepts/time-dilation]], [[../concepts/length-contraction]], [[../equations/lorentz-factor]], [[../equations/time-dilation]], [[../equations/length-contraction]]

## Key Equations

```
γ = 1/√(1 − v²/c²)      (always ≥ 1; equals 1 when v = 0)

Time dilation:   Δt = γ Δt₀    (stationary observer sees longer time)
Length contraction: L = L₀/γ   (moving observer measures shorter length)

c = 3.00×10⁸ m/s
```

## Identifying Proper Quantities

- **Proper time Δt₀**: measured by the observer traveling WITH the clock (the "moving" observer).
- **Proper length L₀**: measured in the rest frame of the object (the "stationary" frame relative to the object).

## Problems

### Part A — Lorentz Factor

1. Calculate γ for v = 0.50c, 0.80c, 0.90c, 0.99c, and 0.999c. Tabulate your results. What do you notice as v → c?

2. At what speed v (in terms of c) does γ = 2.0? At what speed does γ = 10?

### Part B — Time Dilation

3. A muon created in the upper atmosphere has a proper lifetime of 2.20 μs (as measured in its rest frame). It travels at v = 0.995c. What is its dilated lifetime as measured by an observer on Earth?

4. An astronaut travels at 0.80c for what the astronaut's clock measures as 5.0 years. How much time elapses on Earth?

5. Twins: one stays on Earth, one travels at 0.90c for 10 years of Earth time. How much does the traveling twin age?

6. A particle's proper half-life is 1.00×10⁻⁸ s. At what speed must it travel so that Earth observers measure a half-life of 4.00×10⁻⁸ s? Find v/c.

### Part C — Length Contraction

7. A spaceship is 200 m long in its rest frame. It passes Earth at 0.60c. What length does an Earth observer measure?

8. A meter stick moves past you at 0.80c parallel to its length. What do you measure for its length?

9. A muon (Problem 3) travels at 0.995c. The distance from the upper atmosphere to Earth's surface is 60 km as measured on Earth. What is this distance as measured in the muon's rest frame?

### Part D — Combined

10. A spaceship travels at 0.80c from Earth to a star 20 light-years away (Earth frame). (a) How long does the trip take in the Earth frame? (b) How long does the trip take as measured by the astronaut? (c) What is the Earth-star distance measured by the astronaut?

## Check Yourself

Problem 1: γ(0.50c) = 1.155; γ(0.80c) = 1.667; γ(0.90c) = 2.294; γ(0.99c) = 7.089; γ(0.999c) = 22.37
Problem 3: Δt = γΔt₀ = (10.01)(2.20 μs) = 22.0 μs [γ at 0.995c ≈ 10.01]
Problem 7: L = L₀/γ = 200/γ(0.60c). γ = 1/√(1−0.36) = 1/√0.64 = 1.25 → L = 200/1.25 = 160 m

## Mastery Signal

Chris can calculate γ for any speed, correctly identify who measures proper time and proper length, and apply Δt = γΔt₀ and L = L₀/γ without mixing them up.
