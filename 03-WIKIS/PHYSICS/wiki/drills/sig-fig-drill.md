# Significant Figures Drill

## Skill Being Practiced

Applying the correct sig-fig rule (multiplication/division vs. addition/subtraction) and rounding only at the final step.

## Prerequisites

[[../concepts/significant-figures]]

## Instructions

For each problem, identify the operation type first, then compute and round correctly.

## Problems

1. How many significant figures: (a) 78.9 ± 0.2 (b) 3.788 × 10⁹ (c) 2.46 × 10⁻⁶ (d) 0.0053
2. Multiply 12.71 m × 3.46 m and report with correct sig figs.
3. Add 23.2 + 5.174 and report with correct decimal places.
4. Divide 23.94 g by 2.10 cm³ and report the density with correct sig figs.
5. Compute (1.000 1 + 0.000 3) and explain why the result keeps five sig figs even though one input only has one.

## Check Yourself

Problem 2: 44.0 m² (3 sig figs, limited by 3.46 m). Problem 3: 28.4 (one decimal place, limited by 23.2). Problem 4: 11.4 g/cm³ (3 sig figs).

## Mastery Signal

Chris correctly identifies which rule applies (mult/div vs. add/sub) before calculating, every time, without defaulting to one rule out of habit.
