## 1. Measurements and dimensions — 6 points

a. Name the SI base units for length, mass, and time. (1)

b. Convert 72.0 km/h to m/s, showing the conversion factors. (2)

c. A student writes x = x₀ + v₀t + (1/2)at for constant-acceleration motion. Is it dimensionally consistent? Identify and repair the faulty term. (2)

d. Evaluate 2.40 m × 3.2 m with appropriate significant figures. (1)

## 2. Reading motion from a graph — 8 points

A cart has the following velocity-versus-time graph. Plot these points and join consecutive points with straight lines:

| t (s) | 0 | 2 | 4 | 6 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| v (m/s) | 0 | 4 | 4 | −2 |

a. Find its acceleration during each of the three intervals. (3)

b. Find its displacement from t = 0 to t = 6 s. (2)

c. Find its total distance traveled over the same interval. (2)

d. At what time after the start does it reverse direction? (1)

## 3. Components and reconstruction — 10 points

A surveyor walks 30.0 m at 35.0° north of east, then 20.0 m due west. Take east as +x and north as +y.

a. Sketch and resolve the first displacement into components. (3)

b. Find the components of the net displacement. (2)

c. Find the magnitude and direction of the net displacement. (3)

d. Find the total distance walked and explain why it differs from the displacement magnitude. (2)

## 4. Differentiation and the physical meaning — 12 points

A cart's position is

x(t) = (2.00 m) + (6.00 m/s)t − (3.00 m/s²)t² + (0.500 m/s³)t³,

for 0 ≤ t ≤ 4 s, with rightward positive.

a. Obtain v(t) and a(t) by differentiation. (4)

b. Find all times in this interval when the cart is instantaneously at rest. (3)

c. At t = 1 s, state its direction of motion and whether it is speeding up or slowing down. Support your answer with signs. (3)

d. Explain physically what dx/dt measures and why being at rest does not require zero acceleration. (2)

## 5. Integration and initial conditions — 10 points

A cart on a straight track has a(t) = (2.00 m/s³)t. At t = 0, its velocity is −3.00 m/s and its position is 4.00 m. Take rightward as positive.

a. Derive v(t) and x(t), showing how both initial conditions determine the integration constants. (6)

b. Find its displacement between t = 0 and t = 3 s. (2)

c. Explain why that displacement is not the total distance traveled. (2)

## 6. Constant acceleration without a stated time — 10 points

A powered cart increases its speed from 8.00 m/s to 20.0 m/s while traveling 42.0 m along a straight track. Its acceleration is constant and forward is positive.

a. Find the acceleration. Explain why your chosen relationship fits the supplied information. (5)

b. Find the elapsed time. (3)

c. Check the 42.0 m displacement using a different relationship. (2)

## 7. Projectile launched from a platform — 12 points

A ball leaves a 12.0 m-high platform at 16.0 m/s, directed 30.0° above horizontal. It lands on level ground below the platform. Set the origin on the ground directly beneath launch, with +y upward and +x in the launch direction.

a. Sketch the motion and write x(t), y(t), vₓ(t), and vᵧ(t). (4)

b. Find the time to impact and horizontal distance traveled. (4)

c. Find the impact velocity components and impact speed. (3)

d. Explain why the shortcut R = v₀² sin(2θ)/g does or does not apply here. (1)

## 8. Incline with friction — 10 points

A 5.00 kg crate is already sliding down a ramp inclined at 25.0° above horizontal. The coefficient of kinetic friction is 0.200. No other applied force acts on it.

a. Draw a free-body diagram and choose axes. (3)

b. Find the normal force and kinetic-friction magnitude. (3)

c. Find the crate's acceleration. (3)

d. In one sentence, distinguish its weight from the normal force. (1)

## 9. Connected objects — 8 points

A 3.00 kg block rests on a frictionless horizontal table and is connected by a massless string over an ideal pulley to a hanging 2.00 kg block. The system is released from rest. The string stays taut.

a. Draw both free-body diagrams and write a force equation for each block with a consistent sign convention. (4)

b. Find the acceleration and string tension. (3)

c. Explain why the tension is not equal to the hanging block's weight. (1)

## 10. A banked curve — 8 points

A circular road of radius 40.0 m is banked at 20.0° above horizontal, with the outside edge higher than the inside edge. A car follows the curve at constant speed without requiring friction.

a. Determine the speed that permits this motion and the car's acceleration. Show your setup. (6)

b. Explain how it can accelerate while its speed stays constant. (2)

## 11. Relative velocity — 6 points

A boat moves at 4.00 m/s due north relative to the water. The water flows at 3.00 m/s due east relative to the shore.

a. Write the boat's velocity relative to shore in component form, with reference frames labeled. (2)

b. Find its speed and direction relative to shore. (2)

c. If the northward crossing width is 120 m, find the crossing time and eastward drift. (2)
