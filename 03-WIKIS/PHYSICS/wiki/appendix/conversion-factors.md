# Conversion Factors

**Source:** Appendix A, Table A.1

Use these to convert between SI and non-SI units. In PHYS 2211, always work in SI (m, kg, s, N, J) and convert at the start of a problem — not in the middle of algebra.

| 1 km | = 1000 m = 0.621 mi | |

| 1 mm | = 0.001 m = 10⁻³ m | |

## Speed

| From | To SI | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 m/s | = 3.281 ft/s | SI unit |
| 1 km/h | = 0.2778 m/s | ÷ 3.6 to get m/s |
| 1 mi/h (mph) | = 0.4470 m/s = 1.467 ft/s = 1.609 km/h | |
| 1 knot | = 0.5144 m/s | nautical speed |

**Quick trick:** km/h → m/s: divide by 3.6.

## Beginner Traps

**Trap 1:** Mixing cm and m in the same equation. Convert everything to m first.

**Trap 2:** km/h and m/s look similar but differ by a factor of 3.6. Always check: "did I convert speed?"

**Trap 3:** grams are not kilograms. A 500 g mass = 0.500 kg. Never plug grams into F = ma.

**Trap 4:** Degrees vs. radians. Calculators default to degrees. Physics equations using sin/cos in calculus require radians.
