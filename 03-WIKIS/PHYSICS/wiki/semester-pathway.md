### The one-week-ahead rule, and when to break it

Break the lead and work current-week material if any of these is true:

1. An exam is inside seven days — switch to retrieval on tested material.
2. A cold check on the current week fails. The lead is worthless if the
   foundation moved.
3. WebAssign is due and unfinished. Graded work outranks the lead.

Otherwise hold the lead. Losing it once is recoverable; the recovery is one
consolidation week, taken deliberately, not by drifting.
