# Cartesian Coordinates

## Plain-English Definition

A way to locate a point using two perpendicular distances: how far over (x) and how far up (y).

## Physics Meaning

Also called rectangular coordinates. Written (x, y), measured from a fixed origin O along two perpendicular axes. The default coordinate system for most of PHYS 2211.

## Units / Symbols If Applicable

x, y — units of length (m).

## Example

A point 3 m east and 4 m north of the origin has Cartesian coordinates (3, 4) m.

## Common Confusion

Confusing Cartesian (x, y) with polar (r, θ) — they describe the same point differently. See [[polar-coordinates]] and [[../concepts/coordinate-systems]].

## Flashcard

**Q:** What do the two numbers in a Cartesian coordinate pair (x, y) represent?
**A:** Perpendicular distances from the origin: x is horizontal, y is vertical.
