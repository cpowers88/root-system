# Order of Magnitude

## Plain-English Definition

A rough answer expressed as the nearest power of ten, used when exact precision isn't possible or necessary.

## Physics Meaning

Found by writing a number in scientific notation and rounding the power of ten up or down based on whether the multiplier is above or below √10 ≈ 3.16.

## Units / Symbols If Applicable

~ (read as "is on the order of").

## Example

720 m ~ 10³ m (order of magnitude 3); 0.0086 m ~ 10⁻² m.

## Common Confusion

Thinking an order-of-magnitude estimate needs to be precise — it only needs to be right to within roughly a factor of 10.

## Flashcard

**Q:** What does the symbol ~ mean in physics?
**A:** "Is on the order of" — a rough power-of-ten approximation.
