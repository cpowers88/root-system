# Trajectory

## Plain-English Definition

The path an object follows through space. For a projectile, the trajectory is a parabola.

## Physics Meaning

The curve traced by position (x, y) over time. You can find the equation of the trajectory by eliminating t from x(t) and y(t):

```
From x = v₀ₓ t  →  t = x / v₀ₓ
Substitute into y = v₀ᵧ t - ½g t²:
y = (v₀ᵧ/v₀ₓ)x - (g/2v₀ₓ²)x²
```

This is y = Ax - Bx² — the equation of a downward-opening parabola.

## Units / Symbols If Applicable

No special symbol. Plotted on an x-y coordinate graph.

## Example

A ball thrown at 30° traces a parabolic arc that rises, peaks, and falls symmetrically (if landing at the same height).

## Common Confusion

Confusing the trajectory (the geometric path in space) with the motion (which includes time). Two objects can have the same trajectory but different speeds — e.g., one thrown fast and one thrown slow, both at the same angle, follow different-sized parabolas.

## Flashcard

**Q:** What shape is the trajectory of a projectile (no air resistance)?
**A:** A parabola — derived by eliminating t from x(t) and y(t).
