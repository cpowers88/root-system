# Potential Energy

**Definition:** Stored energy associated with the position or configuration of an object in a force field. Two types in PHYS 2211: gravitational (U_g = mgy) and spring/elastic (U_s = ½kx²).

**Unit:** joule (J)

**Key fact:** Only conservative forces (gravity, spring) have associated potential energies. Friction does NOT have a potential energy.

**Flash fact:** "Potential" = it *could* become kinetic. A ball at height y has the potential to gain K = mgy by falling.
