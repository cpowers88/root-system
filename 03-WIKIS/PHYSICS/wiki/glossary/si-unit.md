# SI Unit

## Plain-English Definition

The agreed-upon international standard for measuring a quantity, so scientists anywhere in the world get the same result.

## Physics Meaning

SI (Système International) defines fundamental units of length (meter), mass (kilogram), and time (second), among others, established by international agreement in 1960.

## Units / Symbols If Applicable

m (meter), kg (kilogram), s (second).

## Example

A car's speed reported as 27 m/s uses the SI units of length (m) and time (s).

## Common Confusion

Confusing SI units with US customary units (feet, pounds, seconds) — this course uses SI almost exclusively.

## Flashcard

**Q:** What are the three SI base units used in mechanics?
**A:** Meter (length), kilogram (mass), second (time).
