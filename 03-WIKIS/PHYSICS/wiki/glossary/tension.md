# Glossary — Tension

**Definition:** The pulling force transmitted through a taut string, rope, cable, or rod along its length. Symbol: T. Units: N.

**Physical anchor:** A rope pulling a crate. The rope pulls the crate forward and simultaneously pulls the person backward — equal forces in opposite directions (Newton's Third Law).

**Key properties:**
- Tension always PULLS — strings cannot push. If a rope goes slack, T = 0.
- For a massless rope over a frictionless pulley: tension is the same throughout the entire rope (both sides of the pulley).
- For a rope with mass, tension varies along its length (more advanced, not needed in Stage 5).

**In FBDs:** On the FBD of an object attached to a rope, draw the tension arrow pointing AWAY from the object, along the rope toward where it connects.
