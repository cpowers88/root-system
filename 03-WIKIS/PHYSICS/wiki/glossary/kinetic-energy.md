# Kinetic Energy

**Definition:** The energy an object possesses due to its translational motion. K = ½mv².

**Unit:** joule (J)

**Key fact:** Always ≥ 0. Quadruples when speed doubles. Connected to work via the work-energy theorem: W_net = ΔK.

**Flash fact:** A 1 kg object moving at 1 m/s has K = 0.5 J. The same object at 2 m/s has K = 2 J — quadruple.
