# Velocity

## Plain-English Definition

How fast an object is moving and in which direction.

## Physics Meaning

Velocity is a vector: it has both magnitude and direction. In 1D, direction is captured by the sign (+ or −).

- **Average velocity:** v_avg = Δx/Δt (displacement over time interval)
- **Instantaneous velocity:** v = dx/dt (the slope of the x-t graph at one point)

## Units / Symbols

v (or v₀ for initial, v_f for final); SI unit: m/s.

## Example

A car moving 20 m/s to the east has velocity +20 m/s (east = positive). If it turns and goes 20 m/s west, velocity = −20 m/s. Same speed, opposite velocity.

## Common Confusion

Velocity (vector) ≠ speed (scalar). Speed = |v|, always positive. Velocity has a sign indicating direction.

## Flashcard

**Q:** How is instantaneous velocity defined using calculus?
**A:** v = dx/dt — the derivative of position with respect to time; equal to the slope of the x-t graph at that instant.
