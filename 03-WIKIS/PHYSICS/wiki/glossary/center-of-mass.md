# Glossary: Center of Mass

**center of mass** (CM) — The weighted average position of all mass in a system. For two objects: x_cm = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂)/(m₁ + m₂). The CM moves as if all the mass were located there and acted on by the net external force. Internal forces (collisions between parts) do not change the motion of the CM.
