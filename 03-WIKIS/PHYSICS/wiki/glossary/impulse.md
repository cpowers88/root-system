# Glossary: Impulse

**impulse** (J⃗) — The change in momentum delivered to an object by a force acting over a time interval. J⃗ = F⃗_avg · Δt = Δp⃗ = ∫ F⃗ dt. Units: N·s = kg·m/s. Graphically, impulse equals the area under a Force vs. time graph.
