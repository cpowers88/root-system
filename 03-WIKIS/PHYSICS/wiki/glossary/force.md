# Glossary — Force

**Definition:** A force is a push or pull — a vector interaction between two objects that can cause acceleration. Units: newton (N = kg·m/s²).

**Physical anchor:** Your hand pushing a door, gravity pulling you toward Earth, a rope pulling a crate.

**Key property:** Forces always come in source–recipient pairs (Newton's Third Law). You cannot have an isolated force without a reaction force somewhere.

**In equations:** ΣF = ma — net force produces acceleration. One newton accelerates a 1 kg mass at 1 m/s².
