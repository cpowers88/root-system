# Spring Constant

**Definition:** A measure of a spring's stiffness. Symbol: k. Appears in Hooke's Law: F_s = −kx and spring PE: U_s = ½kx².

**Unit:** N/m (newtons per meter)

**Physical meaning:** How many newtons of force the spring exerts per meter of displacement. k = 100 N/m means 100 N of force for every 1 meter stretched or compressed.

**Key fact:** Larger k = stiffer spring = more force per meter of displacement = more energy stored per meter of displacement.

**Flash fact:** You can determine k experimentally: hang a known mass on a spring, measure how much it stretches. Then k = F/x = mg/x.
