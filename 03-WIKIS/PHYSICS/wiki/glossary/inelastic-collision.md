# Glossary: Inelastic Collision / Perfectly Inelastic Collision

**inelastic collision** — A collision in which momentum is conserved but kinetic energy is NOT conserved. Some KE is converted to internal energy (heat, sound, deformation). Objects may separate after the collision.

**perfectly inelastic collision** — The extreme case of an inelastic collision where the objects stick together and move as one unit after the collision. Kinetic energy loss is maximum for a given initial momentum. Solved with a single momentum equation: m₁v₁ᵢ + m₂v₂ᵢ = (m₁ + m₂)vf.
