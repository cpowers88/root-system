# Power

## Plain-English Definition

How fast work is done or energy is transferred. A more powerful machine can do the same job in less time.

## Physics Meaning

P = W/Δt or P = Fv (when force is parallel to velocity)

## Units / Symbols

P; SI unit watt (W) = J/s. Also used: kilowatt (kW), megawatt (MW), horsepower (1 hp = 746 W).

## Example

A person climbing stairs in 10 s does 3000 J of work: P = 3000/10 = 300 W. A stronger athlete does the same stairs in 6 s: P = 3000/6 = 500 W. Same work, same energy — different power.

## Common Confusion

Power is a *rate* (per second), not a total amount. A 100 W lightbulb running for 1 hour uses 100 × 3600 = 360 000 J of energy — but its power is 100 W throughout.

## Flashcard

**Q:** What is the SI unit of power, and what does it equal in base units?
**A:** Watt (W) = J/s = kg·m²/s³.
