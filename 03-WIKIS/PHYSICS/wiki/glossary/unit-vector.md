# Unit Vector

A vector with magnitude exactly 1 and no units, used solely to indicate a direction.

**Standard unit vectors:** î (points in +x direction), ĵ (points in +y direction), k̂ (points in +z direction).

**Key properties:**
- |î| = |ĵ| = |k̂| = 1
- î·î = ĵ·ĵ = 1 (dot product of parallel unit vectors)
- î·ĵ = 0 (dot product of perpendicular unit vectors)
- î×ĵ = k̂ (cross product — appears in Stage 10)

**Usage:** Any vector can be written in terms of unit vectors: **v⃗ = vx î + vy ĵ**. The unit of the vector comes from the scalar multipliers (vx, vy), not from î or ĵ themselves.
