# Glossary — Weight

**Definition:** The gravitational force that Earth (or another planet/moon) exerts on an object. w = mg. Units: N (newtons). Weight is a force — not a mass.

**Physical anchor:** Your weight (≈700 N) is what a scale reads. On the Moon (g ≈ 1.62 m/s²), you weigh about 1/6 as much — but your mass is unchanged.

**Direction:** Always toward the center of the planet — for all ground-level problems, this means straight down.

**Distinguish from mass:** Mass (kg) is the amount of matter — it never changes. Weight (N) = mass × local gravitational acceleration. In everyday speech people say "weigh 70 kg" but physics demands "mass is 70 kg, weight is 686 N."

**In equations:** w = mg; it appears as the downward arrow on every free body diagram.
