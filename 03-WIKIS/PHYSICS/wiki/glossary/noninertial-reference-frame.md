# Noninertial Reference Frame

**A reference frame that is itself accelerating — Newton's laws do not hold in their simple ΣF = ma form unless a fictitious force is added.**

- Examples: a braking or accelerating car, an accelerating elevator, a spinning carousel.
- Contrast: an **inertial frame** is not accelerating; this is the frame assumed everywhere else in this course.
- Analyzing from inside a noninertial frame requires adding a [[fictitious-force]] term.

*First appears: Stage 6 (Ch 6.3). See [[../concepts/accelerated-reference-frames]].*
