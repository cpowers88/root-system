# Speed

## Plain-English Definition

How fast an object is moving, with no information about direction.

## Physics Meaning

Speed = |v| — the magnitude of velocity. Always positive or zero. A scalar.

- **Average speed** = total distance / total time (not displacement — this is the key difference from average velocity)
- **Instantaneous speed** = |v| at one instant

## Units / Symbols

No special symbol (often just written as |v| or "the speed"); SI unit: m/s.

## Example

A car drives 60 km north, then 60 km south, in 2 hours total.
- Average speed = 120 km / 2 h = 60 km/h
- Average velocity = 0 km/h (net displacement = 0)

## Common Confusion

Speed and velocity are not the same. Speed ignores direction; velocity captures it. A car going 60 mph around a curve has constant speed but changing velocity (direction is changing).

## Flashcard

**Q:** What is the difference between speed and velocity?
**A:** Speed = |v|, a scalar, always positive. Velocity = v, a vector with sign indicating direction. Average speed uses distance; average velocity uses displacement.
