# Position

## Plain-English Definition

Where an object is located, measured from a reference point (the origin) along a chosen axis.

## Physics Meaning

A coordinate value x, in meters, that can be positive (one side of origin) or negative (other side). Choosing the origin and positive direction is the first step of any kinematics problem.

## Units / Symbols

x (or y for vertical); SI unit: meter (m).

## Example

A ball at x = +3.5 m is 3.5 meters to the right of the origin. A car at x = −2.0 m is 2.0 meters to the left.

## Common Confusion

Confusing position with displacement. Position tells you *where* the object is. Displacement (Δx) tells you *how much* the position changed.

## Flashcard

**Q:** What is position in physics, and can it be negative?
**A:** Position x is the location of an object relative to a chosen origin. Yes — it is negative if the object is on the side of the origin you defined as negative.
