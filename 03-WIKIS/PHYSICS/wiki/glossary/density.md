# Density

## Plain-English Definition

How much mass is packed into a given amount of space.

## Physics Meaning

ρ = m/V — mass per unit volume of a uniform substance.

## Units / Symbols If Applicable

ρ (rho); SI unit kg/m³.

## Example

Iron has a density of about 7.86 × 10³ kg/m³; aluminum about 2.70 × 10³ kg/m³ — same volume, iron is much heavier.

## Common Confusion

Confusing density (a property of the *material*, independent of how much of it you have) with mass (a property of a *specific object*, depends on how much material is present).

## Flashcard

**Q:** What is the equation for density, and what are its units?
**A:** ρ = m/V, in kg/m³.
