# Dimension

## Plain-English Definition

The basic physical "category" a quantity belongs to — length, mass, or time — independent of which specific unit is used.

## Physics Meaning

Denoted with brackets, e.g. [v] = L/T. Two quantities can only be added or set equal if their dimensions match.

## Units / Symbols If Applicable

L (length), M (mass), T (time) — these are dimension symbols, not units themselves.

## Example

Speed has dimension L/T regardless of whether you measure it in m/s, mi/h, or any other unit.

## Common Confusion

Confusing "dimension" with "unit." Dimension is the category (length); unit is the specific scale used to measure it (meters, feet, miles).

## Flashcard

**Q:** What's the difference between a dimension and a unit?
**A:** Dimension is the category of physical quantity (e.g., length); unit is the specific scale used to measure it (e.g., meters).
