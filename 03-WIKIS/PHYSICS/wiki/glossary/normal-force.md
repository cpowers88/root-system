# Glossary — Normal Force

**Definition:** The contact force a surface exerts on an object perpendicular (normal) to the surface. It prevents the object from passing through the surface. Symbol: n. Units: N.

**Physical anchor:** The floor pushes up on your feet as you stand — that's the normal force. Without it, gravity would pull you through the floor.

**Direction:** Always perpendicular to (and away from) the surface. On a horizontal floor: straight up. On an inclined ramp: perpendicular to the slope's surface.

**Magnitude:** Found by applying Newton's 2nd Law perpendicular to the surface (usually zero acceleration that direction): n = mg on a flat floor; n = mg cosθ on an incline at angle θ.

**Not always equal to mg:** Add a downward push → n increases. On a slope → n = mg cosθ < mg. In an accelerating elevator → n ≠ mg.
