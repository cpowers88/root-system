# Displacement

## Plain-English Definition

The straight-line change in position from start to finish. How far and in what direction did the object end up from where it started?

## Physics Meaning

Δx = x_f − x₀. A vector quantity in 1D — has a positive or negative sign indicating direction. Does not depend on the path taken.

## Units / Symbols

Δx; SI unit: meter (m).

## Example

A runner goes 100 m east, then 30 m back west. Displacement = +70 m (east). Distance traveled = 130 m. These are different.

## Common Confusion

Displacement ≠ distance. Distance is total path length (always positive). Displacement is the net change in position (can be zero even if you traveled far).

## Flashcard

**Q:** What is the displacement formula, and how does it differ from distance?
**A:** Δx = x_f − x₀. Displacement is the change in position (can be negative or zero). Distance is total path length (always ≥ 0).
