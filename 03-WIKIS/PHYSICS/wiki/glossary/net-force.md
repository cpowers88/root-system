# Glossary — Net Force

**Definition:** The vector sum of all forces acting on an object. Also called the resultant force. Symbol: ΣF (sigma-F). Units: N.

**Physical anchor:** A tug-of-war: two teams pull in opposite directions. If one pulls with 500 N and the other with 600 N, the net force is 100 N toward the stronger team — and that's what accelerates the rope.

**Key point:** Net force determines acceleration, not any individual force. If two 100 N forces point in opposite directions, the net force is zero — the object doesn't accelerate even though forces exist.

**In equations:** ΣF = ma. The "Σ" means "sum of all" — add every force vectorially (with components) before multiplying by mass.
