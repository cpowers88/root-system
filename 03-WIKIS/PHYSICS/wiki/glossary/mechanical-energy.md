# Mechanical Energy

## Plain-English Definition

The total energy a moving object has because of its motion and its position. It includes both kinetic energy (from moving) and potential energy (from height or spring compression).

## Physics Meaning

E_mech = K + U = ½mv² + mgy (+ ½kx² if a spring is present)

## Units / Symbols

E_mech; SI unit joule (J).

## Example

A 2 kg ball at height 5 m moving at 3 m/s:
- K = ½(2)(3²) = 9 J
- U_g = (2)(9.80)(5) = 98 J
- E_mech = 107 J

If no friction is present, E_mech = 107 J at every point along the ball's path.

## Common Confusion

Mechanical energy is NOT the same as kinetic energy. It's the sum of kinetic AND potential. An object at rest at height h has mechanical energy (all potential) even though it isn't moving.

## Flashcard

**Q:** What is mechanical energy and its equation?
**A:** E_mech = K + U — the sum of kinetic and potential energy of a system.
