# Acceleration

## Plain-English Definition

How quickly an object's velocity is changing — whether it is speeding up, slowing down, or changing direction.

## Physics Meaning

A vector. In 1D:
- **Average acceleration:** a_avg = Δv/Δt
- **Instantaneous acceleration:** a = dv/dt = d²x/dt²

Sign: positive if velocity is increasing in the +x direction; negative if decreasing in +x or increasing in −x direction.

## Units / Symbols

a; SI unit: m/s².

## Example

A car going 25 m/s (+x) brakes to 10 m/s in 3 s. Acceleration = (10 − 25)/3 = −5 m/s². The acceleration is negative (opposing motion) even though the car is still moving forward.

## Common Confusion

1. "Decelerating" is informal. Physics says the acceleration has a sign opposite to the velocity direction.
2. Acceleration is NOT zero at the top of a thrown object's path — only velocity is zero there. Acceleration remains −9.80 m/s².
