# Period (Circular Motion)

## Plain-English Definition

The time it takes for an object to complete one full revolution around a circular path.

## Physics Meaning

T = 2πr / v. The circumference of the circle (2πr) divided by the constant speed gives the time per revolution.

## Units / Symbols If Applicable

Symbol: T. Unit: seconds (s). Related: frequency f = 1/T (revolutions per second, in Hz).

## Example

A satellite orbiting at altitude has a period of about 90 minutes (5400 s). The Moon has a period of about 27.3 days. A car wheel spinning at 600 rpm has a period of T = 60/600 = 0.10 s.

## Common Confusion

Confusing period T (time per revolution, in seconds) with frequency f (revolutions per second, in Hz). They are reciprocals: f = 1/T. Also: this T is unrelated to tension (also written T in force problems) — the symbol is reused. Check context.

## Flashcard

**Q:** What is the period of circular motion and how does it relate to speed and radius?
**A:** T = 2πr/v — time for one full revolution. Equivalently, v = 2πr/T.
