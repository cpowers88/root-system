# Projectile

## Plain-English Definition

Any object launched into the air that moves freely under gravity alone (no engine, no thrust, no significant air resistance).

## Physics Meaning

A projectile has zero net horizontal force (so horizontal motion is constant velocity) and a constant downward gravitational force (so vertical motion is free fall). The combination produces a parabolic path.

## Units / Symbols If Applicable

No special symbol. The projectile's position is tracked as (x, y), velocity as (vₓ, vᵧ).

## Example

A kicked football (in the idealized model), a cannonball, a horizontally thrown stone, water from a fountain nozzle.

## Common Confusion

Thinking the projectile has a "forward force" carrying it horizontally. Once launched, there is no horizontal force — it keeps moving horizontally by inertia (Newton's First Law). Gravity pulls it down, but nothing pushes it forward.

## Flashcard

**Q:** What forces act on a projectile after it is launched?
**A:** Only gravity (downward). No horizontal force. Horizontal motion is constant velocity by inertia.
