# Work

**Definition:** The energy transferred to or from an object by a force acting through a displacement. W = F d cos θ, where θ is the angle between the force and displacement vectors.

**Unit:** joule (J = N·m = kg·m²/s²)

**Sign:** Positive when force aids motion; negative when force opposes motion; zero when force is perpendicular to motion.

**What it is NOT:** Work is not effort, not power, not force, and not energy stored. It is specifically the *transfer* of energy via a force-displacement interaction.

**Flash fact:** Normal force and gravity always do zero work on an object moving horizontally on a flat surface — both are perpendicular to the displacement.
