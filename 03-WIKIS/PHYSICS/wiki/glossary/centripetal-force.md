# Centripetal Force

**The net force directed toward the center of a circular path, required to maintain circular motion.**

- Not a new type of force — it is the *role* played by real forces (tension, gravity, friction, normal force).
- Magnitude: ΣF_c = mv²/r
- Always directed inward (toward the center), never outward.
- Centripetal acceleration: a_c = v²/r, always inward.

Do not draw "centripetal force" as a separate arrow on a free body diagram. Draw the real forces; the net inward component is the centripetal force.

*First appears: Stage 6 (Ch 6). Carried into Stage 10 (Ch 10 — rotational motion).*
