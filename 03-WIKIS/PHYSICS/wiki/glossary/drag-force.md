# Drag Force (Resistive Force)

**A force that opposes motion through a fluid (air, water), increasing in magnitude as speed increases.**

Two models:
- **Linear drag:** R = bv — for slow-moving or very small objects. b is the drag coefficient (kg/s).
- **Quadratic drag:** R = ½DρAv² — for fast-moving everyday objects (people, vehicles, balls). D = shape-dependent drag coefficient (dimensionless), ρ = fluid density (kg/m³), A = cross-sectional area (m²).

Always opposes the direction of motion. In free fall, drag acts upward.
