# Polar Coordinates

## Plain-English Definition

A way to locate a point using a distance and a direction: how far away (r) and at what angle (θ).

## Physics Meaning

Written (r, θ). r is always positive — it's a straight-line distance from the origin. θ is measured counterclockwise from the +x axis. The same math a vector's magnitude and direction use.

## Units / Symbols If Applicable

r — length unit (m). θ — degrees or radians.

## Example

A point 5 m from the origin at 37° above the +x axis has polar coordinates (5 m, 37°).

## Common Confusion

Reporting r as negative, or forgetting to check the quadrant when computing θ = tan⁻¹(y/x) from Cartesian coordinates. See [[../common-errors/stage-3-vectors]].

## Flashcard

**Q:** What do r and θ represent in polar coordinates, and what sign can r have?
**A:** r = distance from the origin (always positive). θ = angle from the +x axis, counterclockwise. r is never negative — it's a distance.
