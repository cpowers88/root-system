# Unit/Stage 1 — Physics and Measurement

## Goal

Be fluent in the language physics is written in — units, dimensions, and significant figures — before any equation work begins.

## Mastery Checklist

- [ ] Define SI unit, dimension, order of magnitude, and significant figure from memory
- [ ] Identify which of the three fundamental quantities (L, M, T) a given physical quantity is built from
- [ ] Choose dimensional analysis to check any new equation before trusting it
- [ ] List variables and units for the density equation
- [ ] Convert a compound unit (like speed or density) between SI and US customary systems without flipping a conversion ratio
- [ ] Produce an order-of-magnitude estimate for an unfamiliar real-world quantity, stating assumptions
- [ ] Apply the correct significant-figure rule (mult/div vs. add/sub) without confusing the two
- [ ] Explain out loud why the particle model is a valid simplification, not a "wrong" assumption

## Do Not Move On Until

Chris can convert between SI/US units without a reference sheet, check any equation for dimensional consistency by hand, and apply sig-fig rules correctly to a multi-step calculation — all without prompting.
