## Stage 1 — Physics and Measurement (Ch 1)

**Goal:** Be fluent in the language physics is written in — units, dimensions, and significant figures — before any equation work begins.

**Syllabus alignment:** Ch 01, lecture M Aug 24.
**Prerequisite physics:** None — this is the entry point.
**Prerequisite math:** Scientific notation, basic algebra, powers of ten.

**Core concepts:** SI base units, the particle model, dimensional analysis, unit conversion, order-of-magnitude estimation, significant figures.

**Calculus connections:** None yet. (Calculus enters in Stage 2.)

**Problem types introduced:** Dimensional-consistency check; unit conversion; order-of-magnitude estimation; significant-figure arithmetic.

**Do not move on until:** Chris can convert between SI/US units without a reference sheet, check any equation for dimensional consistency by hand, and apply sig-fig rules to a multi-step calculation.

**Packet location:** `wiki/concepts/`, `wiki/equations/`, `wiki/problem-types/`, `wiki/glossary/`, `wiki/flashcards/`, `wiki/drills/` (see index for exact filenames).

## Stage 2 — Motion in One Dimension (Ch 2)

**Goal:** Describe straight-line motion precisely using position, velocity, and acceleration, and connect each to its calculus definition.

**Syllabus alignment:** Ch 02 begins W Aug 26. The syllabus also prints “F Aug 26,”
which is a day/date conflict; D2L must confirm the second meeting (likely F Aug 28).
**Prerequisite physics:** Stage 1 (units, dimensional analysis).
**Prerequisite math:** Derivatives of polynomials, basic integration, slope of a line/curve.

**Core concepts:** Position, displacement vs. distance, average vs. instantaneous velocity, acceleration, motion diagrams, constant-acceleration kinematics, free fall.

**Calculus connections:** v = dx/dt, a = dv/dt = d²x/dt²; kinematic equations derived by integrating constant acceleration.

**Problem types introduced:** Constant-velocity problems; constant-acceleration problems; free-fall problems; graphical motion analysis (x-t, v-t, a-t graphs).

**Packet status:** Generated; provisionally cleared, subject to a mastery re-check.

## Stage 10 — Rotation of a Rigid Object About a Fixed Axis (Ch 10)

**Goal:** Extend Newton's second law to rotating objects using torque, moment of inertia, and angular kinematics.

**Prerequisite physics:** Stages 1–9 (especially Ch 5 Newton's laws; Ch 7–8 energy).
**Prerequisite math:** Integration for moment of inertia formulas; chain rule for angular acceleration.

**Core concepts:** Angular position/velocity/acceleration, torque, moment of inertia, parallel-axis theorem, rotational kinetic energy, rolling motion, angular momentum of a rigid body.

**Calculus connections:** α = dω/dt; τ = Iα (rotational analogue of F = ma); rotational kinetic energy via integration.

**Problem types introduced:** Angular kinematics problems; torque and Newton's 2nd law for rotation; rotational energy problems; rolling-without-slipping problems.

**Packet status:** Generated; not yet studied or re-verified in the current pass.

## Stage 11 — Angular Momentum (Ch 11)

**Goal:** Apply conservation of angular momentum to systems where torque is zero, analogous to linear momentum conservation.

**Prerequisite physics:** Stage 10 (moment of inertia, torque).
**Core concepts:** Angular momentum L = Iω, torque as rate of change of L, conservation of angular momentum, precession, angular momentum in planetary motion.

**Calculus connections:** τ = dL/dt (rotational analogue of F = dp/dt).

**Problem types introduced:** Angular momentum conservation problems (spinning objects changing shape); gyroscope/precession problems.

**Packet status:** Generated; not yet studied or re-verified in the current pass.
