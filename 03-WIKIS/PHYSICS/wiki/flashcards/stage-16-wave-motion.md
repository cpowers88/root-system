# Flashcards — Stage 16: Wave Motion (Ch 16)

## Cards

### Card 1
**Q:** What is the general traveling wave function, and what do each of its parameters mean?
**A:** y(x,t) = A sin(kx − ωt + φ). A = amplitude (m); k = wave number = 2π/λ (rad/m); ω = angular frequency = 2πf (rad/s); φ = phase constant.

### Card 2
**Q:** Write the relationship between wave speed, frequency, and wavelength.
**A:** v = fλ. Also: v = ω/k.

### Card 3
**Q:** What is the wave speed on a stretched string?
**A:** v = √(T/μ), where T is tension in the string (N) and μ = m/L is the linear mass density (kg/m).

### Card 4
**Q:** What does the wave number k represent?
**A:** k = 2π/λ. It counts how many radians of phase per meter of distance. Large k → short wavelength → waves packed closely together.

### Card 5
**Q:** A wave has frequency 440 Hz and speed 340 m/s. What is its wavelength?
**A:** λ = v/f = 340/440 = 0.773 m.

### Card 6
**Q:** What is the power transmitted by a sinusoidal wave on a string?
**A:** P = ½μω²A²v. Power is proportional to the square of amplitude and to the square of frequency.

### Card 7
**Q:** State the Doppler effect formula for a moving source (at rest observer).
**A:** f_observed = f_source × (v ± v_observer)/(v ∓ v_source). Use + for motion toward, − for motion away (numerator: observer toward = +; denominator: source toward = −).

### Card 8
**Q:** A police siren emits at 1000 Hz. The car moves toward you at 30 m/s (v_sound = 340 m/s). What frequency do you hear?
**A:** f = 1000 × (340)/(340−30) = 1000 × (340/310) ≈ 1097 Hz. (Higher — source approaching.)

### Card 9
**Q:** What is sound intensity, and what are its units?
**A:** I = P/A — power per unit area of the wavefront. Units: W/m². Intensity level in dB: β = 10 log₁₀(I/I₀), where I₀ = 10⁻¹² W/m².

### Card 10
**Q:** A sound intensity doubles. By how many decibels does the level increase?
**A:** ΔdB = 10 log₁₀(2) ≈ 3 dB. Doubling intensity adds about 3 dB; tripling intensity adds about 4.8 dB.

### Card 11
**Q:** What is the linear wave equation?
**A:** ∂²y/∂x² = (1/v²) ∂²y/∂t². Any function of the form f(x − vt) or f(x + vt) is a solution (traveling wave in +x or −x direction).

### Card 12
**Q:** What are the two fundamental categories of waves, and how do they differ?
**A:** Transverse: displacement perpendicular to wave propagation (light, string waves). Longitudinal: displacement parallel to propagation (sound, compression waves in springs).
