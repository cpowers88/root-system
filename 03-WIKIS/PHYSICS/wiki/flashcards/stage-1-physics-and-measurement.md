# Flashcards — Stage 1: Physics and Measurement (Ch 1)

## Cards

### Card 1
**Q:** What are the three fundamental quantities of mechanics, and their SI units?
**A:** Length (meter), mass (kilogram), time (second).

### Card 2
**Q:** What is a "particle model" and when is it valid to use?
**A:** Treating an object as a point with mass but no size. Valid when the object's size and internal processes don't affect the motion being analyzed.

### Card 3
**Q:** What does dimensional analysis check, and what can't it check?
**A:** It checks whether both sides of an equation have matching L/M/T dimensions. It cannot determine dimensionless numerical constants (like ½).

### Card 4
**Q:** How do you convert 38.0 m/s to mi/h?
**A:** Multiply by (1 mi / 1609 m) and (3600 s / 1 h), canceling units: 38.0 m/s ≈ 85.0 mi/h.

### Card 5
**Q:** What does the symbol ~ mean?
**A:** "Is on the order of" — a power-of-ten estimate.

### Card 6
**Q:** Sig fig rule for multiplication/division — what governs the answer's precision?
**A:** The input value with the fewest significant figures.

### Card 7
**Q:** Sig fig rule for addition/subtraction — what governs the answer's precision?
**A:** The input value with the fewest decimal places.

### Card 8
**Q:** What is the equation for density, and what are its units?
**A:** ρ = m/V, units kg/m³.

### Card 9
**Q:** Why is mass not the same as weight?
**A:** Mass (kg) is an inherent amount of matter; weight is a force due to gravity (introduced in Ch. 5) and depends on location.

### Card 10
**Q:** What are the dimensions of speed and acceleration?
**A:** [speed] = L/T; [acceleration] = L/T².
