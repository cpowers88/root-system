# Flashcards — Stage 2: Motion in One Dimension (Ch 2)

## Cards

### Card 1
**Q:** What is displacement, and how does it differ from distance?
**A:** Δx = x_f − x₀. Displacement is the net change in position (vector; can be zero or negative). Distance is the total path length traveled (scalar; always positive).

### Card 2
**Q:** What is the formula for average velocity, and what units does it have?
**A:** v_avg = Δx / Δt. Units: m/s. Uses displacement (not distance).

### Card 3
**Q:** How is instantaneous velocity defined using calculus?
**A:** v = dx/dt — the derivative of position with respect to time; the slope of the x-t graph at one point.

### Card 4
**Q:** How is instantaneous acceleration defined using calculus?
**A:** a = dv/dt = d²x/dt² — the derivative of velocity; the slope of the v-t graph at one point.

### Card 5
**Q:** What does the slope of a v-t graph equal?
**A:** Acceleration (m/s²).

### Card 6
**Q:** What does the area under a v-t graph equal?
**A:** Displacement (m).

### Card 7
**Q:** List the five kinematic equations in words (which quantity each is missing).
**A:** 1: missing Δx (v = v₀ + at). 2: missing v_f. 3: missing t. 4: missing a. 5: missing v₀.

### Card 8
**Q:** What is the acceleration of a freely falling object near Earth's surface? What direction?
**A:** g = 9.80 m/s², directed downward. If up is positive, a = −9.80 m/s².

### Card 9
**Q:** At the highest point of a ball thrown upward, what is its velocity? What is its acceleration?
**A:** Velocity = 0 (momentarily at rest). Acceleration = −9.80 m/s² (gravity never turns off).

### Card 10
**Q:** A car moves in the +x direction and has a negative acceleration. Is it speeding up or slowing down?
**A:** Slowing down. Negative acceleration opposing positive velocity means the car is decelerating.

### Card 11
**Q:** What is the condition for using the kinematic equations?
**A:** Acceleration must be constant (or zero) throughout the time interval.

### Card 12
**Q:** How do you pick the correct kinematic equation to use?
**A:** List all five quantities (x₀, x, v₀, v, a, t). Mark knowns and unknowns. Pick the equation that has your unknown and all three knowns, but not the one unused quantity.

### Card 13
**Q:** What is the slope of an x-t graph equal to?
**A:** Velocity (m/s) at that point.

### Card 14
**Q:** An object is dropped (v₀ = 0) from height h. Write the equation for y(t).
**A:** y = h − ½gt² (taking down as positive: y = ½gt²; or up as positive: y = h − ½gt²).

### Card 15
**Q:** Two objects start at different positions and move at constant velocities. How do you find when they meet?
**A:** Write position equations x₁(t) and x₂(t) for each. Set x₁ = x₂ and solve for t.
