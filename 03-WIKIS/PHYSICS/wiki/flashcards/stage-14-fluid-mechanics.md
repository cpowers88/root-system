# Flashcards — Stage 14: Fluid Mechanics (Ch 14)

## Cards

### Card 1
**Q:** What is pressure, and what are its SI units?
**A:** P = F/A — force per unit area applied perpendicularly to a surface. Units: Pa = N/m². Also useful: 1 atm = 101,325 Pa ≈ 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa.

### Card 2
**Q:** How does pressure vary with depth in a fluid at rest?
**A:** P = P₀ + ρgh, where P₀ is pressure at the surface, ρ is fluid density, g ≈ 9.8 m/s², h is depth below the surface.

### Card 3
**Q:** State Pascal's Law.
**A:** A change in pressure applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted undiminished to every part of the fluid and the walls of the container. This is why hydraulic systems work.

### Card 4
**Q:** State Archimedes' Principle.
**A:** A submerged (or floating) object experiences an upward buoyant force equal to the weight of the fluid displaced: F_B = ρ_fluid · V_submerged · g.

### Card 5
**Q:** When does an object float vs. sink?
**A:** Float: average density of object < density of fluid (or F_B ≥ weight). Sink: average density of object > density of fluid.

### Card 6
**Q:** Write the continuity equation for an ideal fluid and state what it means.
**A:** A₁v₁ = A₂v₂. The volume flow rate (m³/s) is constant along a pipe. Narrower cross section → faster speed; wider → slower. (This is conservation of mass for incompressible flow.)

### Card 7
**Q:** Write Bernoulli's equation and state its physical meaning.
**A:** P + ½ρv² + ρgy = constant. The sum of pressure energy, kinetic energy per unit volume, and gravitational PE per unit volume is conserved along a streamline. Higher speed → lower pressure.

### Card 8
**Q:** Why does an airplane wing generate lift? (Bernoulli explanation)
**A:** Air moves faster over the curved top surface than under the flat bottom, so pressure is lower on top. The net upward pressure difference provides lift.

### Card 9
**Q:** A pipe narrows from radius 2 cm to radius 1 cm. By what factor does the flow speed increase?
**A:** A₁v₁ = A₂v₂; A ∝ r². So A₁/A₂ = (2/1)² = 4. Speed increases by factor 4.

### Card 10
**Q:** What is the buoyant force on an object that fully displaces 0.50 L of water (ρ_water = 1000 kg/m³)?
**A:** V = 0.50 L = 5.0×10⁻⁴ m³. F_B = ρgV = (1000)(9.8)(5.0×10⁻⁴) = 4.9 N.
