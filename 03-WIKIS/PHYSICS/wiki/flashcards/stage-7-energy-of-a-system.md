# Flashcards — Stage 7: Energy of a System (Ch 7)

## Cards

### Card 1
**Q:** What is the definition of work done by a constant force?
**A:** W = F d cos θ, where F is the force magnitude, d is the displacement magnitude, and θ is the angle between the force and displacement vectors. Unit: joule (J).

### Card 2
**Q:** When is the work done by a force equal to zero?
**A:** When the force is perpendicular to the displacement (θ = 90°, so cos 90° = 0), or when there is no displacement (d = 0). Example: a wall you push on but can't move — no displacement, no work.

### Card 3
**Q:** What is kinetic energy? Write the equation with units.
**A:** K = ½mv². The kinetic energy of a moving object. m in kg, v in m/s → K in joules (J = kg·m²/s²).

### Card 4
**Q:** State the work-energy theorem in one sentence and write the equation.
**A:** The net work done on an object equals the change in its kinetic energy. W_net = ΔK = K_f − K_i = ½mv_f² − ½mv_i².

### Card 5
**Q:** What is Hooke's Law? Include sign and units.
**A:** F_s = −kx. The spring force is proportional to displacement x from equilibrium and opposite in direction (hence the minus sign). k is the spring constant in N/m.

### Card 6
**Q:** What is the spring potential energy equation?
**A:** U_s = ½kx². x is the displacement from equilibrium in meters; k in N/m → U_s in joules.

### Card 7
**Q:** What is gravitational potential energy near Earth's surface?
**A:** U_g = mgy. m = mass (kg), g = 9.80 m/s², y = height above a chosen reference point (m). Unit: joules.

### Card 8
**Q:** What makes a force conservative?
**A:** Two equivalent conditions: (1) the work it does on an object moving between two points is independent of the path taken, or (2) the work it does over any closed loop is zero. Gravity and spring force are conservative. Friction is not.

### Card 9
**Q:** How do you find work by a varying force (when F changes with position)?
**A:** W = ∫(x_i → x_f) F_x dx. Geometrically, this is the area under the F-x curve between the two positions.

### Card 10
**Q:** How is force related to potential energy mathematically?
**A:** F_x = −dU/dx. Force is the negative derivative of potential energy with respect to position. Force points in the direction of decreasing potential energy.

### Card 11
**Q:** A 2.0 kg block travels at 3.0 m/s. What is its kinetic energy?
**A:** K = ½(2.0)(3.0²) = ½(2.0)(9.0) = 9.0 J.

### Card 12
**Q:** A spring with k = 200 N/m is compressed 0.10 m. What is Us?
**A:** Us = ½(200)(0.10²) = ½(200)(0.01) = 1.0 J.

### Card 13
**Q:** Does the normal force do work on an object sliding along a horizontal surface?
**A:** No. The normal force is perpendicular to the displacement (θ = 90°), so W = F d cos 90° = 0.

### Card 14
**Q:** What does a negative value of work mean?
**A:** The force has a component opposing the motion — energy is transferred OUT of the system (for that force). Example: friction does negative work, removing kinetic energy.

### Card 15
**Q:** If W_net = +15 J on an object initially at rest, what is its final kinetic energy?
**A:** K_f = K_i + W_net = 0 + 15 = 15 J.
