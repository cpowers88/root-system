# Flashcards — Stage 8: Conservation of Energy (Ch 8)

## Cards

### Card 1
**Q:** What is mechanical energy?
**A:** E_mech = K + U — the sum of kinetic energy and all potential energies in the system.

### Card 2
**Q:** Write the conservation of mechanical energy equation for a frictionless system.
**A:** Ki + Ui = Kf + Uf

### Card 3
**Q:** Write the energy equation when kinetic friction f_k acts over distance d.
**A:** Ki + Ui − f_k d = Kf + Uf

### Card 4
**Q:** Why does the reference height (y = 0) choice not matter, as long as you are consistent?
**A:** Only height differences (Δy) appear in the final equation — the zero cancels. But you must use the same reference on both sides.

### Card 5
**Q:** An object slides down a frictionless ramp from height h, starting from rest. What is its speed at the bottom?
**A:** v = √(2gh). From mgh = ½mv² → v² = 2gh.

### Card 6
**Q:** What happens to the "lost" mechanical energy when friction is present?
**A:** It becomes thermal energy (heat) in the object and the surface. Total energy is conserved; mechanical energy is not.

### Card 7
**Q:** On a ramp of length d at angle θ, what is the vertical height h gained?
**A:** h = d sin θ

### Card 8
**Q:** On a ramp at angle θ, what is the normal force?
**A:** N = mg cos θ

### Card 9
**Q:** What is the equation for average power?
**A:** P_avg = W / Δt

### Card 10
**Q:** What is the equation for power when a constant force F acts on an object moving at speed v (F ∥ v)?
**A:** P = Fv

### Card 11
**Q:** Convert 1 horsepower to watts.
**A:** 1 hp = 746 W ≈ 750 W

### Card 12
**Q:** A 5 kg ball is dropped from 4 m. What is its speed just before impact? (g = 9.80 m/s²)
**A:** v = √(2 × 9.80 × 4) = √78.4 ≈ 8.85 m/s. (Mass cancels — doesn't matter.)

### Card 13
**Q:** What is the key signal that you should use energy conservation instead of Newton's 2nd law?
**A:** The problem asks for speed or height at a specific point without asking about forces or time. Energy conservation relates two states; Newton's law relates force to acceleration at an instant.
