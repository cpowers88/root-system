# Flashcards — Stage 18: Special Relativity (Ch 38)

## Cards

### Card 1
**Q:** State Einstein's two postulates of special relativity.
**A:** (1) The laws of physics are the same in all inertial reference frames. (2) The speed of light in vacuum is c ≈ 3×10⁸ m/s in all inertial frames, regardless of the motion of the source or observer.

### Card 2
**Q:** What is the Lorentz factor γ, and what is its minimum value?
**A:** γ = 1/√(1 − v²/c²). Always ≥ 1. At v = 0, γ = 1 (no relativistic effects). As v → c, γ → ∞.

### Card 3
**Q:** What is time dilation? Which observer measures proper time?
**A:** Moving clocks run slow: Δt = γΔt₀, where Δt₀ is the proper time (measured by the observer traveling with the clock), and Δt is the time measured by the stationary observer (always larger, γ ≥ 1).

### Card 4
**Q:** What is length contraction? Which observer measures proper length?
**A:** Moving objects appear shorter along the direction of motion: L = L₀/γ, where L₀ is the proper length (measured in the rest frame of the object), and L is the contracted length measured by the moving observer (always shorter).

### Card 5
**Q:** What is rest energy and what is its equation?
**A:** E₀ = mc². The energy an object has purely by virtue of having mass, even when at rest. m is the rest mass, c = 3×10⁸ m/s. A tiny mass has enormous rest energy.

### Card 6
**Q:** What is the total relativistic energy of a moving object?
**A:** E = γmc². At rest (γ = 1): E = mc². Moving: E > mc². Kinetic energy is K = (γ − 1)mc².

### Card 7
**Q:** What is relativistic momentum?
**A:** p⃗ = γmv⃗. At low speeds (v << c, γ ≈ 1), this reduces to the classical p = mv. At high speeds, p grows without bound as v → c.

### Card 8
**Q:** What is the energy-momentum relation for a massless particle (like a photon)?
**A:** E² = (pc)² + (mc²)². For massless particles (m = 0): E = pc, so p = E/c = hf/c (using quantum physics). This applies to photons.

### Card 9
**Q:** Why can no object with mass reach the speed of light?
**A:** As v → c, γ → ∞, so the momentum p = γmv → ∞ and energy E = γmc² → ∞. It would take infinite energy to accelerate a massive object to c.

### Card 10
**Q:** What did the Michelson-Morley experiment show?
**A:** The speed of light is the same regardless of Earth's motion through space — there is no "luminiferous aether" and no preferred reference frame for light. This contradicted classical (Galilean) expectations and paved the way for Einstein's postulates.
