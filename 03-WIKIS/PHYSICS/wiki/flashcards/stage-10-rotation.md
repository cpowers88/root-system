# Flashcards — Stage 10: Rotation of a Rigid Object (Ch 10)

## Cards

### Card 1
**Q:** What are the rotational analogues of displacement, velocity, and acceleration?
**A:** Angular displacement θ (rad), angular velocity ω = dθ/dt (rad/s), angular acceleration α = dω/dt (rad/s²).

### Card 2
**Q:** Write the four angular kinematic equations for constant α (analogues of the Big Four).
**A:** ω = ω₀ + αt; θ = θ₀ + ω₀t + ½αt²; ω² = ω₀² + 2αΔθ; θ = θ₀ + ½(ω+ω₀)t.

### Card 3
**Q:** What is the relationship between tangential speed v and angular velocity ω for a point on a rotating object at radius r?
**A:** v = rω. (Also: tangential acceleration a_t = rα; centripetal acceleration a_c = rω² = v²/r.)

### Card 4
**Q:** What is torque, and what equation relates torque to angular acceleration?
**A:** Torque τ = rF sin φ (= r × F, the moment arm times the force). Newton's 2nd law for rotation: τ_net = Iα.

### Card 5
**Q:** What is moment of inertia I, and why does it depend on the mass distribution?
**A:** I = Σmᵢrᵢ² (or ∫r² dm for continuous objects). Mass farther from the axis contributes more to I — it's harder to start or stop rotating.

### Card 6
**Q:** What is the parallel-axis theorem and when is it used?
**A:** I = I_cm + Md², where I_cm is the moment of inertia about the center of mass, M is total mass, and d is the distance between the axes. Used when the rotation axis is parallel to — but not through — the center of mass.

### Card 7
**Q:** Write the expression for rotational kinetic energy.
**A:** KR = ½Iω². It has the same form as translational KE = ½mv², with I replacing m and ω replacing v.

### Card 8
**Q:** What is the condition for rolling without slipping?
**A:** v_cm = Rω and a_cm = Rα. The contact point has zero instantaneous velocity.

### Card 9
**Q:** What is the total kinetic energy of a rolling object?
**A:** K_total = ½mv² + ½Iω² = translational KE + rotational KE. For a uniform solid sphere rolling without slipping: K = ½mv² + ½(2/5)mv²(ω = v/R) = 7/10 mv².

### Card 10
**Q:** What is work done by a torque through an angular displacement Δθ?
**A:** W = τ · Δθ (if τ is constant). In general: W = ∫τ dθ. Units: J (same as translational work).

### Card 11
**Q:** List the linear → rotational analogues: m, v, p, F, x, KE, W.
**A:** m → I; v → ω; p → L (= Iω); F → τ; x → θ; KE → ½Iω²; W = Fx → W = τθ.

### Card 12
**Q:** Two objects with the same mass are released from rest at the top of a ramp. One is a solid cylinder, one is a hollow ring. Which reaches the bottom first?
**A:** The solid cylinder. It has less rotational inertia (I = ½MR²) than the ring (I = MR²), so less energy goes to rotation — more goes to translation — and it rolls faster.
