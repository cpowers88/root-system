# Flashcards — Stage 12: Static Equilibrium and Elasticity (Ch 12)

## Cards

### Card 1
**Q:** What are the two conditions for static equilibrium?
**A:** (1) ΣF⃗ = 0 (translational: net force is zero). (2) Στ = 0 (rotational: net torque is zero about any chosen axis).

### Card 2
**Q:** How do you choose the best pivot point for a torque problem?
**A:** Choose the pivot at the location of an unknown force. That force has zero moment arm about that point, so it produces zero torque and drops out of the torque equation — leaving fewer unknowns.

### Card 3
**Q:** What is the formula for torque, and what is the unit?
**A:** τ = r F sin φ, where r is the distance from the pivot to the point where the force acts, F is the force magnitude, and φ is the angle between r and F. Unit: N·m.

### Card 4
**Q:** What sign is a counterclockwise (CCW) torque?
**A:** Positive (by convention). Clockwise torques are negative. Always define the sign convention at the start.

### Card 5
**Q:** What is center of gravity, and how does it differ from center of mass?
**A:** Center of gravity is the point where gravity's torque effectively acts. For uniform gravitational fields (normal Earth conditions), center of gravity equals center of mass. They differ only in nonuniform gravity fields.

### Card 6
**Q:** What does Young's modulus measure, and what is its equation?
**A:** Tensile (stretching or compressing) stiffness of a material. Y = (F/A) / (ΔL/L₀) = stress/strain. Unit: Pa (N/m²). A stiffer material has a larger Y.

### Card 7
**Q:** What is stress and what is strain?
**A:** Stress = F/A (force per unit area, units Pa). Strain = ΔL/L₀ (fractional change in length, dimensionless). Elastic modulus = stress/strain.

### Card 8
**Q:** A beam is supported at both ends and has a weight hung from the center. Which condition solves for the support forces?
**A:** Use ΣFy = 0 and Στ = 0. Two equations, two unknown support forces. Choose any pivot point; eliminating one unknown from the torque equation makes the algebra simpler.

### Card 9
**Q:** Name the three elastic moduli and what deformation each describes.
**A:** Young's modulus Y: tensile/compressive (length change along force direction). Shear modulus S: shear (parallel-force sliding deformation). Bulk modulus B: uniform compression (volume change).

### Card 10
**Q:** Why can you choose any pivot point when applying Στ = 0?
**A:** Because for an object in equilibrium (ΣF⃗ = 0), the net torque is zero about ANY point — not just the center of mass. This is a consequence of both equilibrium conditions being satisfied simultaneously.
