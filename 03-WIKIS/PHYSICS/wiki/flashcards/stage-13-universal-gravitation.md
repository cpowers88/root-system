# Flashcards — Stage 13: Universal Gravitation (Ch 13)

## Cards

### Card 1
**Q:** Write Newton's law of universal gravitation with all variables and units.
**A:** F_g = G m₁m₂ / r². G = 6.674×10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg². m₁, m₂ in kg. r = center-to-center distance in m. F_g in N.

### Card 2
**Q:** If the distance between two objects doubles, what happens to the gravitational force?
**A:** F ∝ 1/r². Doubling r → force becomes 1/4 of its original value. (Inverse square law.)

### Card 3
**Q:** What is the gravitational field g⃗ at a distance r from a mass M?
**A:** g = GM/r² (magnitude), directed toward M. At Earth's surface: g ≈ 9.8 m/s². This is the general formula — 9.8 is just the value at r = R_Earth.

### Card 4
**Q:** State Kepler's Third Law.
**A:** T² ∝ r³, specifically T² = (4π²/GM) r³. The square of the orbital period is proportional to the cube of the orbital radius. Holds for any satellite orbiting the same central mass M.

### Card 5
**Q:** What is the orbital speed of a satellite in a circular orbit at radius r?
**A:** v_c = √(GM/r). This is found by setting the gravitational force equal to the centripetal force: GMm/r² = mv²/r.

### Card 6
**Q:** What is escape speed, and why doesn't it depend on the object's mass?
**A:** v_e = √(2GM/r). It's the minimum launch speed to escape a gravitational field completely (reach r = ∞ with zero KE). The object's mass cancels when setting KE = |U_grav|.

### Card 7
**Q:** What is gravitational potential energy in the general form (not U = mgh)?
**A:** U = −G m₁m₂ / r. Negative because the bound system has less energy than if the objects were at infinite separation (U = 0 at r = ∞).

### Card 8
**Q:** When is U = mgh valid and when must you use U = −GMm/r?
**A:** U = mgh is valid near Earth's surface where g is approximately constant. Use U = −GMm/r when height is comparable to Earth's radius, or for satellite/orbital problems.

### Card 9
**Q:** What is the total mechanical energy of a satellite in a circular orbit?
**A:** E_total = KE + U = ½mv² + (−GMm/r) = −GMm/(2r). Negative means the satellite is gravitationally bound.

### Card 10
**Q:** What does it mean for a satellite's total energy to be negative?
**A:** The satellite is in a bound orbit — it cannot escape without additional energy input. E_total = 0 would mean it has just enough energy to escape (parabolic trajectory). E_total > 0 means it's on a hyperbolic escape trajectory.
