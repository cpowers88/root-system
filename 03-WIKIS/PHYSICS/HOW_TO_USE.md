### This vault answers: *what physics situation is this, what model applies, and what should I practice next?*

## Start Here

## Standard Work Loop
```text
situation -> model -> equation/units -> example -> drill -> mastery
```
The loop: concept page → worked example (cover the solution, attempt first) → drill → common-errors check.

## What Counts as Proof
A topic is learned when Chris can classify the problem type from the situation alone and get units to check out — not when the page has been read. Full mastery standard (define terms, choose the model, list units, draw the diagram, solve representative problems, explain out loud): `CLAUDE.md § Mastery Standard`.

## Outputs and Where They Go
Study aids only (drills, flashcards, formula cards, diagrams). Graded coursework is never produced here — same academic-integrity boundary as `03-WIKIS\PYTHON`. Rare physics-for-business bridges route through the castle, not new pages here.

## Boundaries
No jumping to stage 10 rotational dynamics because it looks cool — `parked-advanced/` exists for a reason. One stage's frontier at a time; off-subject tangents go to `parking-lot.md`.

## How the Hub Learns From Use
After a session: log drills, add errors to `wiki/common-errors/`, update `current-position.md` at stage boundaries, append `wiki/log.md`. Known cleanup debt: stage tags are inconsistent (`stage-5` vs `stage-05`) — normalize to unpadded (`stages/stage-5`) as pages get touched; until then, tag filters must check both forms.

## Close
