## Purpose

This page exists so "read more of the raw folder" never substitutes for a real decision. Every source below earns deeper processing only by answering a named question that a live page, offer, or client decision actually needs — not because it's sitting in the folder unread. 69 sources are classified here; none are re-summarized for their own sake, and none of their content is duplicated into this page.

## How to Use

Look a source up here before opening it. Check its `Processing` value (read-fully / read-selectively / lookup-only / park) and its `Reopen trigger` — if the current question doesn't match the trigger, the source almost certainly isn't the bottleneck. ## Curated Reading Sequence

This is a starting sequence, not a permanent curriculum. Each entry is a filename and the one question it should answer — nothing more.

**Business foundation**
- theE-MythRevisited — Is a specific role in the business over-dependent on Chris personally, and what would systemizing it look like?
- GoodStraatedgyBadStrategy — What is the actual strategy kernel (diagnosis + guiding policy + coherent action) behind the current vehicle choice?
- Entrepreneurship — What foundational venture-formation concept is missing from venture-fundamentals.md?
- TheGreatCEOWithin — What operating cadence or meeting rhythm should Chris adopt at the current headcount of one?
- theChecklisManifesto — What checklist would catch the most likely failure mode in the next client deliverable?

**Operation/implementation**
- thePhoenixProject — Where is the single active constraint (Theory-of-Constraints bottleneck) in the current delivery workflow?
- profitFirst — Does the current cash-allocation method protect profit before expenses are paid?
- MeasureWhatMatters — What OKR or scorecard structure should replace ad hoc progress tracking?
- StrategicModelingandBusinessDynamics — Is there a feedback loop in the business model not yet captured in theory-of-constraints.md?
- NewCodeofEstimating — What estimating method would a construction-vertical audit need to validate first?
- Estimatingforesidentailconstructino — What residential-construction-specific estimating workflow would an audit target first?

**Customer/market/decision**
- theMomTest — What question sequence would surface a real (not imagined) customer problem in the next discovery call?
- PredictionMachines — Which part of a client's workflow is a prediction problem AI can cheapen, versus a judgment problem it can't?
- MKTG13-Principlesofmarketing — Is there a fundamentals gap in the current sales-system this closes (only if not already covered)?
- StorytellingwithData — What chart or dashboard layout would make a client's audit findings undeniable?
- BTOS + Census datasets — What is the current, sourced AI-adoption rate by sector/firm-size to cite in a proposal or market-map update?

**Technology/AI implementation**
- AllInOnAI — What enterprise AI-operating-model pattern from a real case study could Chris adapt for an SMB client?
- state-of-ai-2026 — What does this report claim about 2026 AI adoption, and is the publisher authoritative enough to cite?
- The AI Dossier — What AI-landscape claim here is worth citing, and who actually published it?
- AI and ML Red — Is there anything beyond vendor marketing language worth extracting?
- Data in Construction — What Procore-defined construction-data category maps to a specific audit finding?
- Procore Tool Training — Which Procore tool would a construction-vertical client audit need Chris to already understand?
- api-overview — What EDGAR API capability would a finance-data integration actually need?
