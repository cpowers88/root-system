# Workflow Automation Pathway

> The workhorse service: removing repetitive manual work from SMB operations with integration and automation.

## Purpose
Define the workflow automation service line — what it covers, how it's scoped, built, and priced — since this pathway generates the most projects and trains every core delivery skill.

## Key Idea
SMBs run on **human middleware**: people copying data between systems, re-typing form submissions, forwarding emails, updating spreadsheets, and chasing statuses. Each instance is small; the annual total is enormous. This pathway replaces human middleware with reliable automated pipelines — integration platforms (Make, n8n, Zapier), APIs, and AI steps where judgment-like processing is needed (classification, extraction, drafting).

## Why It Matters
- **Broadest demand:** every SMB has at least three automatable workflows; no vertical knowledge required to start.
- **Clearest ROI story:** "This task took your staff 11 hours a week; now it takes zero" is the easiest sentence in the business.
- **Best training ground:** builds the integration, API, and reliability skills every other pathway depends on. See [[skill-roadmap|Skill Roadmap]].
- **Natural retainer feed:** automations need monitoring, maintenance, and extension forever. See [[retainer-model|Retainer Model]].

## What Gets Sold (Typical Builds)
- **Lead intake → CRM:** form/call/email leads auto-created, enriched, assigned, and acknowledged
- **Quote/estimate assembly:** intake data → templated quote → approval → delivery → follow-up sequence
- **Invoice & billing flows:** job completion → invoice generation → send → payment reminders → bookkeeping sync
- **Scheduling & dispatch:** booking requests → calendar/dispatch board → confirmations → reminders
- **Onboarding pipelines:** new client/employee → document collection → account setup → task checklists
- **Notification & escalation:** stalled deals, overdue jobs, negative reviews, low stock → right person alerted
- **AI-augmented steps inside any of the above:** email/lead classification, data extraction from messages, drafted replies for human approval

## Fulfillment Workflow
1. Scope from audit findings: pick workflows with clear triggers, digital inputs, and measurable volume
2. Document current state (from the [[smb-ai-audit-method|audit]]) and define target state with the client
3. Build in a sandbox against real sample data
4. **Error handling first-class:** every automation gets failure alerts, retry logic, and a logged fallback to a human. Silent failure is how you lose clients.
5. Parallel-run with the manual process for 1–2 weeks
6. Cutover, train staff (short screen recordings), document in the client's system inventory
7. Handoff meeting → measure the before/after → [[case-study-template|case study]] → retainer offer

Full delivery standards: [[fulfillment-system|Fulfillment System]].

## Pricing
- Quick-win single workflow: **$2,000–$5,000**
- Multi-workflow package (3–5 processes): **$8,000–$20,000**
- Maintenance: covered under retainer tiers, or 15–20% of build cost annually
- Anchor price to quantified annual savings — target the build costing 25–50% of year-one savings. Details: [[pricing-models|Pricing Models]].

## Practical Actions
- Master one integration platform deeply (see [[tool-stack|Tool Stack]]) rather than three shallowly.
- Build a personal library of the 7 build patterns above as reusable templates; every delivery should add to the library.
- Automate 3 of your own workflows first (lead tracking, invoicing, reporting) — practice plus proof.

## Beginner Version
Deliver single-workflow quick wins ($2K–$5K) on a no-code platform. Choose workflows where failure is annoying, not catastrophic (skip payroll and anything compliance-critical). One client, one workflow, done excellently, measured honestly.

## Intermediate Version
Multi-workflow packages ($8K–$20K) sold from audit findings, each delivered with the full [[quality-control-and-risk-gates|gate architecture]] and a [[human-role-redesign|role card]] for whoever now supervises the flow. A personal pattern library covers the 7 standard builds; delivery hours per pattern drop every repetition and get logged in the [[financial-model|Financial Model]].

## Advanced Version
Multi-system architectures with custom API code where platforms fall short, self-hosted n8n for clients with data-control needs, monitoring dashboards across all client automations, and a template library that lets a mid-level hire deliver 80% of builds. Standard patterns per vertical.

## Revenue Connection
Highest project volume of any pathway and the main generator of retainer conversions. A steady state of 2–3 automation projects/month plus their accumulated retainers is on its own a $250K–$500K/yr practice — the backbone of the [[one-year-plan|One-Year Plan]].

## Human-Agent Management Connection
This pathway replaces **human middleware** with pipelines — which means every build changes someone's job. The staffer who did the copying becomes the [[agent-manager-job-design|workflow operator]] who reviews exceptions and flags rule updates; the AI-augmented steps (classification, extraction, drafting) always land behind [[quality-control-and-risk-gates|review gates]]. Sell the capacity gain, deliver the role redesign — that pairing is what makes these clients stay.

## Risks / Failure Modes
- **Silent failure** — the pathway's cardinal risk; error handling and alerting are first-class or the build doesn't ship.
- **Automating a broken process** — pipeline-izing chaos just produces faster chaos; messy workflows get redesigned and documented first (Rung 2's SOP buildout in the [[service-offer-ladder|Service Offer Ladder]]).
- **Compliance-critical scope too early** — payroll, regulated filings, and anything with legal blast radius wait until your gate discipline is proven.
