# Project Handoff Checklist

Run at the close of every project — [[fulfillment-system|Fulfillment System]], stage 6. The handoff meeting is the highest-leverage 60 minutes in the client lifecycle: satisfaction peaks here, and this checklist converts that peak into a retainer, a case study, and referrals. **A project isn't done when the build works; it's done when this list is empty.**

## Before the Handoff Meeting
- [ ] All SOW acceptance tests passed — **by the client's hands**, not just yours
- [ ] Parallel run completed (if applicable); agreed accuracy/reliability demonstrated and noted
- [ ] Error alerts wired into your central monitoring channel and test-fired once
- [ ] "After" metrics measured against the SOW §3 baselines
- [ ] System documentation written: what it does, how systems connect, **what to do when X breaks** (the section clients actually use)
- [ ] Training videos recorded (≤5 min each, one per workflow)
- [ ] Client system inventory updated (their doc *and* your internal record)
- [ ] All accounts confirmed in client's name; credential access documented in the password manager
- [ ] Final invoice prepared
- [ ] Retainer one-pager printed/attached ([[retainer-model|Retainer Model]] tiers)
- [ ] Case-study ask prepared ([[case-study-template|Case Study Template]])

## The Handoff Meeting (60 min agenda)
1. **The before/after (10 min):** SOW metrics table, baseline vs. now. Lead with the number: *"Quotes took 2 days; they now take 2 hours. That's worth about $[X]/yr at your volume."*
2. **Live walkthrough (15 min):** client's own person drives the workflow while you narrate
3. **Documentation & training tour (10 min):** where everything lives, who to call for what
4. **The retainer conversation (10 min) — verbatim opener:**
   > Everything we built needs someone watching it — software updates break integrations, your volume will grow, and there's a Phase 2 list from the audit we haven't touched. Who's responsible for that going forward: your team, or ours? Here's what that looks like either way. *(hand over the tier one-pager)*
5. **The case study ask (5 min):**
   > These results are exactly what other [vertical] owners ask me to prove. Can I write this up — anonymized or named, your call — and grab two sentences from you on what changed?
6. **The referral ask (5 min):** the script from [[outreach-scripts|outreach scripts §1]]
7. **Schedule the 30-day check-in before leaving the room**

## Within 1 Week After
- [ ] Handoff recap email: metrics table, doc links, support terms, retainer decision status
- [ ] Case study drafted while details are fresh
- [ ] Delivery playbook updated: what to repeat, what to never do again, actual hours vs. estimate → [[financial-model|Financial Model]] tracking
- [ ] Reusable components harvested into the template library ([[tool-stack|Tool Stack]])
- [ ] CRM updated: client stage → retainer/dormant, referrals logged as new leads

## At the 30-Day Check-In
- [ ] Adoption check: is everyone actually using it? (If not, fix now — [[risks-and-failure-modes|Risks & Failure Modes]] #8)
- [ ] Metrics re-measured — numbers usually improve; update the case study
- [ ] Retainer offer revisited if declined at handoff (a month of "who fixes this?" reality is persuasive)
- [ ] Phase 2 conversation: "Ready to look at the next item from the audit roadmap?"
