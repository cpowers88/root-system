# Audit Intake Questionnaire

Send on audit signing; require completion before Day 1. Purpose: arrive at interviews already knowing the landscape, so interview time goes to depth, not basics. Keep it under 20 minutes to complete — a form tool ([[tool-stack|Tool Stack]]) feeding your CRM is ideal.

Method reference: [[smb-ai-audit-method|SMB AI Audit Method, Step 1]].

---

## Business Basics
1. Company name / your name and role:
2. What does the business do, in one or two sentences?
3. Approximate annual revenue (range is fine): ☐ <$1M ☐ $1–5M ☐ $5–15M ☐ $15–50M
4. Team size (full-time / part-time / contractors):
5. Who besides you can make decisions about operations and software?

## How Work Flows
6. Walk me through what happens from "a customer first contacts you" to "you get paid" — just the big steps, in order:
7. Which step in that chain is slowest or most annoying?
8. What work regularly piles up or gets done late?
9. What do customers complain about most (even mildly)?

## Systems & Tools
10. List the software the business runs on (CRM, accounting, scheduling, industry tools, spreadsheets — all of it):
11. Which of these tools talk to each other automatically, if any?
12. What still lives on paper, in email threads, or in someone's head?
13. Any software you bought that never got fully adopted? What happened?

## People & Time
14. Who spends the most time on repetitive computer work, and roughly what are they doing?
15. If [that person] were out for two weeks, what would break?
16. Rough volumes per month: leads ___ / quotes-estimates ___ / invoices ___ / jobs-orders ___ (best guesses fine)

## Pain & Priorities
17. If you could wave a wand and make one operational problem disappear, which one?
18. What's it costing you? (their guess, in $ or hours — we'll verify)
19. Anything you've already tried to fix this? What happened?
20. Anything explicitly OFF the table for this audit? (departments, systems, sacred cows)

## Logistics
21. Best 2–4 people for me to interview (name, role, 30–45 min each):
22. Can I observe one process being performed live (in person or screen share)? Which one is most representative?
23. Preferred meeting times and channel:
