# Weekly Scorecard

Every Friday, 20 minutes. This is the flywheel's instrument panel — the [[sales-system|Sales System]] minimums, the [[financial-model|Financial Model]] funnel, and the [[risks-and-failure-modes|failure-mode]] early warnings on one page. Copy into a running doc or spreadsheet; one column per week so trends are visible at a glance.
