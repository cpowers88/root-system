# First 30 Days

> Foundation month: core skills, the audit offer built, internal systems live, and outreach started. Nothing fancy — everything real.

## Timeline Gate — Read This First
"First 30 days" means the first 30 days of **authorized active client acquisition**,
not the 30 days after reading this page. `CURRENT_STRATEGY.md` owns the first-client
milestone; CASTLE/NOW opens execution. Before then, pull forward only bounded method
practice or real workflow observation that does not require outreach, filing,
purchasing, or a new commitment.

## Purpose
Give the first month of active acquisition an exact agenda, so momentum comes from a checklist instead of motivation, and so day 30 ends with an offer in the market rather than a pile of research.

## Key Idea
The first 30 days have exactly four outputs, and everything else is deferred:
1. **Working fluency** in one automation platform + LLM basics
2. **The audit offer**, fully built (templates, price, one-pager)
3. **Your own business systems** running on the [[tool-stack|tool stack]] — practice and proof in one
4. **Outreach started** — first conversations booked before you feel ready

You are not trying to make money in month 1 (if you do, bonus). You are trying to make month 2–3 revenue *inevitable*. See [[first-90-days|First 90 Days]] for where this leads.

## Why It Matters
The two standard month-1 failure modes are opposites: endless preparation (courses, tools, branding, no outreach) and premature selling (pitching projects you can't yet scope or deliver). A fixed agenda with both build *and* outreach deadlines prevents both. See [[what-not-to-do|What NOT To Do]].

## Week-by-Week

### Week 1 — Stack and skills sprint
- Set up the beginner [[tool-stack|tool stack]]: Make, HubSpot free, Airtable, password manager, bookkeeping
- Build 3 practice automations for your own business: contact form → CRM → notification; invoice generation; a simple daily report
- LLM basics: extraction and classification exercises against sample documents (Claude API)
- Read this wiki's [[north-star-alignment|North Star]], [[market-map|Market Map]], and [[most-profitable-pathways|Most Profitable Pathways]]; pick your 1–2 starting verticals

### Week 2 — Build the audit product
- Copy and customize the three ready-made audit templates: [[audit-intake-questionnaire|intake questionnaire]], [[audit-interview-guide|interview guide]], [[audit-report-template|report skeleton]] ([[smb-ai-audit-method|SMB AI Audit Method]] explains the method behind them)
- Practice the full method on a free guinea pig — your own operation, a friend's business, or a family business. Produce a real report; this is your rehearsal and first portfolio piece
- Map 2 more example businesses on paper (from interviews or firsthand knowledge — 3 total mapped this month): flow map + [[human-agent-operating-model|six-bucket task inventory]] for each. Pattern recognition across three businesses is what makes audit #1 feel familiar instead of terrifying
- Set audit price ($1,500–$2,500 to start — [[pricing-models|Pricing Models]]) and write the one-page audit description + calendar link
- Draft your positioning sentence ([[sales-system|Sales System]])

### Week 3 — Open the pipeline
- List 50 warm contacts and 5 potential referral partners; begin 5 contacts/day
- Write and rehearse the audit sales conversation **out loud**
- Join/attend at least one local or vertical business community event
- Continue building: one more practice automation in your chosen vertical's typical workflow (e.g., a quote-assembly flow)

### Week 4 — Conversations and iteration
- Target: **5+ discovery conversations held, 2+ audit proposals delivered** (sold is a bonus; proposed is the requirement)
- Refine the pitch after every conversation — keep a "what they said / what worked" log
- Set up your prospect pipeline in your own CRM and your weekly sales-minimum schedule
- Write your month-1 delivery playbook notes ([[fulfillment-system|Fulfillment System]] habit starts now)

## Day-30 Scorecard
| Output | Bar |
|---|---|
| Automation platform fluency | 4+ working automations built |
| Audit product | Templates done + 1 full practice audit delivered |
| Own systems | CRM, bookkeeping, password mgmt, monitoring live |
| Pipeline | 50-contact list, 5+ conversations, 2+ audit proposals out |

Hit 3 of 4 and you're on track. Miss the pipeline row and fix *that* first — it's the one that pays.

## Practical Actions
The week-by-week list *is* the practical actions. Schedule it into a calendar today; unscheduled plans are wishes.

## Beginner Version
This entire page is the beginner version. If working around a day job: same outputs, 5 weeks instead of 4, with outreach concentrated on two fixed evenings + Saturday.

## Intermediate Version
If you arrive with existing automation/engineering skills (Chris does), compress week 1 to days and spend the surplus on the two things engineering doesn't teach: the interview craft of [[smb-ai-audit-method|process discovery]] and the out-loud sales rehearsal. The build outputs are the easy 4 of the scorecard; the pipeline row is still the one that decides the month.

## Advanced Version
Not applicable — but this page gets reused: it's the template for **onboarding hire #1** (their first 30 days mirror weeks 1–2) and for **entering a new vertical** (weeks 2–4 rerun against the new market). See [[three-year-plan|Three-Year Plan]].

## Revenue Connection
Month 1 revenue target: $0–$2,500 (one audit sold is a strong result). The real return is that every month-2+ dollar traces to a month-1 output: the audit product, the practiced pitch, and the opened pipeline.

## Human-Agent Management Connection
Run your own month-1 systems as the [[progressive-operating-thesis|operating loop]] from day one: AI drafts your outreach and reports, you review and improve the prompts, and you keep a personal exception log. By day 30 you'll have lived every role in [[agent-manager-job-design|Agent Manager Job Design]] at miniature scale — which is what makes the audit's redesign sections credible in month 2.

## Risks / Failure Modes
- **Endless preparation** — courses, branding, tool research, no outreach; the week-3 deadline exists to break it.
- **Premature selling** — pitching projects you can't yet scope; month 1 sells conversations and audits only.
- **Skipping the practice audit** — the rehearsal is where the method's rough edges get found for free instead of on a paying client.
