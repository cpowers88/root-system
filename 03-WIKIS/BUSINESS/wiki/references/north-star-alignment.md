# North Star Alignment

> Routing bridge between durable direction, the active business hypothesis, and
> this wiki's methods. It deliberately does not restate the controlling documents.

## Authority

1. `.ROOT\01-NORTH_STAR\NORTH_STAR.md` controls the destination, fixed
   commitments, permanent capability base, priorities, guardrails, and Ratchet.
2. `.ROOT\01-NORTH_STAR\Goals & Milestones\CURRENT_STRATEGY.md` controls the
   current Advisor-Builder vehicle, access wedges, first offer, assumptions,
   milestones, and evidence rules.
3. This BUSINESS wiki owns deeper research, methods, pricing models, offer options,
   and blank delivery templates. It may challenge the strategy with evidence; it
   may not silently replace it.
4. CASTLE owns phase/test sequencing and proof status. `.ROOT\NOW.md` owns today's
   next action.

If these sources disagree, stop copying wording. Reconcile the lower-authority
artifact to the higher-authority one and preserve the evidence that exposed drift.

## Current Strategic Thesis

The active hypothesis is the industry-agnostic Advisor-Builder loop:

```text
observe -> diagnose -> engineer -> deploy only what is justified
        -> train/maintain -> harvest a reusable asset
```

Real estate and construction are access wedges, not identity limits. The first
offer is an observation session plus concise written audit. Retained improvement is
the leading revenue-engine hypothesis, not a guaranteed outcome. Use
`CURRENT_STRATEGY.md` for the exact live wording and proof milestones.

## Three-Track Boundary

- **School:** fixed spine and deadline authority.
- **Technology:** permanent capability fuel, proven through real use.
- **Business/value creation:** tests demand, delivery, income, and asset compounding.

The tracks coordinate; this wiki does not declare its own calendar priority. Before
acting on a roadmap page, reconcile it to CURRENT_STRATEGY, CASTLE, the live school
frontiers, and NOW.

## Decision Filter

Before an offer, market, tool, or initiative becomes active, ask:

1. What real problem and measurable consequence does it address?
2. Which current strategy assumption or milestone would it test?
3. What evidence already exists, and where is its permanent home?
4. What is the smallest bounded proof, and what does it displace?
5. What outcome would strengthen, weaken, or end the hypothesis?
6. Can it fit school, family, privacy, and human-approval constraints?

New profit ideas pass CASTLE's `adding-a-profit-skill` gate. Material external
change may enter Watchtower after its evidence is preserved here. Our own field
results return directly through CASTLE to CURRENT_STRATEGY.

## Ratchet Rule

Quarterly review separates the destination from the vehicle. Use measured demand,
revenue, outcomes, constraints, and material technology/market changes to keep,
refine, narrow, or replace the vehicle. Novelty is not evidence. Chris may authorize
a mid-cycle change after an explicit impact review; AI never rewrites strategy
silently.
