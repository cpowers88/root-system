# CLAUDE.md — Education Wiki OS (General KSU Support)

## Scope

This wiki was narrowed on July 7, 2026, when its original Python/CS content moved
wholesale to `03-WIKIS\PYTHON` (that content was already 100% Python/CS — there was
nothing general-KSU inside it to leave behind). This wiki starts empty and fills as
Chris's other KSU coursework needs staged support.

This vault supports **TCOM, ECON, ENGR, and any other KSU coursework that isn't
subject-specific enough to earn its own wiki** — Python/CS lives in
`03-WIKIS\PYTHON`, Physics lives in `03-WIKIS\PHYSICS`. If a subject grows enough
staged material to justify its own hub, it gets carved out the same way PYTHON was.

## System Boundary

- This vault supports independent learning, vocabulary review, concept
  explanation, practice drills, and personal study planning for general KSU
  coursework.
- `02-LIBRARY\00-SCHOOL\` holds the actual course files (syllabi, notes, assignments)
  per `WHERE_IT_GOES.md` — this wiki is refined study support, not a mirror of
  the course folder.
- `03-WIKIS\BUSINESS` is the business/North-Star execution wiki (FORGE, which
  held this role until July 7, 2026, has retired). Do not mix this vault with
  BUSINESS unless Chris explicitly asks for a transfer or bridge.

## Shared Wiki Rules

The shared layer for all `03-WIKIS` hubs — raw/ immutability, large-source
chunking, session start/close minimums, update-over-create, contradiction
flagging, recency markers, the lint pass, and the academic-integrity boundary
this wiki operates under — lives in `00-BRAIN\AGENT.md § Wiki Shared
Layer`. One copy, zero drift. This file carries only this wiki's own rules.

## Folder Structure

```text
raw/          # syllabi, course policies, reference material — immutable
wiki/
  index.md
  log.md
  current-position.md   # live per-course status (TCOM/ECON/ENGR, Fall 2026)
```

Build out `concepts/`, `glossary/`, `flashcards/`, `drills/` per course only when
a course's material actually lands here — do not pre-build empty structure for
courses that aren't using this wiki yet.

## Final Operating Principle

This wiki activates one course at a time, on demand. Do not build ahead of
actual need — an empty, honest scaffold beats a pre-built structure nobody fills.
