# Common Errors — Where the TCOM Grade Is Actually Lost

**The risk in this course is not understanding. It is non-compliance.**
Prof. Diamond is a hard grader with a strict late-work penalty, and the material
itself is not conceptually difficult. Points leave over format, naming, channel,
and timing — every one of which is fully controllable and none of which requires
understanding anything.

Treat this page as a pre-submission checklist, not as reading.

## 1. Wrong file name

Required: `LastName_04_AssignmentName.docx` — e.g. `Powers_04_TechnicalDescription.docx`

`04` is the section number and does not change. Memorize the pattern before
Aug 24; there is a Week 1 quiz on it, which is the course telling you plainly
that naming is graded.

## 2. Wrong submission channel

**D2L Assignments folder only** — with exactly one exception.

- **The Business Email assignment is submitted by actually emailing Prof. Diamond**,
  because the deliverable *is* a real email.
- **Never email a document attachment for any other assignment.** Her syllabus:
  *"I do NOT accept email attachments for any assignment."*

Submitting the right document through the wrong channel scores as not submitted.

## 3. Executive summary written as an introduction

The two are for different readers. See `../concepts/technical-report-anatomy.md`.
This is the most common *structural* error in the report.

## 4. Drafting before deciding the audience

A draft that will not start is almost always a draft with no named reader. Ask
"who is reading this and what do they need to decide?" before writing a sentence.

## 5. Improvising format

Match the rubric exactly. Never invent structure where a template exists — the
course ships templates in `raw\Open-TC_Course-Resources\` for nearly every
deliverable. Creative deviation is not rewarded here; it is marked down.

## 6. Missing a weekly report section

The technical report is built across Weeks 6–12 with sections reviewed weekly. One
missed section does not cost one week — it makes every later section late.

## 7. Trusting the printed syllabus dates

The syllabus schedule table carries **recycled January/Spring dates inside a Fall
2026 header** (flag #57 data-quality note). The *weekly rhythm* is right; the
printed dates are not. **Trust D2L for dates and for the assignment-weights table**,
which is cut off in the captured scan.

## 8. Treating attendance as free

In person, TTh. First 3 unexcused absences are free; from the 4th, roughly 3.5
points off the final grade each. 6 absences ≈ a full letter grade, 9 ≈ two. Missing
class for a non-emergency reason — work, travel, studying for another test — is a
zero on that day's in-class work.

## Pre-submission compliance check

Run every time, from `HAT_TCOM.md`:

- [ ] File named `LastName_04_AssignmentName.docx`
- [ ] Uploaded to the D2L Assignments folder (not email — unless it *is* the email)
- [ ] Rubric reviewed, every required element present
- [ ] Word/page count within spec
