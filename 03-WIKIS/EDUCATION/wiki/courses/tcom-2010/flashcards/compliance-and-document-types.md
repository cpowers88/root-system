# Flashcards — TCOM Compliance and Document Types

Free recall from memory, then check. Recognition is not retrieval — do not read the
answers first. Cold-check these at the start of a TCOM session, unannounced.

## Compliance — the cheap points

**Q:** What is the exact file-naming pattern for a TCOM submission?
**A:** `LastName_04_AssignmentName.docx` — e.g. `Powers_04_TechnicalDescription.docx`

**Q:** Where does every TCOM assignment go?
**A:** The D2L Assignments folder. Email attachments are not accepted for any
assignment.

**Q:** What is the single exception, and why?
**A:** The Business Email assignment — Chris emails Prof. Diamond directly, because
the deliverable *is* a real email.

**Q:** How many unexcused absences are free, and what does the next one cost?
**A:** Three. From the 4th, roughly 3.5 points off the final grade each.

**Q:** Should you trust the printed dates in the syllabus schedule table?
**A:** No — recycled January/Spring dates under a Fall 2026 header. Trust the
weekly rhythm and check D2L for actual dates.

## The decision model

**Q:** The four questions to answer before drafting anything.
**A:** Audience, purpose, genre, constraints.

**Q:** The one question that unsticks a stalled draft.
**A:** "Who is reading this, and what do they need to decide?"

**Q:** Purpose is stated as a ______, not a ______.
**A:** An action the reader can take — not a topic.

## Document types

**Q:** Executive summary vs. introduction — who is each for?
**A:** Executive summary: the decision-maker who may read nothing else; must stand
alone. Introduction: the reader about to read everything; orients them.

**Q:** What is a transmittal?
**A:** The short professional message handing the finished document to whoever
commissioned it.

**Q:** What is being tested in usability testing?
**A:** The document. Not the reader.

**Q:** What makes a proposal different from a report?
**A:** A proposal argues that a problem should be solved a specific way, usually to
get approval or resources. A report delivers findings on work done.

**Q:** Genre is a ______, not a template file.
**A:** Reader expectation. The template exists because the expectation does.

## The report build

**Q:** Over which weeks is the technical report assembled?
**A:** Roughly Weeks 6–12, section by section, reviewed weekly.

**Q:** What is the real failure mode on the report?
**A:** Not a bad report — a missed weekly section, which makes every later section
late.

**Q:** Which two deliverables have no ready-made template in the course ancillaries?
**A:** The Report Group Charter & Task Schedule (Week 6) and the Reflective Memo
(Week 12).
