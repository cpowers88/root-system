## ECON 1000 — Contemporary Economic Issues (BAC / 80643)

- **Format:** 2 credit hours, 14 weeks, face-to-face Tue/Thu 8:00–8:50 am,
  Marietta Q-202. Instructor: Zeynep Kelani (zkelani@kennesaw.edu; office
  hours by appointment Tue 9:15–11:00).
- **Textbook:** *Contemporary Economic Issues*, Mathews & Patrono (5th ed.),
  eBook via D2L Day One Access.
- **Grading:** Exam 1 25% · Exam 2 25% · Quizzes 50% (two highest of four
  count 25% each; two lowest dropped). Scale ~89.5 = A cutoff, rounded.
- **Assessments:** 2 exams (40 MC, 60 min, online via D2L, NOT cumulative,
  both mandatory) + 4 quizzes (10 MC, 20 min). All due 11:00 pm Eastern.
  **Zero make-ups, zero extensions, no exceptions** — missed = 0. Up to 5
  extra-credit points via announced assignments only.
- **Schedule spine (Fall 2026):** Ch. 1–5 → Quiz Ch. 1-2-3 (wk 3, 9/8) →
  Quiz Ch. 4-5 (wk 5, 9/22) → **Exam 1 Tue 9/29 (Ch. 1–5, online, no class
  meeting)** → Ch. 7–9 → Quiz (wk 9, 10/22) → Ch. 10–12 → Quiz (wk 12,
  11/12) → Financial Literacy special section + extra-credit quiz (wk 13) →
  **Final Exam Thu 12/3 (Ch. 7–12 only, online)**.
- **Topics:** production models, economic systems (capitalism vs socialism),
  markets/surplus/deadweight loss, GDP & growth, inflation, unemployment,
  market failure, government failure, inequality & redistribution,
  financial literacy.
- Danger-weeks note: Chapters 7–9 and 10–12 land inside Oct 5 – Nov 11 —
  the school-only window covers the entire back half of this course.

## TCOM 2010 — Technical Writing (Section 04 / 85633)

- **Format:** 3 credit hours (prereq ENGL 1101), Radow College. Instructor:
  Lisa Diamond (ldiamon@kennesaw.edu or lisa.diamond@kennesaw.edu — email
  only; MS Teams evening meetings by appointment; directory phone doesn't
  reach her).
- **Textbook:** free open textbook, link given in class ($0 materials).
- **Content:** technical descriptions, instructions, proposals,
  recommendation reports; rhetorical theory, audience analysis, document
  design, visual aids, editing; at least one complete technical report.
  Units visible in the schedule: business email + memos, ethics analysis,
  audience analysis, observation assessment; file-naming conventions
  enforced (`Lastname_Section#_Draft.docx`).
- **Grading style:** assignments graded after due date (midnight due, 8 am
  grace); 2-point assignments are pass/fail, larger ones rubric-graded.
  Business Email unit weight 15 (draft 15% / final 85% within the unit);
  full weights table is only partially legible in the scan — pull the
  final table from D2L in week 1.
- Serves Area F for engineering/CS majors — the professional-writing rep
  feeds every client-facing artifact skill in the business track.

## ENGR 1000 — Introduction to Engineering (⚠️ Fall 2025 syllabus)

- **Format:** 1 credit hour, asynchronous online, no meetings. Instructor
  (2025): Laura Ruhala. No materials cost.
- **Grading (2025):** attendance quizzes 50% (one per module, lowest
  dropped) + assignments 50%; 90/80/70/60 scale. Deadlines firm, no late
  work; week 15 offers one replacement assignment.
- **Content:** engineering disciplines at KSU, advising/registration and
  plan of study, study/test/time-management skills, basic math and
  programming, teamwork.
- **This is last year's syllabus** (Section W01/81227, Fall 2025) — grabbed
  June 2026 as a preview. Get the Fall 2026 version when it posts and
  replace this section.
