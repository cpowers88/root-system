# TCOM 2010 — Semester Resource Map

Maps the real, exact-section syllabus schedule (see
`fall-2026-course-briefs.md` and `02-LIBRARY\00-SCHOOL\03-TCOM\`) to the
actual assigned textbook (*Open Technical Communication*, `raw\Open-TC-PDF.pdf`),
its instructor ancillary package (`raw\Open-TC_Course-Resources\`), and its
per-example worked files (`raw\Linked-Resources\`, numbered by the book's own
chapter.section scheme, e.g. `2-1_` = Chapter 2, section 1).

**Trust the weekly rhythm, not the printed calendar dates** — the syllabus's
own schedule table carries recycled January/Spring dates inside its Fall
2026 header (see the Data-Quality Flags on `fall-2026-course-briefs.md`).
Weeks below are relative (Week 1, Week 2, ...), Tuesday/Thursday, per the
syllabus's own weekly structure.

## Gaps found while mapping (nothing invented to fill them)

- **Report Group Charter & Task Schedule** (Week 6) has no matching
  ancillary template in `Course-Resources` — closest fit is the generic
  `Activities\Schedule.docx` / `Schedule_Blank.docx`, not a purpose-built
  charter template. Chris may need to build this one from the syllabus's
  own linked sample (`Sample Group Charter`, `Roles & Tasks within Your
  Team`, `Sample Task Schedule` — all named directly in the real syllabus's
  D2L links, not captured in raw since they sit behind D2L auth).
- **Reflective Memo** (Week 12) has no dedicated ancillary template either —
  build from the general Memo template plus the syllabus's own framing.
- `Sample-Syllabi/` in `Course-Resources` was deliberately excluded from
  this map — it's the publisher's generic course syllabi, not Chris's real
  section, and would only add noise.
