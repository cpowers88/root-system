# How to Use the North Star

Use this folder to answer three different questions without mixing their
answers into one file.

## 1. Is this permanent?

Open `NORTH_STAR.md`.

It owns:

- the function of `.ROOT`;
- the fixed destination and commitments;
- Chris's authority and AI's limits;
- the priority order when tracks conflict;
- the evidence rule for changing direction; and
- the ownership map for everything below it.

AI may propose an amendment, but it may not write to `NORTH_STAR.md` without
Chris's explicit approval. No AI may write under a `raw\` folder without
Chris authorizing the named exception.

## 2. Is this a changeable target?

Open `Goals & Milestones\`.

Use it for:

- the current business strategy;
- time-bound school, capability, and revenue goals;
- milestone definitions and proof bars; and
- the ranked cross-domain capability gap.

A goal or milestone states the target, deadline or review trigger, owner, and
proof required. It points to the owning wiki or project for live progress; it
does not copy that progress into this folder.

## 3. Is this a system requirement?

Open `System Contracts\`.

Use it for requirements that every relevant part of `.ROOT` must satisfy,
including capability, information flow, owner returns, validation, and
escalation. Exact file placement and naming remain owned by
`00-BRAIN\WHERE_IT_GOES.md`.

## Change path

For an ordinary adaptive change:

`signal → evidence owner → CASTLE review → Chris approval when consequential → owning goal or strategy update`

For a proposed change to the permanent law:

`evidence → impact review → Chris explicit approval → NORTH_STAR.md edit → validation`

## Review rhythm

- Weekly and monthly reviews check movement and capacity without rewriting the
  law.
- Quarterly Ratchet review checks the fixed destination separately from the
  current vehicle.
- A material event may trigger an earlier review.
- Review records live in `00-BRAIN\Session_Logs\`; only approved direction
  changes return here.

## Before closing a change

Confirm:

1. one owner holds each fact;
2. permanent law and adaptive plans remain separated;
3. wiki or project progress was not duplicated here;
4. affected links and owner paths resolve;
5. Chris approved every consequential or law-level change; and
6. the appropriate validation and review record were completed.
