## Selection rule

At each monthly review:

1. identify the next 90-day outcome;
2. locate the one capability most likely to block it;
3. confirm the gap from observed performance;
4. choose the smallest repeated practice that produces proof;
5. route execution to the owner and CASTLE; and
6. change this ranking only when evidence changes the bottleneck.

Do not open parallel curricula merely because several weaknesses exist.

## Value connection

Capability work must eventually support at least one of:

- successful independent course performance;
- a functioning project used in a real workflow;
- a verified reduction in time, error, risk, or uncertainty;
- a reusable technical or delivery asset;
- a qualified business problem or paid outcome; or
- a stronger decision supported by evidence.

Learning remains valid before revenue. It becomes economic evidence only after
real use, buyer behavior, measurable outcome, or repeated reuse.
