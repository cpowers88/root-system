## Protected readiness gates

### CSE 1321 and CSE 1321L

Before course activation:

- close the current PYTHON Stage 3 loop gate through fresh independent proof;
- establish working fluency with functions, parameters, and return values;
- retrieve and explain variables, types, Boolean logic, selection, and iteration;
- read a short program, identify state and control flow, predict output, and
  explain the result before running it;
- write pseudocode before implementation and debug from observed output or an
  error message; and
- confirm the exact lecture and lab policies from the official Fall 2026
  syllabi and D2L.

During the semester:

- the live course module controls topic order;
- graded status is assumed whenever unclear;
- no generative AI drafts, solves, rewrites, or debugs submitted work;
- private ungraded practice must use fresh examples, not transformed assignment
  prompts; and
- learner progress moves only from independent performance.

### PHYS 2211

Before course activation:

- continue from the PHYSICS wiki's verified live frontier;
- make vectors, two-dimensional motion, force diagrams, units, and calculus
  meaning retrievable under independent conditions;
- solve representative problems using `diagram → knowns/unknowns → governing
  principle → equation → units → reasonableness check`;
- use equations with their assumptions and physical meaning, not as detached
  symbol patterns; and
- replace neighbor-section assumptions when the exact Section 54 syllabus and
  instructor information become available.

During the semester:

- lecture and exact-section requirements override provisional preparation;
- maintain a short reading-ahead buffer when mastery evidence supports it;
- run retrieval and mixed-problem review before each major exam;
- log the error class after a miss: concept, representation, equation choice,
  algebra/calculus, units, or execution; and
- slow the path when prerequisite weakness appears instead of generating more
  content.

### Value-production gate

Coursework is not automatically a business project. After real use, review
course-permitted learning for:

- a reusable technical method;
- a non-submission proof project;
- a clearer workflow, data, or systems model;
- a sanitized business capability asset; or
- a system improvement supported by observed evidence.

Nothing is harvested when doing so violates course policy, copies submitted
work, exposes private course material, or displaces required learning.
