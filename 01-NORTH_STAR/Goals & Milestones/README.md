# Goals & Milestones

This folder holds the route, not the destination and not today's checklist.

Use it for outcomes that matter enough to coordinate across courses, wikis,
projects, or business work but may change when evidence changes.

## Current structure

- `OPERATIONS.md` — machine rules for every goal and milestone.
- `HOW_TO_USE.md` — the human workflow for creating and reviewing them.
- `CURRENT_STRATEGY.md` — the current business vehicle under test.
- `fall_2026_semester.md` — the Fall 2026 academic outcome and proof gates.
- `capability_development_goal.md` — the integrated capability stack and
  current cross-domain weak-link order.
- `value_production_goal.md` — the evidence ladder from capability through
  real use, demand, revenue, and repeatability.

Weekly plans do not belong here. CASTLE sequences execution; Session Logs hold
retrospective reviews; wikis hold live domain truth.
