## Proof ladder

Evidence advances in this order; later stages cannot be claimed from earlier
activity.

1. **Capability proof** — Chris independently performs the method.
2. **Use proof** — the method or artifact is used on a permitted real workflow.
3. **Problem proof** — a real person recognizes the documented cost, delay,
   error, risk, or decision problem.
4. **Outcome proof** — a response produces an observed improvement or a better
   decision.
5. **Demand proof** — a qualified person grants access, requests more work, or
   expresses credible willingness to pay.
6. **Revenue proof** — payment is received.
7. **Repeatability proof** — the result or asset works again with lower effort
   or stronger economics.
