# NORTH_STAR.md — Chris Powers
### The controlling direction for `.ROOT` | Read every session
### Last updated: July 19, 2026 — mission, capability, capacity, and deadline alignment

## The Compass — Why This Exists

`.ROOT` exists to help me build freedom and security for my family by growing my ability to learn, solve valuable problems,
build systems, and turn proven work into income and lasting assets — and to help more people as that capability grows.

## The Mission — What I'm Becoming

Become an elite AI, technology, business, and systems integrator—an Advisor-Builder who can enter an unfamiliar operation,
understand the real workflow, identify costly friction, and integrate the right people, process, data, software, automation,
and AI to create measurable value and compounding assets.

Technical breadth and depth serve judgment. Markets, offers, vendors, tools, and
revenue models remain strategies to test—not identity.

## `.ROOT`'s Job

`.ROOT` is my human-governed, self-improving AI operating partner. It senses change, researches, teaches, organizes knowledge,
supports focused execution, helps build and maintain systems, tests opportunities, records evidence, and improves through
reviewed outcomes. I make the final decisions; AI may challenge, execute, validate, and learn within approved boundaries.

## Fixed Destination, Commitments, and Near-Term Priority

- **North Star deadline:** October 8, 2031 — my 50th birthday. This is a hard
  measurement date; the vehicle may change, but the date does not.
- **Income floor:** $500K–$1M annual. This is a floor, not a ceiling.
- **Education:** complete the KSU BS in Industrial & Systems Engineering; the degree
  is fixed even if the business vehicle changes.
- **Near-term capability:** learn how real workflows, business applications, data, automation, AI, and integrations fit together;
  understand what organizations use and why; prove the ability to combine technology that improves work. Aligned income may
  follow, but unrelated revenue does not outrank this capability unless Chris explicitly chooses a bounded exception.
- **Near-term funding constraint:** the July 2026 funding change requires additional income before Spring 2027 enrollment.
  Aligned income is preferred; REVENUE_LAB owns the evidence, and this constraint does not make unrelated revenue outrank
  capability building without Chris's explicit decision.
- **Permanent capability base:** systems thinking and workflow diagnosis; AI, software, data, and automation; technology-stack
  integration, security, operational reliability, communication, and business judgment.
- **Human governance:** Chris owns direction, timing, relationships, accountability, consequential decisions, and final quality. AI supplies leverage.

## The Three Coordinated Tracks

1. **School — the spine.** Meet fixed deadlines and turn formal engineering learning into transferable capability and real application.
2. **Technology — the fuel.** Build across software, data, AI, automation, integration, security, and the evolving technology landscape through real use.
3. **Business and value creation — the engine.** Solve valuable problems, create income, and compound proven work into assets.

Fixed school commitments come first. During semesters, preserve the 5–10-hour weekly
technology/business floor whenever deadlines allow. Chris owns timing outside fixed
commitments; work is scoped to declared capacity, and extra time is upside. Urgent
workflow, aligned technology, or client evidence outranks routine queue work. Unrelated
revenue does not displace capability building without Chris's decision.

## Current Best Strategy — The Bet Being Tested

The leading strategy is the Advisor-Builder business: observe how an operation runs,
identify costly workflow friction and its economic consequence, redesign the right
people/process/data/technology combination, implement the smallest sound response,
support the people involved, maintain what works, and harvest proven wins.

It receives serious effort and remains replaceable when evidence points toward
something more valuable, practical, scalable, or compatible with real capacity.
Assumptions, offers, milestones, and review conditions live in
`01-NORTH_STAR\Goals & Milestones\CURRENT_STRATEGY.md`.

## The Engine — Capability Into Compounding Value

Whenever practical, real work should do more than solve something once. It should
strengthen a maintained system or integration, reusable method or delivery asset,
owned application, or proof-led content/audience asset.

**The Standing Engine Question:**

> What valuable problem is most worth solving next, and what must we learn, prove,
> build, or integrate to solve it exceptionally well — or get ahead of it — and
> convert the result into income or a compounding asset?

## The Ratchet — Evidence Moves the Strategy

Committed to the destination, flexible about the vehicle. Strategy changes on
evidence—demand, revenue, outcomes, constraints, capacity, or material technology
change—not because a new idea is exciting.

1. Economic targets are floors; early wins may raise them. The October 8, 2031
   measurement deadline does not move.
2. Review strategy quarterly, or sooner when Chris authorizes an impact-reviewed
   response to material evidence.
3. A missed milestone requires diagnosis of market, access, offer, execution,
   timing, capacity, and measurement before strategy changes.
4. `...projectSuccess\` watches external change; signals influence this star only
   through evidence, CASTLE review, and measured outcomes.

## Operating Guardrails

- **Fact over theory:** map the real process and test the claim before building.
- **Validate before productizing:** yourself → one real use or client → many.
- **Ideas are not commitments:** new profit ideas pass the CASTLE gate.
- **Planning can imitate progress:** if system work displaces learning, proof,
  delivery, or aligned value evidence, return to the real output.
- **Capacity is real:** fixed commitments constrain the field; Chris owns timing;
  the system reduces friction rather than manufacturing pressure.
- **AI is leverage, not accountability:** nothing consequential ships without
  Chris's quality and approval gate.
