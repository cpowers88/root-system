## The Mission

Become a **top 1% AI, Technology, and Business integrator and application
developer**, backed by a formal education in systems engineering.

**Positioning: The Advisor-Builder.** Not a general software developer, not a
theoretical consultant. I operate where business reality meets technical
execution — the surgical strike when a company is bleeding margin on
double-entry, manual reporting, broken data flows, or underutilized software.
AI-first is the identity, not a feature: I redesign businesses around
human + AI operating models.

**The Execution Loop** (elevator version; full 9-step model in Track 3):
1. **Observe** — walk the floor, map the actual workflows, not the corporate fantasy
2. **Diagnose** — find the operational friction and the exact dollars it costs
3. **Engineer** — design the streamlined system (APQC standards; Python, SQL, Flask, Make.com)
4. **Deploy & Retain** — train the team, maintain on retainer — and every engagement produces assets I keep

The ISYE BS gives the formal methodology, vocabulary, and credential. AI
leverage lets a two-member team out-produce firms ten times the size.

**North Star date:** October 8, 2031 — my 50th birthday.
**Revenue floor:** $500K–$1M annual. *A floor, not a ceiling — see The Ratchet.*
**First client:** March 2027. **Team at the star:** Chris + AI, maximally leveraged.

**Entry wedges (dual, parallel — stated once here, referenced everywhere else):**
- *Immediate — Real Estate via Heather's network.* Full-time realtor with warm
  access to agents, lawyers, brokers, investors, developers. Warm intros replace
  cold outreach; fragmented, data-heavy, audit-ripe industry. Fastest path to
  first paid conversation.
- *Parallel — Construction domain credibility.* I know the culture, language,
  and field workflows; $2M–$15M contractors carry huge normalized
  inefficiencies invisible to owners. I can walk a job site and be taken seriously.
- *Both:* no software required to start — eyes, a clipboard, a report; huge
  technology adoption gap in field operations.

The methodology is industry-agnostic. These are wedges of access, not identity
limits — any business with operational waste qualifies.

## The Ratchet — The Star Pushes Itself Forward

Arrive ahead of the target, already aiming higher. The star evolves by
mechanism, not mood:

1. **Floors, not ceilings.** Every number here is a minimum; beating one early moves the target.
2. **The ratchet turns at quarterly reviews only.** Early milestones or capability jumps (AI counts) raise targets at the quarterly, with reasons recorded. Between quarterlies, targets hold still.
3. **Ratchet up, never down** without an explicit conversation.
4. **The Watchtower feeds the ratchet.** `...projectSuccess\` tracks where AI/tech/markets move; signals route through the castle's adding-a-profit-skill gate; survivors can justify a turn at the next quarterly.

"Stay ahead of all trends" is a procedure, not a wish.

## The Engine — A Compounding Digital-Asset Portfolio

Retainer service work is the revenue core and the learning engine. Every
engagement deliberately produces assets that compound. Four asset classes,
each expected to carry milestones:

1. **Client retainer systems** — the core engine: audits → builds → maintenance retainers that grow in value.
2. **Productized templates and playbooks** — audit kits, workflow maps, SOP libraries, dashboard kits — built once from real engagements, sold repeatedly. **Indexed by APQC PCF** so assets organize by business function: a payroll bottleneck deploys from `PCF_10.0_Manage_Finance`, not from scratch.
3. **Standalone software / applications** — personal and client builds (tracker, POL) that graduate into products. Application development is part of the identity.
4. **Content and audience** — documented proof of work compounding into reach, trust, inbound demand.

**The Standing Engine Question** — every weekly, quarterly, and Watchtower sweep:

> What is the most profitable thing this system could be doing right now,
> and what do we need to learn to be the best at it — or ahead of it?

The answer is allowed to change — through the ratchet, the Watchtower, and
the castle gate. Mechanism, not impulse.

- Log the session before closing when continuity matters.

- **Sell the Hole, Not the Drill.** Clients buy recaptured time and stopped profit leaks; code is just the delivery mechanism.
- **Fact Over Theory.** Never build an automation until the manual process is thoroughly mapped. Assumptions kill audits.
- **Protect the Core.** Time is the scarcest resource: any recurring admin task over two minutes gets scripted or eliminated.

## Known Risks

- **Scope creep:** idea generation is highest-tier. Defenses: track order, no-orphan-skills, castle gate, quarterly-only ratchet.
- **Ratchet abuse:** "the goal evolved" never justifies abandoning a deliverable mid-cycle.
- **Avoidance disguised as planning:** if the system was rebuilt twice in a week, ask what the week actually produced. (July 6, 2026: rebuilt twice in one day — skeleton froze same day. The next rebuild request gets this line quoted back.)
- **Time compression:** family of nine + full-time school — the system reduces friction or it goes (see Protect the Core).
- **Market narrowing:** the wedges are *access*, not identity. The methodology stays industry-agnostic.
- **Credential gap:** the degree is the primary credential. Bs acceptable, As preferred, every ISYE course connects to a service.
- **Wiki as time sink:** wikis matter only when they produce promoted artifacts; if wiki sessions outnumber skill/audit sessions, rebalance.
- **AI dependency:** AI is the workforce; judgment, relationships, accountability stay human. Nothing ships without my quality gate.
- **Heather-network dependency:** the immediate wedge leans on one warm network — build construction and a third channel in parallel.
