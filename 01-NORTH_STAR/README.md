# North Star

This folder separates what must remain stable from what is allowed to change.

## Start here

Read `NORTH_STAR.md` for the permanent law: where `.ROOT` is going, what Chris
retains authority over, how competing priorities are ordered, and how evidence
may change the current plan.

The law is intentionally short and machine-oriented. It does not explain the
whole system or contain the current business plan.

## What lives here

- `NORTH_STAR.md` — permanent direction and authority.
- `Goals & Milestones\` — current strategy, time-bound goals, milestones, and
  cross-domain capability priorities.
- `System Contracts\` — requirements that translate the law into system
  behavior and information flow.
- `HOW_TO_USE.md` — the human workflow for reviewing or changing this layer.

## What does not live here

- Today's work and sequencing belong to `NOW.md` and CASTLE.
- Domain knowledge and learner progress belong to the owning wiki.
- Projects belong to their project or business workspace.
- Session, weekly, monthly, and quarterly review records belong to
  `00-BRAIN\Session_Logs\`.
- AI behavior, file safety, and session procedure belong to
  `00-BRAIN\AGENT.md`.

## Plain-language test

If changing a statement would change the destination or permanent rules, review
the law. If it would change the current route, target, timing, or business bet,
review `Goals & Milestones`. If it describes how `.ROOT` must operate, review
`System Contracts`.
