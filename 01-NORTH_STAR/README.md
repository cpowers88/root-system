# 01-NORTH_STAR — Direction Router
### Last updated: July 14, 2026 — progressive loading installed

## What This Folder Owns

This is the mission layer: where Chris is going, which commitments are fixed, what
business vehicle is currently being tested, and how evidence earns a change. It is
not a project tracker, research library, or second command center.

## Load Only What the Question Needs

| Question | File |
|---|---|
| What is the durable direction and what may not drift? | `NORTH_STAR.md` — always load for strategic or priority work |
| What business vehicle are we seriously testing now? | `Goals & Milestones\CURRENT_STRATEGY.md` |
| What must be proven before Fall 2026 begins? | `Goals & Milestones\PRE-SEMESTER_PREP_PLAN.md` |
| What must the AI OS be capable of? | `System Contracts\ROOT_CAPABILITY_CONTRACT.md` |
| What is the current weak link? | `SKILL_GAP_ANALYSIS.md` |
| What happened this week/month/quarter? | `Weekly Reviews\` and its template |

`NORTH_STAR.md` controls every companion. A companion may add current detail; it may
not silently change the destination, fixed commitments, priority policy, human
governance, or evidence rule.

## Folder Boundaries

- CASTLE sequences phases, skills, tests, and proof status; it does not rewrite the star.
- Domain wikis own research and learner truth.
- `...projectSuccess` owns material external signals, not strategy decisions.
- `System Contracts\` translates the star into capability/return requirements; it
  does not replace AGENT.md governance.
- `.ROOT\NOW.md` owns today's next action.
- Completed milestone evidence may be logged in `Goals & Milestones\`; live project
  execution remains in its owning realm.

Handoffs remain in `00-BRAIN\Session_Logs\`, not here.

## Review Contract

### Weekly

Use the weekly template to review the three tracks, family/capacity, the Engine
Question, and the Watchtower sweep. Return only material movement to current-position,
CURRENT_STRATEGY, or the appropriate evidence home.

### Monthly

1. Read the month's weeklies and handoffs for patterns.
2. Score School, Technology, Business/Value, and Personal capacity.
3. Recheck the one weak link in `SKILL_GAP_ANALYSIS.md`.
4. Reconcile active current-position pages and open `SYSTEM_FLAGS.md`.
5. Set at most three priorities for the next month.
6. Record whether the system moved closer to October 8, 2031.

### Quarterly Ratchet Review

Review the destination separately from the vehicle:

1. Confirm fixed commitments and economic floor.
2. Review measured outcomes against the assumptions and milestones in
   `CURRENT_STRATEGY.md`.
3. Bring in only material Watchtower signals and completed CASTLE tests.
4. Keep, refine, replace, or narrow the vehicle based on evidence.
5. Raise a floor when results justify it; do not lower one to make a report look good.

Chris may authorize a mid-cycle strategy change after an explicit impact review.
AI may propose and challenge; it may not silently rewrite the star or strategy.
