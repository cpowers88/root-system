# Phase 4 — First Offer Readiness

**Window**: February – March 2027
**One-line purpose**: Assemble and rehearse the sellable offer so March 2027 conversations have something concrete to buy.

## Purpose
Convert Phases 2–3 capability into named, priced products. Per the offer ladder:
never sell one big thing to a stranger — sell a **small diagnostic** to a stranger,
a **scoped project** to a believer, a **retainer** to a client with working systems
(source: service-offer-ladder.md).

## Why It Matters to the North Star
The current strategy targets the first paid consulting conversation for March 2027
(`CURRENT_STRATEGY.md`). The audit is "a paid sales process
disguised as consulting — and real consulting disguised as a sales process": the
client pays to have waste quantified, and the report's last page is the next sale's
first page (source: smb-ai-audit-method.md). Charging is non-negotiable — free
audits attract non-buyers.

## Skills Needed
- Offer construction & pricing — Commercial
- Consultative discovery, rehearsed OUT LOUD — Commercial
- Audit delivery method (5 steps, 1–2 weeks) — Diagnostic/Delivery
- Proposal/SOW usage — Delivery

## Skills NOT Needed Yet
- Implementation building — Rung 2 delivery matters AFTER an audit sells it
- Team/delegation anything — Phase 9
