# Phase 4 — First Offer Readiness

**Window**: February – March 2027
**One-line purpose**: Assemble and rehearse the current offer hypothesis so qualified conversations have something concrete to evaluate and buy.

## Purpose
Convert Phases 2–3 capability into a named, priced offer that can be tested. The
current offer-ladder hypothesis starts with a small diagnostic, then earns any
scoped implementation or maintained relationship through evidence
(source: service-offer-ladder.md).

## Why It Matters to the North Star
The current strategy targets the first paid consulting conversation for March 2027
(`CURRENT_STRATEGY.md`). Its paid-floor default distinguishes willingness-to-pay
evidence from friendly participation. Chris may authorize a bounded $0 test only as
a named exception, on the same terms as [[adding-a-profit-skill]] § When to Break
It — proof sentence, displacement, and stop condition recorded before it starts.
This exception never redefines the audit's paid-floor default.

## Skills Needed
- Offer construction & pricing — Commercial
- Consultative discovery, rehearsed OUT LOUD — Commercial
- Audit delivery method (5 steps, 1–2 weeks) — Diagnostic/Delivery
- Proposal/SOW usage — Delivery

## Skills NOT Needed Yet

- Team/delegation anything — Phase 9
