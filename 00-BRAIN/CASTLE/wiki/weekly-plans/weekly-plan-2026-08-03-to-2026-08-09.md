## Physics Block Contract — Daytime, Handwritten

Every block uses the live PHYSICS method:

`physical system -> calculus relationship -> symbolic construction -> physical meaning -> 1–2 applications -> light cold check`

| ID | Semester-wide calculus connection | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Motion chain and 2D components: `x(t) -> v(t) -> a(t)`, integration back with initial conditions, x/y sharing time | Derive and explain one 2D motion pair from a sketch |
| P2 | Circular motion: direction change, radial acceleration, tangential acceleration | Work the open circular-motion rep and explain what each derivative changes physically |
| P3 | Newton's second law as a differential relationship: force changes momentum/motion | Construct `F = ma` from a real force system and connect each term to the mechanism |
| P4 | Work and power: force accumulated over distance; energy changing over time | Interpret one `F-x` area and one power derivative |
| P5 | Impulse and momentum: force accumulated over time | Interpret one `F-t` area and connect it to momentum change |
| P6 | Rotation and angular momentum: angular derivatives, torque, moment of inertia, `tau = dL/dt` | Map each linear quantity to its rotational counterpart; use the right-hand rule physically |
| P7 | Springs, oscillations, and waves: second derivatives, sinusoidal behavior, wave propagation | Explain why acceleration points back toward equilibrium and how oscillation becomes a wave |
| P8 | Full-map retrieval | Given eight physical situations, identify the calculus operation and governing relationship without notes; mark misses for Weeks C–D |

This is breadth-first exposure, not eight stage passes. Stage 4 remains the
learner-truth frontier until its own gate moves.
