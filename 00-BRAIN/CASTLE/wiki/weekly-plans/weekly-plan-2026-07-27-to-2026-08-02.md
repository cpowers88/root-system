# Weekly Plan — July 27 to August 2, 2026
### Full-course-load school simulation · 32 blocks · Monday–Friday · Chris-approved 2026-07-26

**One block = 50 minutes single-subject focus + 10-minute break off-screen.**
Not divisible. Interrupted past ~10 min = half a block, recorded as such.

**Physics is handwritten — iPad.** Artifact is a photo/PDF of the page.
**Python is typed.** Notes `.md`, programs `.py` — programming produces programs.

## The capacity finding — read this before August 24

Laying 32 blocks against real hours exposed something the block count alone hid.

**Your weekday container is Monday–Friday, 9:00–5:00, minus lunch = 7 slots/day,
34 this week.** That is about **28 focused hours**. This week that is enough,
because there are no classes — every hour is study.

**During the semester it will not be.** Class and commute will consume roughly
14 of those hours: PHYS MWF 9:10–10:05 plus the Friday breakout, TCOM TTh
9:35–10:55, ECON TTh 8:00–8:55, CSE lecture and lab, and travel to Marietta.
That leaves roughly **20 study hours inside 9:00–5:00**, against a real
requirement of **26–39**.

> **Gap: about 6–19 hours per week that cannot fit in weekdays 9-to-5.**

Your remaining windows are Saturday 2:00–8:00 and Sunday 10:00–6:00 — about 14
hours. That closes the gap, but it means **weekend study becomes structural during
the semester, not optional.** Evenings stay reserved for Ben and family.

Nothing needs deciding today. But this is the real constraint, it is arithmetic
rather than opinion, and it is better known six weeks early than discovered in
September. Sunday's capacity verdict should be written with this in view.
