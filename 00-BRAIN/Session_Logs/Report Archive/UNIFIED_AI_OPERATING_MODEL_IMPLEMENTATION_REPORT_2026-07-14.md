## Outcome

`.ROOT` now operates as one human-governed AI team. Claude, Codex, and ATLAS
retain capability profiles, but model names no longer reserve files, folders,
roles, or task types. A capable AI with access may carry an in-scope request
from diagnosis through implementation and validation.

or better alternative once. Except for the narrow hard stops in `AGENT.md`, it
then follows Chris's direction. DIVERGE and CONVERGE are AI work modes, not
constraints on Chris.
