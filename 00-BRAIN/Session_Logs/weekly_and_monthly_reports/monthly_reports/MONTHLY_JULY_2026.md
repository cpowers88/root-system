## Exact Wording Tune Recommended

Do not relax the proof gates. Improve the interface.

Use one controlling Python card:

> **Build first:** attempt the smallest cold construction.  
> **Diagnose:** identify the exact failed concept or pattern.  
> **Read narrowly:** open only the matching section or source.  
> **Rebuild:** close the source and construct again.  
> **Prove:** pass the named cold test before advancing.

Directly below it, show one row per stage with:

`Stage | packet generated | studied | attempted | satisfied | next proof | unlocked reading`

This answers “what exists, where am I, what do I do, and why is later material
locked?” without creating a second source of truth.
