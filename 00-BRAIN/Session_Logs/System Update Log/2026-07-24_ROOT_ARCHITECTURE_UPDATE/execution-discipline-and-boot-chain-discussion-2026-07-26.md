Codex's argument for narrowing rule 8 is: *"Today Codex wrote and graded the

source batch documents — a model evaluating its own output scores measurably
better than a third party would score it.

> A drill may be graded by the teaching AI **when the grading is deterministic**

> Where the judgment is subjective or the promotion is consequential, an
> independent evaluator is required.

did the work, rather than on either model's self-report.
