# North Star Review Handoff and Fresh-Chat Prompt

## Decision direction from Chris

The architecture should be built from the inside outward:

```text
durable law and idea -> adaptive goals/milestones -> wiki ownership
-> CASTLE sequencing and maintenance -> shared BRAIN governance
```

Every layer is observable from above and is expected to perform its own work
well. Oversight does not erase local ownership; it verifies interfaces,
quality, and return paths.

The intended authority rule is:

- `NORTH_STAR.md` owns durable truth: mission, destination, fixed commitments,
  permanent capability direction, human authority, and the evidence rule.
- Business plans, offers, markets, and other replaceable vehicles do not belong
  in the North Star.
- `Goals & Milestones` owns adaptive goals and milestones. A material change is
  proposed through external evidence/Watchtower, evaluated and sequenced by
  CASTLE, and requires Chris's authorization before it changes live direction.
- `...projectSuccess` may surface material external evidence; it does not change
  goals by itself.
- A durable outcome such as completing the Industrial & Systems Engineering
  degree may belong in the fixed North Star commitments. School preparation,
  course readiness, and temporary academic targets belong in milestones because
  their path and timing can change.
- Each important wiki may have a goal or milestone page appropriate to its
  importance, but it must point upward to the governing goal and must not create
  a competing North Star.
- CASTLE owns cross-system sequencing and maintenance: why now, next action,
  owner, proof, return path, architecture proposals, dependency maps, and
  impact checks. It does not own direction or domain evidence.

These are design decisions to test against the live files, not permission to
rewrite them blindly.
