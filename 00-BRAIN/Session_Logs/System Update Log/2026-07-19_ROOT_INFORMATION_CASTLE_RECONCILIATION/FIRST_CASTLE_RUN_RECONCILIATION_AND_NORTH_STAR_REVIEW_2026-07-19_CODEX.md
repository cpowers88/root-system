## Strategy Language That Has Hardened Beyond Evidence

The phase path is useful, but several statements read as predetermined truth:

- Every course directly maps to a service.
- Phase 5 will sell exactly one thing.
- Flask/APIs/automation are assumed future implementation choices.
- Audit → implementation → retainer conversions are treated as known.
- $1,500–$2,500 beginner audit pricing is treated as settled.
- Retainers, first hiring, and multi-industry operating-company steps read as a
  guaranteed sequence rather than the current economic hypothesis.
- Free audits are prohibited absolutely before Chris-specific market evidence is
  available.

Recommended treatment: retain the serious path and its milestones, but label tool,
pricing, conversion, offer, and delivery claims as **current strategy assumptions**
until field evidence validates or replaces them.

Suggested standing sentence for the phase map:

> These phases operationalize the current strategy and its proof milestones. They
> are sequencing hypotheses, not identity or guaranteed chronology; the North Star
> and evidence Ratchet may reorder the vehicle, tools, offers, and delivery model.

Specific corrections:

- “Every course becomes a sellable service” → “Every course is reviewed for
  transferable capability, real application, and possible economic value.”
- “Phase 5 sells exactly one thing” → “The current first-offer hypothesis is an
  observation session plus concise audit; field evidence decides whether it holds.”
- “Findings without visuals don't sell” → visuals should make evidence easier to
  understand and act on; decision usefulness is the proof.
- Phase 4 prices and conversion rates → source-derived hypotheses/ranges pending
  qualified conversations and paid outcomes.
- Named tools → examples or current candidates selected only when a verified
  workflow need justifies them.

## Technology-Integrator Coverage

The permanent destination is not encyclopedic mastery of every tool. It is deep
technology judgment plus the ability to integrate a complete solution across:

`workflow → requirements → people/process → data/state → software/services →`
`APIs/automation/AI → security/governance → reliability/observability →`
`deployment/adoption/operation/recovery → measured economic value`

CASTLE should preserve this outcome view in a few lines and route technical depth
to owning wikis. The skill map may add or rename a small number of capability rows
for architecture, integration, security, reliability, deployment, adoption, and
operation. It should not grow into another technology library.
