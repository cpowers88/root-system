## Proposed Changes

### 1. Phase map — sequence, not a second strategy

- Label phase windows and commercial exits as working sequence under the
  current strategy.
- Replace the copied strategy-milestone table with a pointer to
  `CURRENT_STRATEGY.md`; CASTLE tracks proof movement without owning a second
  copy of prices and targets.
- Keep the October 8, 2031 deadline and economic floor because North Star owns
  them.
- Remove unsupported destination requirements such as a multi-industry client
  base and a fixed stance against human hiring.
- Replace named future stacks such as Flask/API with outcome language unless a
  live proof has selected the tool.

### 2. Phase 0 — generalize headings, preserve the live tracker proof

- `Tools to Learn` → `Tools or Methods in the Current Proof`.
- `Business Capability Unlocked` → `Capability and Value Enabled`.
- Keep SQLite/Python specifics because the approved tracker actually uses them.

### 3. Phase 1 — coursework builds capability, not mandatory services

Replace “every course maps to a service” with:

> Coursework builds transferable capability. Connect it to a real workflow,
> engineering, technology, or business application when useful; no course is
> required to become a standalone service.

- Remove time management from `Skills Needed`; A2 correctly treats capacity
  management as a constraint.
- Replace “Git/GitHub on every build” with evidence-appropriate repository
  discipline.
- Replace the stale exact email action with pointers to the pre-semester plan
  and `NOW.md` until the phase opens.

### 4. Phase 2 — transferable diagnosis under a current offer hypothesis

- Describe the observation audit as the current first-offer hypothesis, not the
  only thing a future phase can sell.
- Replace “findings without visuals don't sell” with decision-useful evidence;
  the best format may be a visual, table, calculation, demonstration, or concise
  explanation.
- Make Looker Studio an option chosen through the Recommendation Ladder, not a
  required identity.
- Replace the scheduling command with the proof condition: the next approved
  live observation is sequenced through `NOW.md` within Chris-declared capacity.

### 5. Phase 3 — preserve the outcome, loosen the stack

Use this durable pipeline:

```text
messy operational data -> validated structure -> defensible analysis
                       -> decision-ready communication
```

SQLite, pandas, Looker Studio, Markdown, and PDF remain useful current options.
They are selected by the problem, constraints, and proof vehicle rather than
installed as a permanent sequence. A visual is required only when it materially
improves the decision.

### 6. Phase 4 — keep commercial discipline without freezing the offer

- Present the offer ladder, prices, conversion percentages, and target counts as
  current source-derived assumptions to test.
- Preserve a paid-proof guardrail, but state that Chris may explicitly authorize
  a bounded $0 test after naming the evidence, displacement, and stop condition.
- Broaden the contact pool from construction-only to qualified targets reached
  through the strongest current access wedge.
- Keep rehearsal, pricing logic, and real conversations as proof; do not require
  three particular offer rungs unless live evidence still supports them.

### 7. Templates — capability and evidence before monetization

- Phase: `Tools to Learn` → `Tools, Methods, or Platforms Under Test`;
  `Business Capability Unlocked` → `Capability and Value Enabled`.
- Skill: `What Service It Unlocks` → `Outcome or Value It Enables`.
- Project: replace the required service connection with named value paths:
  academic, technical, operational, employability, commercial, or reusable
  asset.
- Evidence: replace “Self-use counts. Client use counts more. Paid use counts
  most.” Evidence strength must match the claim: independent performance may be
  strongest for mastery; real operational use for usefulness; willingness to
  pay or paid use for commercial proof.

### 8. Profit gate — broaden value without opening the floodgates

Replace the no-orphan test with:

> The idea must serve a fixed commitment, an active capability gap, a real
> workflow or project, employability, a current strategy assumption, or another
> credible North Star value path. If profit is claimed, name the economic
> mechanism and the evidence needed.

Replace rigid phase admission with a phase or activation condition. Far-horizon
ideas remain parked unless new evidence makes an earlier bounded test valuable.
Keep source, displacement, proof, exception, and Chris-approval requirements.
