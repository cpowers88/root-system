# Self-Evolving AI OS Direction Review — July 14, 2026

## Outcome

**Directionally correct; not yet expressed as one complete operating loop.**

The live architecture already contains the necessary organs: Watchtower senses,
the domain wikis research and teach, CASTLE sequences, projects and business
artifacts provide proof, reviews capture outcomes, and AI Automation Systems plus
SYSTEM_FLAGS/SYSTEM_LEARNINGS govern improvement. The problem is not a missing
major folder. The problem is that these pieces describe themselves as adjacent
systems rather than as one human-governed learning-and-value loop.

The right next move is a focused pointer-and-interface pass after Chris reviews
this report. It is not another skeleton rebuild.

## Best Description of the Intended AI OS

> **`.ROOT` is Chris's human-governed, self-improving AI operating partner. It
> continuously senses change, researches and teaches what matters next, turns
> verified knowledge into structured wikis and usable capability, directs and
> helps execute real work, learns from measured outcomes, maintains its own
> memory and operating rules through evidence and approval, and works beside
> Chris as business partner, systems engineer, builder, and strategist—always
> evolving toward the most valuable true north Chris can presently see.**

This wording keeps three truths together:

1. **Partner, not filing cabinet:** the OS helps think, teach, decide, build,
   maintain, and strategize.
2. **Self-improving, not autonomously self-rewriting:** evidence can generate a
   proposal; Chris controls consequential and governance changes.
3. **Adaptive, not aimless:** strategy may change, but change must follow proof,
   opportunity, and the North Star—not novelty.

## The Canonical Closed Loop the System Should Express

```text
SENSE -> RESEARCH -> TEACH -> STRUCTURE -> DECIDE -> BUILD -> PROVE
  ^                                                        |
  |                                                        v
EVOLVE <- REVIEW <- LEARN <- MEASURED OUTCOME <- DEPLOY / USE
```

Human governance surrounds the loop: Chris sets intent, approves consequential
action and governance change, judges quality, and may redirect the system.

## Wiki Folder-Structure Review

### What is pointed correctly

- All eight hubs separate immutable `raw/` evidence from derived `wiki/`
  knowledge and carry local operating contracts.
- The three learning hubs name proof correctly: reading/generated pages are not
  mastery; drills, explain-backs, mini-projects, and applied use are.
- Research hubs avoid speculative empty folder trees. Revenue Lab and Education
  are appropriately flat; Technology uses category folders because volume
  earned them; AI Automation Systems separates proposals from research.
- BUSINESS is the strategy/blank-master layer, while private client work and
  reusable sanitized assets have distinct homes.

### What needs correction

1. **The common interface hides different hub types.** PYTHON/PHYSICS are staged
   learning engines; TECHNOLOGY/SYSTEMS/AIAS are research/retrieval engines;
   BUSINESS/REVENUE_LAB are application/decision engines. Each needs to declare
   its hub type and one canonical current-truth pointer. Do this in existing
   operating files; do not create another folder layer.
2. **Two live `HOW_TO_USE.md` files are semantically stale.** PYTHON still says
   Stage 1 while its index and CASTLE say Stage 2. TECHNOLOGY says 68 pages and
   no landscape research while its index says 107 pages and four landscape
   pages.
3. **Large reference hubs need retrieval improvement, not relocation.** SYSTEMS
   has 100 flat wiki files. Its index sections and tags currently provide the
   retrieval layer; moving 100 files would violate the frozen-skeleton intent
   without evidence of failed retrieval. Test search/retrieval first. If it
   fails repeatedly, improve index views before moving files.
4. **Generated-curriculum volume is a maintenance risk.** PHYSICS and PYTHON
   contain hundreds of derived pages, many ahead of the live frontier. Their
   current-position/mastery gates protect Chris from treating generation as
   learning, but semantic drift in those gates becomes high impact. Those
   current-truth pointers deserve the highest wiki-maintenance priority.

## CASTLE Review

### What is pointed correctly

- CASTLE has a clear job: orient, sequence, gate, and receive proof without
  absorbing research or doing domain work.
- Skills must name phase, service, proof project, and evidence rung.
- The new opportunity queue connects evidence to a bounded test and later asset
  harvest.
- Phase 0 accurately remains open for real D2L data and the August review even
  though tracker V1 shipped.

### What needs correction

1. **CASTLE describes the path but not the entire self-evolving loop in one
   place.** `ROOT_OPERATING_MANUAL.md` comes closest. CASTLE should point to one
   canonical loop and require the same return packet from any meaningful proof:
   outcome, evidence link, capability/status movement, reusable asset candidate,
   and system-learning candidate.
2. **`source-map.md` overclaims central ownership.** Its rule says every new
   external source must receive a CASTLE row before influencing any page. In
   practice, hundreds of domain sources are governed by hub source maps/indexes.
   CASTLE should register only sources that steer roadmap/phase/skill decisions;
   domain sources should remain registered in their owning wiki.
3. **Scale claims have drifted.** `north-star-roadmap.md` says BUSINESS holds
   roughly 230 pages, while the current hub has about 54 wiki pages. Another
   CASTLE line describes roughly 230 pages across BUSINESS and SYSTEMS, while
   their current combined count is about 154. Counts should be removed where
   they do not drive decisions or derived automatically where they do.

## `...projectSuccess` / Watchtower Review

### What is pointed correctly

- The eyes-not-hands boundary is exactly right. Trend sensing should never open
  a project, change the roadmap, or add a skill by itself.
- A two-file design is appropriately small.
- Tiered sources and the CASTLE gate protect against trend chasing.

### What needs correction

1. **The radar is an inbox more than a learning sensor.** It has two entries and
   no standardized evidence-home link, falsifiable consequence, next check, or
   linked gate outcome. Add those fields to the existing board rather than
   adding files.
2. **The inbound contract is vague.** TECHNOLOGY and AIAS say they feed the
   Watchtower, but neither defines when research becomes a radar signal. The
   promotion rule should be: new external change + material consequence for an
   active assumption/stack/market + evidence link + review trigger.
3. **The return path stops too early.** A gated signal should link to the CASTLE
   verdict, later measured outcome, and any resulting North Star ratchet. That
   turns the Watchtower from “news remembered” into “prediction tested.”
4. **Placement authority omits the realm.** `START_HERE.md` and NORTH_STAR point
   to `...projectSuccess`, but `WHERE_IT_GOES.md` does not define what belongs
   there. Add a pointer-level placement rule; do not rename or move the folder
   during the live test window.

## Self-Evolution Review

The current safety model is correct: research -> evidence -> proposal -> Chris
approval -> promotion -> validation. The missing proof is **post-change outcome
learning**. Most approved AIAS proposals record that they were applied, but older
records do not consistently state the expected behavior, check date, observed
outcome, and keep/change/revert verdict.

The existing `SYSTEM_LEARNINGS.md` pilot already defines the right lifecycle. The
next improvement should use it, not create another brain, database, agent queue, or
autonomous rewrite cycle. Every approved system proposal should eventually answer:

- What behavior did we expect to improve?
- What evidence will show improvement or regression?
- When will it be checked?
- What happened?
- Keep, modify, or revert?

Until that cycle runs several times, `.ROOT` is **self-improvement-capable**, not
yet proven self-evolving.
