# Proposed replacement — EDUCATION operations

## Function

EDUCATION owns reusable learning methods, education research, and the shared
course-support conventions used by subjects without a dedicated wiki. Official
course facts and graded-work artifacts remain with their school owners.

**Shared layer:** load `00-BRAIN/WIKI_SHARED_LAYER.md` for every EDUCATION
session. This file adds education and academic-integrity rules.

## Ownership

| Truth | Owner |
|---|---|
| Mission, AI limits, academic-integrity boundary | `NORTH_STAR.md` and `AGENT.md` |
| Semester outcomes and priorities | `fall_2026_semester.md` |
| Exact-section source status | `04-SCHOOL/SYLLABUS_STATUS.md` |
| Official captures and course artifacts | the named course owner under `04-SCHOOL` or its authorized evidence home |
| Reusable learning methods and education research | this hub |
| Python learner truth | `03-WIKIS/PYTHON/wiki/current-position.md` |
| Physics learner truth | `03-WIKIS/PHYSICS/wiki/current-position.md` |
| Cross-system sequencing | CASTLE and `NOW.md` |

An official course source overrides every derivative page. Course readiness is
not meta-learning truth, and meta-learning research is not a course schedule.

## State owner

EDUCATION does not own a combined cross-course `current-position.md`.

- Learning-method research is reference material and needs no daily frontier.
- Exact course status stays with `SYLLABUS_STATUS.md` and the named course owner.
- Sequencing stays with CASTLE/NOW.

If a future EDUCATION project develops a real staged frontier, create a narrowly
named state owner for that project after approval; do not recreate a mixed
dashboard.

## Structure

```text
wiki/
  index.md       canonical catalog
  methods/       reusable learning and retrieval methods
  references/    education research and systems
  protocols/     shared course-support methods that are not course truth
  log.md         operational deltas and source coverage
```

Existing course folders remain in place until an approved migration names their
destination. Do not move or duplicate TCOM, ECON, or ENGR material as part of
adopting this contract.

## Research and method intake

1. Name the learning or education question and intended user.
2. Verify source scope, authority, date, and limitations.
3. Process large sources under the shared chunking rule.
4. Separate evidence, source claim, inference, practical method, and unknown.
5. Update an existing method/reference owner before creating a page.
6. Translate a method into observable use: trigger, action, evidence, and
   disconfirmation.

**Complete when:** the result is reusable beyond one assignment or course date,
traceable to evidence, and routed to one owner.

## Course-support query

1. Open the exact course owner and `SYLLABUS_STATUS.md` when policy or source
   certainty matters.
2. Verify whether the work is graded and what AI assistance the course permits.
3. Load only the relevant reusable EDUCATION method.
4. End with independent learner performance or a precise verification need;
   page creation is not proof.

**Complete when:** the answer respects the exact course policy and produces no
duplicate course authority.

## Academic boundary

AI may explain concepts, create fresh private practice, review Chris's reasoning,
and help plan independent work. When graded status or policy is unclear, stop
and ask. Do not draft, solve, rewrite, or debug prohibited submitted work.

## Close

Follow the shared close. Additionally record whether the result changed:

- a reusable learning method;
- a course owner's verified status;
- learner evidence; or
- nothing beyond the session.

The hub is healthy when a fresh session can find the relevant method without
mistaking EDUCATION for the owner of course schedules, learner frontiers, or
graded artifacts.
