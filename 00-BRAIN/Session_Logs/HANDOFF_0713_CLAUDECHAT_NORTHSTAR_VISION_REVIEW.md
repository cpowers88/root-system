## Claude Code's Observations (one input, not the ruling)

   invariant identity layer from a rotating strategy layer means Chris's
   specific business model (audit consulting, real estate/construction
   wedges) can fail, pivot, or get replaced entirely without requiring a
   rewrite of what `.ROOT` fundamentally is. That's real architectural
   value, not just reframing for its own sake.

   found it" against what Chris actually wrote: humanity without labels,
   the specific memory of meeting people from other countries, wanting to
   bring *those nations* money too, not just extract value from them. The
   generic version could have been written by any founder. The specific
   version is the one likely to still mean something years from now. If
   you carry a Vision layer forward, consider whether it should keep
   Chris's original specificity rather than the smoothed paraphrase.
