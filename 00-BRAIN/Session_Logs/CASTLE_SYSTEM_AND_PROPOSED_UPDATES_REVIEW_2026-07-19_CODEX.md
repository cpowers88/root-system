# CASTLE System and Proposed Updates Review — July 19, 2026

## Executive Verdict

**Verdict: KEEP the CASTLE architecture; execute a bounded semantic and cockpit
remediation; do not open another redesign program.**

CASTLE has the right structural job and mostly the right organs. It clearly owns
sequencing and proof status without absorbing domain work. Its phase map, skill
map, opportunity gate, proof-project pattern, and relationship to the North Star
are unusually strong. The July 19 decision to keep one modular vault and reject
new planes-as-structure, metadata expansion, repository extraction, vector
retrieval, and broad module migration was correct.

The weak point is the live command surface. `NOW.md` is no longer a one-screen,
one-priority cockpit; several volatile facts are copied across CASTLE pages; one
previously closed source-ownership/count drift class has recurred; and the newly
installed Information Flow Contract is logically useful but not yet operationally
proven. These are interface and semantic-maintenance failures, not evidence that
the physical architecture is wrong.

The best next move is a two-slice repair:

1. **Truth pass:** reconcile the few demonstrable semantic conflicts and close the
   July 19 proposal records cleanly.
2. **Cockpit pass:** reduce `NOW.md` to an executable front door that points to
   owning plans instead of copying them.

Then run the already planned contractor trace during the Bootcamp and stop. Any
larger change must earn itself from that real-use evidence.

changed.

## Material Findings

### F1 — HIGH: The cockpit contradicts its own operating promise

**Failure condition.** START_HERE describes `NOW.md` as “one screen, one priority.”
The live file is 182 lines / 15,460 bytes with 16 open checkboxes, six completed
checkboxes, three long start items, a five-row state table, a full weekly checklist,
an eight-day Bootcamp schedule, upcoming triggers, boundaries, and owner pointers.

**Impact.** The page asks Chris to visually parse the project plan before starting.
That works against the exact aptitude constraint the system was designed to solve:
provide a skeleton and one next action, not a wide menu. It also increases copied
truth and refresh burden.

**Smallest credible correction.** Keep `NOW.md` as a pointer cockpit with:

- one **Do now** action;
- one protected school floor;
- at most three **After that / waiting** items;
- hard dates and approval gates;
- links to the Bootcamp plan, current-position, and opportunity queue.

Move no evidence and create no new dashboard. The owning plan already exists.

**Acceptance.** A cold open identifies the next physical action in under 30
seconds; the page is under roughly 80 lines; no full multi-day plan is copied.

### F2 — HIGH if accepted: Closed source/count drift has recurred

This is the same defect class as closed flag #74, so `SYSTEM_FLAGS.md` rules require
it to return as HIGH if Chris accepts the finding.

Evidence:

- `CASTLE\OPERATIONS.md` says **every source** lands in CASTLE `source-map`.
- `source-map.md`, the profit gate, and hub ownership rules say only
  **roadmap-relevant** sources register in CASTLE; domains own their evidence.
- `source-map.md` says SYSTEMS has 98 pages; the live SYSTEMS wiki currently has
  133 Markdown files.
- `current-position.md` says the business-model wiki has 51 pages; the live
  `ai-integration-company` subtree currently has 61 Markdown files.

**Impact.** One AI may rebuild a central source ledger while another follows the
distributed hub contract. Copied counts inevitably go stale and falsely imply
that the map, not the owner index, is canonical.

**Smallest credible correction.** Change the OPERATIONS rule and source-map
registration footer to “roadmap-shaping/relevant sources only.” Remove live scale
counts from CASTLE and point to the owning index. Do not replace them with fresh
numbers.

**Acceptance.** One repository search finds no live CASTLE rule requiring all hub
sources to register centrally and no volatile hub page-count claim outside log
history.

### F3 — HIGH: CASTLE's skill truth trails completed evidence

`current-position.md` and `NOW.md` say the first private Looker/Data Studio
dashboard rep was completed and verified July 16. `skill-map.md` still marks
“Data visualization / Looker Studio” as `not-started`.

**Impact.** CASTLE owns skill/proof status. A stale status here can resequence work
incorrectly and makes the command center disagree with its own current-position
page.

**Correction.** Move the skill to `building`, link the July 16 evidence location,
and state the next rung: a dashboard that supports a real operational decision
with a defensible calculation. Do not call one private guided dashboard proven.

### F4 — MEDIUM-HIGH: CASTLE's local authority contract contradicts its duties

`OPERATIONS.md` says approval is required for **ANY file outside CASTLE**. The same
file requires CASTLE sessions to maintain root `NOW.md`, append the daily record
under `Session_Logs`, and sometimes write a handoff. Those duties are already
Chris-approved standing operations, but the exception is not stated.

The local Session Boot Order also restates a shortened chain that omits
`CHRIS_CORE.md` and `SYSTEM_FLAGS.md`, even though the universal chain requires
them. The local CLAUDE pointer similarly jumps from surface profile to OPERATIONS.

**Impact.** A careful AI must either violate the literal autonomy paragraph or
fail the close contract; another AI may treat the shortened local list as complete
and miss required context.

**Correction.** Make the local boot instruction say “complete the universal
orientation chain in AGENT.md, then load index → last three log entries → roadmap.”
State the pre-approved outside-CASTLE exceptions explicitly: Castle-owned `NOW.md`
and the required DAILY/handoff append. Everything else remains approval-gated.

### F5 — MEDIUM-HIGH: The Information Flow Contract is useful but unproven

The July 19 challenge chain correctly rejected the broad eight-gate program. Chris
then explicitly authorized a narrower interface install: one translation contract,
no physical change, no new metadata, and no structural planes. That decision is
coherent and should stand.

Two debts remain:

1. The synthesis required two real traces before promotion, but the contract was
   installed before the contractor trace. Chris had authority to override the
   sequence; the result is still **accepted governance awaiting operating proof**.
2. The contract says every meaningful item is “always in exactly one” of eight
   states. Real work can simultaneously have trusted source evidence, structured
   state, an active build, and an unresolved measured outcome. Without a selection
   rule, different operators may assign different states.

**Correction after the Bootcamp trace, not before.** Define the assigned state as
the **next unresolved transformation** for the specific trace, or remove “exactly
one.” Keep the seven-line trace as the operating interface and the eight-state table
as translation/reference. Do not add `flow_stage` metadata.

**Acceptance.** Chris and a fresh AI independently identify the same owner, next
unresolved state, next action, proof, and return path for the contractor case and
one system-change case.

### F6 — MEDIUM: The proposal chain remains active after the decision

The redesign review, implementation procedure, independent review, and synthesis
remain live Session_Log records with `timeline: now` or `timeline: next` and
`status: active` / `draft`. `NOW.md` says the broad program was rejected/deferred
and the narrow interface remodel was executed.

**Impact.** Property queries can surface superseded proposals as current work. A
future operator may resume Gate 1 from the draft procedure even though Chris chose
a different path.

**Correction.** Preserve all four records, but add explicit decision disposition:

- original redesign: `superseded-by-decision`;
- eight-gate procedure: `rejected-as-written` or `parked-reference`;
- independent review: `accepted-as-challenge-input`;
- synthesis: `modified-by-Chris-decision`;
- each points to the live Information Flow Contract and the exact remaining trace.

Use only status values approved for Session Logs; if new values are undesirable,
use `timeline: reference` plus a visible Decision block. Archive only with Chris's
separate approval.

### F7 — MEDIUM: CASTLE templates do not consistently return the canonical packet

The Capability Contract defines five fields for every meaningful proof: outcome,
evidence link, capability/status movement, reusable-asset candidate, and
system-learning candidate. CASTLE's evidence and project templates capture artifact,
demonstration, witness/use, gaps, and status log, but omit the two candidate fields
and do not explicitly name the return owner.

**Impact.** The doctrine is stronger than the work surface. Asset harvest and
system learning remain dependent on memory during close.

**Correction.** Add a short “Return Packet” close block to `evidence-template.md`
and `project-template.md`; point to the Capability Contract rather than redefining
the fields. Do not duplicate the full contract in every template.

### F8 — MEDIUM: Some roadmap claims outrun evidence

Two examples:

- Phase 1 says every ISYE course maps to a service. Coursework can create
  transferable capability, but not every course automatically becomes sellable.
- Phase 4 presents 50–70% / 60% conversion figures and $1,500–$2,500 beginner
  pricing as working offer truth. `CURRENT_STRATEGY.md` explicitly treats
  willingness to pay, audit conversion, and retainer economics as assumptions to
  test.

**Impact.** Research-derived numbers can silently harden into strategy and cause
premature pricing or milestone confidence.

**Correction.** Say each course is reviewed for transferable client capability.
Label offer conversion and pricing as source-derived hypotheses/ranges until real
conversations and paid work validate them.

### F9 — MEDIUM: The “Every Page” index is incomplete

`wiki\index.md` does not name `CASTLE\CODEX.md` or any of the seven templates.
Its header says it was updated July 16 while later CASTLE state changed.

**Impact.** Cold discovery depends on filesystem search instead of the advertised
complete index.

**Correction.** Either list the missing root profile and template set, or rename
the index promise to “operational pages” and define exclusions. Complete indexing
is preferable because the set is small.

### F10 — LOW-MEDIUM: The append-only log is becoming a retrieval surface

`wiki\log.md` is 85,344 bytes / 1,163 lines. New entries append at the bottom, but
opening the page starts on older July 17 entries; July 18 entries are at the tail.
The boot rule “last three entries” is clear to a tool, less convenient to a human.

**Correction.** For now, add one sentence at the top: “Newest entries are at the
bottom.” At the first monthly boundary, decide whether to keep one file or roll
history into monthly archived logs while preserving `log.md` as the current entry
surface. Do not restructure mid-Bootcamp.
