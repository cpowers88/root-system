# Evening Reading Instructions

At 5:00 p.m. local time, read `MORNING_BRIEF.md`, `NOW.md`, today's DAILY, both active bootcamp evidence files, and the relevant owner reading queue, then replace root `EVENING_READING.md` with one available 15–25 minute slice that prepares the next proof; prefer an owned book or official course reading, then the most immediately useful technology, systems, programming, business, or engineering prerequisite.

Write exactly three one-sentence lines of no more than 35 words each: **READ** gives the source plus exact chapter/section/pages or file; **WHY** connects it to tomorrow's action or current capability gap; **STOP** gives the precise endpoint. Never assign a broad chapter, treat reading as mastery, or copy owner context into the brief.

The next measured session records whether the assignment was completed, useful,
well-timed, and correctly scoped; that result returns to the evidence lane it served
and is cross-reviewed Sunday.
