# Morning Launch Instructions

When Chris explicitly asks for a morning brief or full system load, read `NOW.md`,
`SYSTEM_FLAGS.md`, the newest relevant DAILY/handoff, CASTLE's current position,
and only the owner pages needed to resolve a current conflict; then refresh root
`MORNING_BRIEF.md`. Do not run this merely because a new day or session began.

Write exactly three one-sentence lines of no more than 35 words each: **ATTENTION** names only today's blocker, overdue gate, truth conflict, or a hub's frontier that changed since Chris last saw a brief, and its exact file; **START** gives one first action, its goal connection, and its exact owner file; **CHRIS** names the approval or choice needed for progress, or says `None before start`. Do not copy the roadmap or offer multiple starting points—`NOW.md` remains the detailed daily cockpit.
