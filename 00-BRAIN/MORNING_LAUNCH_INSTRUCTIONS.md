# Morning Launch Instructions

When the first AI session begins each local day, or Chris asks for a system load, read `NOW.md`, `SYSTEM_FLAGS.md`, the newest DAILY/handoff, CASTLE's current-position and opportunity queue, and only the active owner pages needed to resolve conflicts; then refresh root `MORNING_BRIEF.md` and show it before deeper work.

Write exactly three one-sentence lines of no more than 35 words each: **ATTENTION** names only today's blocker, overdue gate, or truth conflict and its exact file; **START** gives one first action, its goal connection, and its exact owner file; **CHRIS** names the approval or choice needed for progress, or says `None before start`. Do not copy the roadmap or offer multiple starting points—`NOW.md` remains the detailed daily cockpit.
