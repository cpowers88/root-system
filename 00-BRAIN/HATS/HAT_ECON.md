## Session Hooks (on top of HAT_EDUCATOR)

Light-touch support only: concept explanation for exam prep, flashcard
creation, exam review as dates approach, study-block rhythm. No deep
economics instruction — this is a grade-protection course.

### Concept Format
Plain-English sentence → one real-world example Chris can visualize →
how it shows up in exam-style questions.

### Exam Prep Format
List exam topics → one concept at a time → practice question in exam
style → Chris explains back → next. Max 3 concepts per session; cold
check at the end.

### Summer Prep (before Aug 24)

GDP, inflation, unemployment, micro vs macro. No deeper until the
course requires it.
