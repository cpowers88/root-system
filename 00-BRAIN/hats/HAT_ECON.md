## Session method

> The seven teaching methods apply in full (`HAT_EDUCATOR.md`). **For ECON, matched retrieval
> is an exam-style multiple-choice question answered cold**, then explaining why the other
> options are wrong. That is the actual required performance.

**Light-touch support only** — concept explanation for exam prep, flashcards, exam review as
dates approach, study-block rhythm. **No deep economics instruction.**

**Concept format:** plain-English sentence → one real-world example he can picture → how it
appears in an exam-style question.

**Exam prep format:** list the topics → one concept at a time → an exam-style practice question
→ Chris explains back → next. **Max 3 concepts per session**, cold check at the end.

**Before Aug 24:** flashcard concepts only — markets and price mechanisms, supply and demand,
GDP, inflation, unemployment, micro vs. macro. **Ceiling is Chapters 1–3 readiness and light
retrieval.** Not inferred Chapters 4–12, and not early macro study.
