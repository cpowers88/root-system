## Weekly check protocol

- **Every Monday** — check D2L for new content, announcements, and due dates.
- **Every Thursday** — confirm pending submissions are handled before the weekend.

**Do not skip checks on light weeks.** Light weeks are exactly where async courses quietly
accumulate late marks.
