  One deliberate refinement: internal system friction should not be copied into the commercial opportunity queue. It
  belongs in SYSTEM_FLAGS.md. The opportunity queue can link to a flag when system friction has commercial or strategic
  impact, but it should not duplicate its lifecycle.

  ## Opportunity lifecycle

  The central queue should use the stages you approved:

  captured → triaged → researching → worth testing → active build/offer → tested → won / lost / parked → asset harvested

  Types:

  - Client lead
  - Observed waste
  - Technology opportunity
  - Learning-to-application opportunity

  A real client lead bypasses normal review: capture the evidence, mark it urgent, and move directly to active triage.
  Money and real-world feedback win.

  ## Priority policy

  Replace the misleading idea that school, tech, business, and system work are four competing tracks.

  - School obligations are non-negotiable calendar constraints and capability infrastructure.
  - Technology capability is the permanent compounding engine.
  - Business opportunity and client value are the economic North Star.
  - System evolution is supporting infrastructure, always allowed when it removes demonstrated friction.

  During semesters, protect a 5–10 hour weekly floor for technology/business whenever school deadlines allow. NOW.md
  should make that visible without letting a daily dashboard become a second strategy document.

  ## AI model policy

  Keep specialist recommendations, remove artificial walls.

  Any AI may help with any in-scope task. For each meaningful task, the system identifies:

  - recommended lead;
  - required challenger or validator when the work is consequential;
  - evidence needed before calling it complete.

  The default strengths remain useful:

  - Claude Chat: teaching, strategy, synthesis, ongoing conversation
  - Codex: audits, exact briefs, validation design, structural diagnosis
  - Claude Code: edits, tools, scripts, implementation, tests
  - ATLAS: independent challenge, alternative framing, conceptual pressure-test

  Every model should surface relevant alternatives or risks. It should not agree automatically, nor oppose
  automatically.

  ## Skills architecture

  Your concern is correct: AGENT.md must stay thin.

  - AGENT.md becomes the stable constitution, routing rule, safety boundary, and extension trigger table.
  - HAT files remain cross-model working modes.
  - Skills hold detailed repeatable procedures and load only when needed.
  - Wiki CLAUDE.md files hold domain-specific operating rules.
  - AI Automation proposals are the only path for changing reusable system behavior.

  The system should never cut important procedure text merely to keep AGENT.md short. It should move the procedure into
  a skill and retain a one-line trigger/pointer in AGENT.md.
